Inside Sunac Mall （融创茂） in Huangdao, the Sunac Ali Adventure Park （融创阿狸冒险乐园） is a fully indoor kids’ park built around Ali, the little red-fox cartoon character — which makes this Halloween outing completely weather-proof. The special parent-child ticket covers one adult (18+) and one child (under 18) for a full day: all five themed zones are unlimited-entry, unlimited-play for the day — Harvest Barn (interactive pool and sand pit), Maze Adventure (soft-play maze and slides), Star-Pink Ocean (a giant ocean-ball pit), Dream Workshop (soft building blocks) and Soft Planet (inflatable castle and trampolines). Individual attractions may pause for maintenance on the day; what’s actually open on site rules. The Halloween ticket runs the park’s whole spooky season — 19 days, Oct 22 to Nov 9 — and every day of the week counts, school nights included, which is rare for a holiday special.

It is a one-ticket park with a forgiving twist: once you’re in, you can step out and re-enter the same day with the staff stamp, so a lunch break at the mall doesn’t end the fun. Two rides sit outside the ticket and charge separately — the mini driving school (¥20 per 10 minutes) and the karting ride (¥29.9 per 3 laps) — and food, shopping and other mall extras are pay-as-you-go.

Tickets are for the named holder only; the limit is 1 ticket per order and 3 per phone number per day. Unused vouchers can be cancelled free of charge before Nov 13, 2026 (no refund after that, and partly used orders are non-refundable).
