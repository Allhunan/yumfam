“Go to the mountains — nature has the answers” is this place’s motto, and it delivers: 21 themed treehouses and villas — Lego-block, North American, steam-train, lighthouse, watchtower, flying-saucer and even a 123㎡ “airplane” masterwork — scattered through the pine forests of Qiyun Mountain, one of China’s four great Taoist mountains. Each sits apart with its own patch of mountain and cloud, many with viewing decks where you can watch the morning mist from bed. Around 3 hours by car from Shanghai, 2 hours from Hangzhou, with a shuttle from Huangshan North station.

Every package bundles the full scenic ticket pack: Qiyun Mountain entry, round-trip cableway, Hengjiang bamboo raft, jungle-crossing course, Xiaoyao Street shows and the 150-acre Ziyoujia Camp (petting zoo, rope courses and more), plus breakfasts at the Meihualu restaurant and a shuttle service. 🎁 November bonus: from Nov 1–30, 2026, visitors under 18 get free Qiyun Mountain entry (with ID at the ticket window).

⚠️ Booking & extras: Reserve by 20:00 on the day of use; refundable before you reserve. One child under 1.2 m stays free on most packages. For the Huangshan North station shuttle, book by 20:00 the day before.
