In Jimei’s Xinglin district, Xiamen Lingling International Circus City runs an autumn weekend format built for families: the Animal Kingdom opens for afternoon entry — giant pandas, giraffes you can get close to, ring-tailed lemurs, alpacas, white tigers and brown bears — and at 18:30 the big top lights up for “Forest Secret Realm,” a gold-medal circus production staged in a Guinness-record circus theatre seating 10,000. It was created by a team whose credits include Chimelong’s circus choreography and Cirque du Soleil technical consulting, on a rare water-and-land dual stage mixing fountain screens with fire acts; the headline Kenyan “Wheel of Death” has been upgraded to six wheels at 17 metres high. Buy the circus ticket and the same day’s Animal Kingdom entry is included free — one voucher, one afternoon-to-evening outing, no ticket exchange: buy by 15:00 the same day (19:00 for the circus tier) and scan in with your ID and QR code.

⚠️ The timing rules are strict — plan around them:

  • Animal Kingdom entry is 15:00–16:30 only, and the animals “clock off” at 17:00 — arrive at 15:00 sharp to see the pandas awake-ish and catch everything.
  • Tickets are single-entry: once you leave, you can’t re-enter.
  • Each ticket covers one adult or child of 1.0m+, and each ticket-holder may bring one child under 1.0m free.
  • All tickets in one order must enter together (no partial refunds, no splitting) — buy separately if your group arrives separately. Max 5 tickets per order, ID and phone across the whole season.
  • Not included: animal feeding, the Kids’ Fun World, the Ice & Snow Kingdom and parking.
