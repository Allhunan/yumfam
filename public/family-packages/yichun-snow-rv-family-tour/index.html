Deal price: 6-day / 5-night snow RV tour, child — from ¥4,980 (≈$692; adult from ¥5,980, early-bird ¥1,000 off) · Departures Jan 19 – Feb 21, 2027 · One family per RV

This is winter in Heilongjiang done properly: six days from Harbin into the snow country around Yichun, travelling in a heated snow-themed RV — one family to a vehicle — through the Lesser Khingan forest. The trip packs in a 1-on-1 ski lesson, ice fishing and a winter-catch demonstration, reindeer interaction and feeding, an Oroqen tribe visit, a snowfield hotpot glamping lunch, and 10+ snow activities, with a guide, a safety host and a photographer along the way. Nights are in 5-diamond Harbin hotels and Yichun resorts, not in the RV.

The route (6 days / 5 nights)

  • Day 1 — Arrive in Harbin, overnight in a 5-diamond hotel (Shibo Pavilion / Crown Plaza or similar)
  • Day 2 — RV to Tieli: Xiaoyao Baxian Lake ice carnival, overnight at the lakeside hotel
  • Day 3–4 — Yichun: Jiufeng Mountain, Oroqen folk village, Riyuexia National Forest Park, ski coaching, snow activities; overnight at Yuenan Mountain Resort / Kailiya Sen or similar
  • Day 5 — Return toward Harbin, overnight in a 5-diamond hotel
  • Day 6 — Free morning in Harbin, then make your own way home

  • Other departures mostly child ¥5,680 (≈$789) / adult ¥6,680 (≈$928); peak Feb 8–13 departure child ¥5,980 / adult ¥6,980 (4 spots left at last check)

Good to know

  • 1 adult + 1 child minimum per booking; children must take a bed. A 1-adult-1-child booking pays a ¥1,000 room supplement plus a ¥1,000-per-person RV supplement; 2 adults + 1 or 2 children pay no supplement
  • One RV holds 4–5 people; one family minimum to run, 5 RVs maximum — confirm the group with customer service before buying your transport to Harbin
  • Transport to and from Harbin is NOT included; the tour starts and ends in Harbin
  • Not suitable for pregnant travellers; tell the operator in advance if anyone is 65+
  • Cancellation: free until 15 days before departure, 50% loss within 8 days, 80% within 2 days, 90% within 1 day, 100% after
