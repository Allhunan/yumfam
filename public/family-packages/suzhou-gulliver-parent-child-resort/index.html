Inside the Jiangnan Botanical Garden on the shores of Taihu Lake (Wuzhong District, Suzhou — under an hour from the city centre), the Suzhou Gulliver Parent-Child Resort Hotel turns Gulliver’s Travels into a place to sleep. Instead of merely tolerating children, the resort is built from a child’s eye level: a “Lilliput” of oversized furniture seen as the tiny residents would see it, proportion-play surprises hidden in the corridors, and garden landscaping that shrinks adults to finger size. Some rooms even have star-gazing skylights. Every tier includes breakfast for two, 2 tickets to the DuoDuo petting zoo (alpacas, cats, peacocks — close enough to stroke, feed and photograph), the indoor kids’ park, and free roaming of the botanical garden itself, with its themed planting zones and small zoo.

Families with dogs aren’t left out either: both themed tiers include pet-friendly room options, so the whole household can come. The two-night tiers flex as 1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night, handy for two-family trips.

⚠️ Fine print that matters: weekends and New Year’s Day genuinely carry no surcharge, but Spring Festival (Feb 5–10, 2027) adds ¥300 per room-night (¥150 on the Botanical Garden Hotel tier) — book those dates with eyes open. Reserve by 20:00 the day before; check-in 14:00, out 12:00. All guests check in at the Jiangnan Botanical Garden Hotel — navigate to the garden’s South Gate (999 Linhu Road).
