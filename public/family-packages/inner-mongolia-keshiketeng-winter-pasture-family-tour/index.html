A different route from the Chongli/Ulan Butong snow tour also on this site: this one stays out on the Keshiketeng grasslands, built around the Mongolian winter pasture. Six days from Beijing to Chengde and deep into Inner Mongolia — winter fishing on Dalinor Lake, rafting down a rime-lined river, a night in a snow village, and a hard-core off-road crossing of the Greater Khingan snow forest in a Toyota Prado. The pace is slow by design, with a family tour leader, a photographer and a 1-to-5 ski coach travelling with the group.

Highlights, as listed on the live product page:

  • Mongolian winter pasture stay — a night with a herding family: a nomad feast, horse-head fiddle and throat-singing performance, and the local customs of a working winter pasture.
  • Dalinor Lake winter fishing — the thousand-year-old winter-net tradition: the ceremony, breaking the ice, setting the net and watching the catch come up through the ice (moved to a nearby lake if Dalinor is closed for conservation).
  • Rime rafting — drifting down a river lined with rime ice, a 40-minute float through the snow forest.
  • Off-road snow-forest crossing — Toyota Prado wild-luxury safari through the Greater Khingan primeval snow forest, meeting the “children of the forest” and riding out across the snowfields for photos.
  • E’ertengnai snow village — horse-drawn sleighs across open country, unlimited snow-tube runs, a fire-offering ceremony and a fireworks night.
  • Skiing — a session at a national winter-games ski resort with a 1-to-5 coach, plus dark-sky stargazing on a clear night.
