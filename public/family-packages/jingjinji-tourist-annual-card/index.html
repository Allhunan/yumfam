Jing-Jin-Ji Attraction Annual Pass from ¥42.5 (≈$6) — 150+ Beijing, Tianjin & Hebei Sights

Deal price: standard pass from ¥42.5 (≈$6) · Premium ¥95 (≈$13) · Supreme ¥189 (≈$27) · Buy-and-use e-card, refundable anytime before activation

The 2027 Jing-Jin-Ji tourist annual pass （京津冀名胜文化休闲旅游年卡） is one electronic card for a year of days out across Beijing, Tianjin and Hebei, in three tiers. The Standard （畅游） pass covers 150+ attractions, including the CCTV Tower (gate ¥90 — twice the card’s price on its own), Juyongguan Great Wall (¥40), Lianhuashan Ski Resort (¥128), the Longmai hot-spring bamboo grove (¥150), Phoenix Ridge (¥25, three free entries), Mangshan Forest Park (¥30, unlimited entries), Jingdong Grand Karst Cave (¥80) and 50+ new additions this year, from a 798 VR cinema (one of 13 films, ¥158) to Sony ExploraScience, Yeyahu Lake and Miaofeng Mountain (unlimited). The Premium （臻选） pass widens that to 160+, adding the likes of Fuguo Underwater World (¥130), Mutianyu Great Wall, the Beijing Auto Museum and a Liangma River day cruise. The Supreme （至尊） pass covers 170+ — everything in the lower tiers plus 20+ exclusive attractions, headlined by Beijing Aquarium including Beijing Zoo (¥175), Tianjin Wildlife Park (¥160), the Ruilin Bay resort hot spring (¥268), Beijing Wildlife Park (¥150, one entry at a discounted ¥99), Jinshanling and Huangyaguan Great Wall, the Shougang SoReal sci-fi park (¥139) and Dule Temple. Across all tiers the combined gate value tops ¥20,000.

⚠️ Read the mechanics before you buy: the pass covers first-gate admission only — in-park rides and extras are on you. Each attraction sets its own reception window, visit limits and blackout dates (some are seasonal, some close on national holidays, and several require booking a day ahead), and the attraction list itself can change — the official WeChat account “京津冀旅游年票” (bjnianpiao) is the live directory, so check your target sight there before every trip. One card is for one named person only (non-transferable, no replacement if “lost”), one entry per attraction per day, and there’s no insurance included.

Good to know

  • Buy now, activate later: cards can be activated any day from Sep 11, 2026 to Dec 31, 2027, and per the product instructions the card becomes usable 24 hours after activation — activate the day before your first outing, not at the gate.
  • At the attraction, show the activated card on your phone and swipe in; some sights also ask for the holder’s own ID. If a valid card fails to scan at a covered attraction, contact the issuer’s office on the spot — leaving and buying your own ticket isn’t reimbursed afterwards.
  • Unactivated, unused cards are refundable anytime.
