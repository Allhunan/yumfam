⚠️ Important seasonal note: the indoor water park is closed for maintenance from November 2, 2026 to April 2, 2027, and tickets are replaced by Xihe Hot Spring during that period (open November 5, 2026 – March 28, 2027). If you are buying right now, you are buying a hot-spring ticket that converts back to the water park when it reopens in April 2027.

When open, the water park is a 5,600㎡ indoor heated space under a transparent dome — a steady 30°C all year — with 30+ water attractions including a sports pool for water basketball and climbing challenges, so it works in any weather. The park sits beside the 4A Nanbei Lake scenic area in Haiyan County, about an hour’s drive from both Shanghai and Hangzhou. Fantasy Island, the dry-play zone, runs 10:00–20:00 year-round.

Ticket options cover every group shape: a single water park ticket is ¥99 (≈$14, was ¥288); two people ¥188 (≈$26); one adult + one child (1–1.5 m) ¥158 (≈$22); two adults + one child ¥258 (≈$36). Fantasy Island tickets are ¥99 for a child (0.8–1.5 m) and ¥138 (≈$19) for one adult + one child, and there are 1-adult-1-child monthly (¥499, ≈$69) and season cards (¥699, ≈$97) valid 31 and 95 days respectively from activation.

Practical notes: water park hours when open are Sunday–Thursday 10:00–17:00 and Friday, Saturday and holidays 10:00–18:00 (indoor section only). Book by 23:58 the day before; entry is by ID card, no ticket exchange needed. Unused tickets can be refunded anytime; split separate orders if your group wants to enter separately. Address: Senbo Resort Park, 1677 Ganpu Avenue, Ganpu Town, Haiyan County, Jiaxing, Zhejiang.
