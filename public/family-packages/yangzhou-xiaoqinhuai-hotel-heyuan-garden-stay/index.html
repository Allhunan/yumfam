Deal price: deluxe room + breakfast for 2 — ¥458 (≈$65, was ¥655) · 2 nights deluxe room + breakfast + 2 Heyuan Garden tickets with guided tour + weekend scenic-boat ride — ¥898 (≈$126, was ¥1,300) · Valid Oct 15, 2026 – May 31, 2027 · Book by 21:00 on the day of arrival

Yangzhou rewards slow travel, and this hotel is built for exactly that. The Yihe Yinyi Renfengli Hotel （颐和隐逸仁丰里酒店 — listed on the platform as the Xiaoqinhuai Hotel) is a brand-new, 48-room hideaway at 89 Renfengli, Wenchang Middle Road, deep in the old city’s fishbone lanes of blue brick and grey tile. It occupies Xinyuan, the former private garden of late-Qing salt merchant Zhou Yifu — a heritage building that later served as a street office — reworked with carved-red-lacquer and mother-of-pearl inlay details, courtyard calm, and none of the big-hotel bustle. Dongguan Street’s food stalls and Pishi Street’s indie shops are walkable from the door, and Geyuan Garden is nearby.

Breakfast deserves its own line: the hotel’s Delin Xiaoguan tea house descends from the Pande Lin tea house, serving Yangzhou morning tea since 1953 — three generations of hand-made, alkaline-fermented dough work, a technique inscribed as intangible cultural heritage in 2025. In Yangzhou, morning tea is the main event, and here it comes with the room.

⚠️ The surcharge calendar is the thing to plan around. Through Mar 14, 2027 almost everything is base price — only Jan 1–2 and Feb 6–10 add ¥300/night. From Mar 15 to May 31, 2027 (peak “misty March” season), every night adds ¥200 (Sun–Fri) or ¥300 (Sat), with Qingming (Apr 2–4) and Labor Day (Apr 30–May 4) at +¥600/night. Book by 21:00 on arrival day; unbooked vouchers refund anytime, confirmed bookings generally don’t. One structural catch on the ¥898 bundle: it can be converted to 2 rooms × 1 night, but then it covers room + breakfast only — the Heyuan tickets, tour and boat ride drop out.
