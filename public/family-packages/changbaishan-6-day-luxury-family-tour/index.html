This is the new luxury Changbaishan tour for the 2026–27 winter season, and it went on sale this morning (November 18). The pitch is “here, not Switzerland”: a night in a Swiss-style resort village at the foot of Changbaishan, family skiing at the quieter Huamei resort, two nights at a hot-spring hotel inside the Heaven Lake scenic area, and a finish in Yanji — the Korean-border city — by vintage tram. Five nights, all in international-brand hotels, with a family tour leader, a study guide and a photographer travelling with the group.

The six days run like this:

  • Day 1 — Arrive in Changchun. Airport pickup at Changchun Longjia Airport, check in at the Hyatt Regency Changchun, opening ceremony in the evening. Free time to explore the “This Has Mountains” mall, the Puppet Emperor’s Palace or the old tram line.
  • Day 2 — Changchun → Huamei resort. A 5-hour ride in a 2+1 “land first-class” coach to the Swiss-style resort village. Afternoon snowmobiling and horse-drawn sleighs, then the resort’s own snow activities — snow go-karts, ice bikes, ice sleds, a deer park — and an evening float parade and molten-iron fireworks.
  • Day 3 — Skiing and the snow-house hotpot. Family skiing at Huamei resort (gear included, a 5-minute walk from the rooms), then a Hobbit snow-house hotpot lunch, a snow hike tracking animal prints with a Changbaishan research-institute expert, and a northeastern iron-pot stew dinner. Two nights at the Crowne Plaza Hot Spring Hotel follow.
  • Day 4 — Heaven Lake, all day. A full day on Changbaishan’s north slope: Heaven Lake, Green Deep Pool, the Changbai Waterfall and the Underground Forest, plus feeding the reindeer. The hotel sits inside the scenic area, so you start after breakfast while day-trippers are still queueing at the gate.
  • Day 5 — Rime rafting, ice fishing, Yanji. Morning rafting down the rime-lined river, then ice fishing and winter-net fishing on the frozen lake, and on to Yanji for a Korean dinner and the Yanbian University photo wall.
  • Day 6 — Yanji by tram. A ride on Yanji’s vintage tram (three runs a day) with a folk-music performance, the Korean Folk Village and the Yanbian Museum, then airport/station drop-off.

Good to know

  • This is a destination join-in tour departing from Changchun (airport pickup included) and ending in Yanji — your transport to Changchun and home from Yanji is not included.
  • After ordering, contact customer service to confirm your departure before buying any flights or trains.
  • Expect real cold (around −20°C and below): pack down jackets, thermal trousers, non-slip warm boots, lip balm and sunglasses.
  • Heaven Lake sits at altitude and its opening depends on the day’s weather — if the scenic area closes, the ticket is refunded.
  • The rime on the river and the snow-house hotpot both depend on the weather; the operator swaps in an equivalent indoor option if conditions don’t allow them.
