A stay inside a pottery town: Yao Lake Town (窑湖小镇) is a lakeside old-street-and-kiln resort in Yixing, the Jiangsu city famous for purple-clay (zisha) teapots. The town is built around that heritage — a recreated dragon kiln, old-street lanes, a bird show in a valley theatre, and the “Cloud Train,” a hillside mini-train looping through bamboo, tea fields and the lake (guests call the night run, with its water-screen, lights and fireworks, the signature experience). The town is open 09:00–22:00 with last entry at 21:00, and staying guests can come and go freely during their stay, with shuttle buses inside the resort.

The entry tier is a night at the Taolu boutique guesthouse (30–40㎡ king or balcony twin room) with breakfast for two, town tickets for two and “Flying Bird” show tickets for two, plus a Yao Lake souvenir gift, a Yao Lake shopping discount perk and 20% off selected restaurants. The Yaochang Resort Hotel tiers add the hillside train or a rowboat ride (pick one) and a wish lantern: ¥699 (≈$97, was ¥999) for a 72–76㎡ king/twin room for two, ¥799 (≈$111, was ¥1,099) for a 78㎡ three-person family room, ¥999 (≈$139, was ¥1,299) for a four-person family room. The Yao Lake Grand Hotel tiers run ¥799 (≈$111, was ¥1,199) for a 46㎡ Taoran room, ¥899 (≈$125, was ¥1,299) for a 46㎡ Zhuyin balcony room, and ¥999 (≈$139, was ¥1,399) for a 62㎡ family room (king + single bed, breakfast for three). Two-night options run ¥1,199 (≈$167, was ¥1,499) at the Yaochang and ¥1,299 (≈$180, was ¥1,699) at the Grand Hotel, the latter adding Red Chamber-themed attraction tickets.

Practical notes: surcharges are ¥100 per room-night on Fridays and ¥300 on Saturdays. Book by 17:00 the day before; check-in is from 15:00, check-out by 12:00, and you enter with your ID. Breakfast at the Yaochang runs 07:00–10:00, with children under 1.2 m eating free. Rooms are first-come, first-served after booking, and a confirmed reservation is generally non-refundable while unreserved vouchers can be refunded. Address: Yao Lake Town, Zhangzhu Town, Yixing, Wuxi, Jiangsu (navigate to the Yao Lake Town visitor centre).
