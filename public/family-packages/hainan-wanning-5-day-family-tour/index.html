This one is built for families who want a tropical break without the drill-sergeant schedule: no early mornings, no rushing, no changing hotels every night. The group meets in Haikou and ends in Sanya, spending the middle of the trip in Wanning — the stretch of coast that travel magazines keep calling China’s answer to Southern California, Bali and the Maldives in one place — and the pace is deliberately slow, with a professional tour leader looking after the kids the whole way.

The five days mix rainforest and beach. In Yanoda, a 5A rainforest park, kids explore the rainforest with a guide — learning the names and survival tricks of tropical plants — and finish with a rainforest graduation certificate. At Shenzhou Peninsula there’s a 1-on-4 surfing lesson with a professional coach, Amazon-style rainforest paddleboarding, and a floating-island carnival, plus a glass beach, a lighthouse, crab-catching on the shore and local coconut chicken. The hotels are part of the point: one night in a luxury-select hotel in Haikou, two nights in a row at the Arcadia Resort Hotel on Xiangshui Bay (a beachfront hotel the running-man TV show used), and one night inside the Yanoda rainforest itself — so the rainforest walks and glass viewing platforms start right outside your door, with no commute.
