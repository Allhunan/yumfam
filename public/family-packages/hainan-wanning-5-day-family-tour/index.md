**Deal price: 5 days / 4 nights from ¥3,980 per child** (≈$553, was ¥4,780, early-bird ¥800 off) · **Adults from ¥4,980** (≈$692) · Ages 5–15 · Departures Dec 25, 2026 – Feb 26, 2027 · Book at least 2 days ahead, then confirm with customer service before buying your flights

This one is built for families who want a tropical break without the drill-sergeant schedule: **no early mornings, no rushing, no changing hotels every night**. The group meets in Haikou and ends in Sanya, spending the middle of the trip in Wanning — the stretch of coast that travel magazines keep calling China's answer to Southern California, Bali and the Maldives in one place — and the pace is deliberately slow, with a professional tour leader looking after the kids the whole way.

The five days mix rainforest and beach. In **Yanoda**, a 5A rainforest park, kids explore the rainforest with a guide — learning the names and survival tricks of tropical plants — and finish with a rainforest graduation certificate. At **Shenzhou Peninsula** there's a 1-on-4 surfing lesson with a professional coach, Amazon-style rainforest paddleboarding, and a floating-island carnival, plus a glass beach, a lighthouse, crab-catching on the shore and local coconut chicken. The hotels are part of the point: **one night in a luxury-select hotel in Haikou, two nights in a row at the Arcadia Resort Hotel on Xiangshui Bay** (a beachfront hotel the running-man TV show used), and **one night inside the Yanoda rainforest** itself — so the rainforest walks and glass viewing platforms start right outside your door, with no commute.

**Packages**

- **5 days / 4 nights — from ¥3,980 per child** (≈$553, was ¥4,780): child price is for a child not taking a bed; **adults from ¥4,980** (≈$692). Includes the D2–D5 coach, 4 nights in the hotels above, breakfast on D2–D5 plus the marked lunches and dinners, all listed activities and a tour leader. A family of one can form a group; the group caps at 25 people.

**Departures** — Christmas Dec 25–29, New Year Dec 30 – Jan 3, winter-break departures Jan 18–22, Jan 23–27, Jan 28 – Feb 1, Feb 2–6, Feb 7–11, Feb 12–16, Feb 17–21 and Feb 22–26. Spring Festival departures (Feb 2–26) carry a supplement of ¥1,000–1,600 per person.

**Good to know:** this is a destination-joining tour — flights and trains to and from Hainan are **not included**, so buy your transport only after customer service confirms the group is running. One family can form a group (minimum one family, maximum 25 people). Ages are 5–15 for children. Pregnant travellers cannot join, and families with travellers over 65 should tell the operator before booking. Cancellation: free before 15 days out, then sliding charges — check the booking page's cancellation table before you pay.
