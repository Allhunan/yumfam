On the Hui’an coast in Quanzhou’s Taiwan Investment Zone, the Bashian Guohai (“Eight Immortals Crossing the Sea”) Resort pairs a night at one of two themed hotels with its Fairy Tale World theme park. You choose at booking: a Eurasia room at the Water Park Hotel (163 themed rooms, 35–70㎡, in a castle-style building between hill and sea; mountain or garden view, assigned) or a Navigator twin / Voyager family / Dreamer family room at the Zheng He Treasure Ship Hotel (200+ nautical-themed rooms, 50㎡ and up, all with balconies and a full set of kids’ amenities down to step stools and child toiletries). Either way the bundle adds buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.2m), Fairy Tale World admission for 2A2C with unlimited re-entry throughout the stay, welcome fruit on arrival, free parking, and evening access to the Treasure Ship’s private beach and the Ocean Hotel’s private pool.

The re-entry is the point: register at the front desk, enrol your face at the park gate, then drift in and out — ride in the morning, nap through the midday heat, go back for the evening. Off-peak weekends carry no surcharge.

⚠️ Two conditions to respect: reserve at least a day ahead (by 23:59 the day before; check-in 14:00, out 12:00), and note that once a reservation is confirmed it cannot be changed, cancelled or refunded — unreserved vouchers stay refundable, but pick your date carefully. Room types can’t be upgraded. Taller kids top up on site: breakfast ¥30 for 1.2–1.4m, ¥68 at 1.4m+; Fairy Tale World ¥130 / ¥170 on the same scale.

Good to know: the resort’s other big draw, the Ocean Kingdom marine park (beluga encounters, underwater theatre, sea-lion shows, 9:30–18:00), is a separate ticket not included here — several reviewers actually rate it the resort’s highlight, so budget for it if aquariums are your thing; the Russian circus is currently closed pending a reopening date. Driving in, navigate to “泉州欧乐堡海洋王国乐园”; buses 706 and K508 stop at the resort.
