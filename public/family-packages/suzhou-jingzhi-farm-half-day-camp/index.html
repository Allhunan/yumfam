Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child (under 12) half-day camp — ¥98 (≈$14, was ¥128) · Valid October 24 – December 13, 2026 · 09:00–16:00

Autumn turns the rice paddies gold at Jingzhi Farm （鲸治·部落生态循环农场）, an eco-farm in Linhu, Wuzhong — and this half-day camp drops the family right into the harvest. The morning runs on farm work and farm games: a grain talk and farm-tool intro, rice cutting and threshing, scarecrow making, tug-of-war, egg tapping, a paper-plane contest, goose ring toss and pet feeding. Between games there is a rice-paddy oil-painting photo stop, a paddy maze treasure hunt and the farm’s net-famous rice-paddy swing, and every adult gets a free Americano. The egg-tapping game has a real stake: beat the other families and the day’s “egg champion” takes home a free-range chicken if 30+ families are playing (or 10 eggs if fewer); the paper-plane winner takes home stream-tracing tickets for next year.

Good to know

  • Runs 09:00–16:00; buy and use the same day, no ticket exchange needed — show the booking code on arrival. Unused vouchers are refundable any time.
  • Lunch is not included (bamboo-tube rice, cured-pork rice, BBQ and roast meat are sold on site), and neither are sweet-potato digging, orange picking, board games or stream tracing. Some games run at set times, so expect to queue when it’s busy. Address: Jingzhi Farm, Yaojia Village, Linhu Town, Wuzhong District, Suzhou, Jiangsu.
