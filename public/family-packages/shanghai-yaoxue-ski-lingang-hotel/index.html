Shanghai’s Yaoxue Ice & Snow World — a ~350,000㎡ indoor winter resort with a 98,000㎡+ real-snow hall kept at −3 °C to −5 °C — is the rare ski trip that needs no mountain. The hotel half of the deal is the Lingang Ice-Snow Mingcheng Hotel, the snow world’s own south-district partner hotel — a twin-peaks building styled like an ice kingdom, a short walk from the slopes, with ice-and-snow themed superior rooms (dry/wet separated bathrooms, picture windows, a kids’ playroom downstairs).

Be clear about the two kinds of fun on sale, because the tiers split cleanly: snow-play （娱雪） is the Nordic snow park — level 3 is the family sweet spot (igloo engineers, rainbow snow village, snow playground, space balls), and the snow train climbs to the Mont Blanc base camp on level 5 for sled runs and snow-ladder adventures. Skiing （滑雪） is the real slopes. The ¥799 entry tier is snow-play; actual ski tickets start at the ¥999 tier. Every tier includes a hot-spring session and free parking for hotel guests.

⚠️ Weekend surcharges & rules: Friday/Saturday nights add ¥100 on the snow-play tiers and ¥200 on the ski tiers. Book by 23:59 the day before; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Every guest checks in with their own ID — adults with ID cards, children (including infants) with the household register.
