A Yellow Sea beach break with the kids’ parks built in: the ¥599 tier buys a deluxe room or a Senbo cabin room for 1 night with breakfast for 2, 2 tickets to either the water park or Fantasy Island (pick at check-in, unlimited re-entry until noon the next day), a family photo shoot (3 retouched photos + a 15-second video), welcome fruit and a first-night minibar round. The resort sits inside the Wanpingkou Seaside Scenic Area, so the beach is a walk away, and weekends and New Year carry no surcharge.

Step up for more: the ¥699 (≈$97) elegant room adds 20 arcade tokens and 2 7D cinema tickets per night; the ¥1288 (≈$179) 2-night elegant-room tier repeats the park tickets and perks nightly; and the ¥1688 (≈$234) Little Ocean family room sleeps more, with breakfast for 3 and 3 park tickets per night plus the photo shoot.

Good to know: book at least 1 day ahead by 23:59; check-in 15:00, checkout 12:00. ⚠️ Water park closure: the water park closes for maintenance from December 1, 2026 (expected through March 31, 2027) — during the closure, tickets are redeemable for Fantasy Island only, so winter stays are kids’-park stays, not water-park stays. Breakfast extras at the hotel: children under 1.2 m free, 1.2–1.5 m ¥68, over 1.5 m ¥98. Kids entering Fantasy Island must wear the designated socks; water-park visitors must wear swimwear. Unbooked bookings are refundable.
