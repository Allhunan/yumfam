The Anji Yinjun Jinjiang Castle Hotel （安吉银润锦江城堡酒店， 8 Tianshi Avenue, Anji, Huzhou) is every kid’s fantasy base camp: a European-style castle in the bamboo hills of Anji — French-style gardens, an artificial lake, 386 rooms — with a covered walkway from the second floor straight into Hello Kitty Park, Sanrio’s first park outside Japan. Sleep in the castle, stroll into the park, and with 2-day unlimited re-entry on the bundled tickets you can dip in and out by face scan at the gate — ride in the morning, nap at the hotel, come back for the evening lights.

The park itself covers six themed zones — Friendship Plaza, Happy Harbor, Music Village, Steam Kingdom, Elf Forest and Kitty’s Courtyard — with dozens of rides including the colorful slide Ferris wheel (one of very few in Asia), the parade, character meet-and-greets, and a water-play zone in Happy Harbor. Back at the castle there’s an indoor kids’ play center (trampoline, slides, ocean-ball pool), a gym, the castle cinema and free parking, and deluxe rooms start at 35㎡ with oversized tubs.

⚠️ Mind the Saturday surcharge: most tiers add ¥100/room/night on Saturdays; the Hello Kitty themed room adds ¥300 on Fridays and Saturdays. Book by 12:00 noon the day before arrival (earlier than most listings) — confirmed bookings are generally non-refundable, unbooked ones refund anytime. Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00, and every guest needs ID (household register for children). One money-saving tip from a guest review below: if you need extra kids’ park tickets, buy the unlimited-entry child tickets at the hotel front desk rather than elsewhere.
