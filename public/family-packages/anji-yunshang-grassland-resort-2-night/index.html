High in Anji’s bamboo hills (Shanchuan Township), the Cloud Grassland resort spreads across roughly 5,000 mu of mountain: the Xianxia Town at the 1,168 m summit runs 100+ immersive performances day and night with costumed NPCs hidden around its lanes; the High Mountain Grassland has a Hobbit-style village, rainbow and rail grass-sliding, a cloud train and a petting zoo (alpacas, ponies, Dorper sheep); and the Cliff Heartbeat park adds a 2.3 km cable car, the A-shaped Sky Stair glass platform jutting 80.9 m over the cliff, cloud swings up to 12 m and an 82 m crystal corridor bridge. This autumn off-peak bundle puts you in the resort’s Yunshanjing Hotel — an elegant king/twin for 2 consecutive nights — and the voucher is redeemable across all three resort hotels: upgrade to a basic room at the bamboo-hidden Yinzhu (white-walled villa clusters, no more than three floors, rooms with viewing balconies) or the pet-friendly Songsushu Tribe Hotel (100 treehouse-style rooms among the pines, with squirrels, alpacas, marmots and peacocks on site, a mini coaster, game rooms and kids’ toiletries provided) for +¥200/night, paid on site.
