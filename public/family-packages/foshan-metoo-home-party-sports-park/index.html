A rainy-day (or scorching-day) lifesaver for families in the Greater Bay Area: METOO HOME PATRY in Lecong, Shunde is a 10,000㎡ sci-fi-themed sports arena across two floors, with 11 themed zones and 60+ activities. The ground floor (4,000㎡, 7 m ceilings) is the physical side — climbing and slide matrices, go-karts and a zipline. Upstairs (6,000㎡) goes digital: third-generation VR free-roam, F1 simulator rigs, digital skiing and motion games, plus a sports zone with bowling, baseball, basketball, tennis, billiards and shooting/archery. Three themed escape rooms (cliff, sinkhole, slate) and a social floor with karaoke, golf simulators and live-action CS round it out — genuinely pitched at everyone from young kids to team-building adults.

⚠️ Read before you go — the fine print that matters: The arena is closed on Mondays (except public holidays). Hours: Tue–Fri 10:30–21:00; weekends & holidays 10:00–21:00. The 3-hour pass does NOT allow re-entry once you leave, and it needs a ¥40/person deposit (refunded in full if you don’t overstay). All-day tickets do allow re-entry — out for up to 90 minutes at a time, 180 minutes total. Everyone plays in grip socks (bring your own or buy at the desk for ¥5/pair; no shoes, no bare feet), and a ¥1.4/person accident-insurance fee applies to every ticket.
