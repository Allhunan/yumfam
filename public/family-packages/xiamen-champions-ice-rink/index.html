Forget the synthetic panels — this is real ice, 1,600 square meters of it, an Olympic-standard sheet held at a constant -10°C year-round. Champions Ice Rink (冠军冰场) in Xiamen’s Zhonghua City has been the city’s serious skating venue since 2015, with a full ice-making system, professional lighting and sound, and a coaching team of competition-level athletes. One ticket covers two skaters for a full 2 hours — take the kids, take a date, or split it with a friend.

Everything practical is bundled: skate, helmet and sock rental is free, insurance is included, and the rink is right in the Zhongshan Road commercial district — shopping, restaurants and the mall’s other entertainment are all in the same building, so the skate session slots neatly into a family day out. There’s a gear-up area, spectator seating, and coaches on hand (coaching itself is extra, if your kid wants hands-on instruction).

⚠️ Fine print that matters: usable Monday to Sunday. Refundable anytime before use; expired codes auto-refund. Book ahead online, then walk in — no ticket exchange needed. Two hours genuinely fills fast with kids; reviewers note it’s “very worth it for the price.”
