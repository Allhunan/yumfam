In Shapingba’s Sunac Cultural Tourism City, the “Crazy Gods Carnival” （疯神狂欢节） is Chongqing Sunac’s Halloween festival (Oct 16 – Nov 1; Halloween programme through Oct 31), spanning two parks. Yule Town （渝乐小镇） is the outdoor amusement park: its passes bundle 28 rides, from the Legend of Twin Dragons coaster and Fly Over Chongqing simulator to a pirate ship, canyon rafting and a kids’ zone. Sea World （海世界）, inside Sunac Mall next door, is the aquarium park (7 rides, marine shows). Most Yule Town passes play for 2 consecutive days, and the afternoon, student, single and combo passes each add one session at the Youming Yizhan haunted house （幽冥役站） within the 2 days.

⚠️ The booking rules are strict — plan around them:

  • Every pass must be bought by 23:59 the day before your visit and is valid only for the date you select.
  • Afternoon passes run 16:00–22:00 (last entry 30 min before closing). Yule Town opens 11:00–21:00 Mon–Fri and 11:00–22:00 on weekends; Sea World runs 10:00–18:00 and is single-entry only.
  • Max 1 ticket per order, and partly used orders can’t be refunded — split orders if your group needs different ticket types.
  • Kids under 100cm ride free at Yule Town with a paying adult; at Sea World, kids under 1.2m and seniors 65+ enter free with ID. Under-1.5m children need one paying adult riding with each of them.
  • Not included: pay-per-play extras (the Wugu Jinghun haunted house, rainbow glass slide, climbing, karts, snow castle, typhoon experience), food and shopping.
