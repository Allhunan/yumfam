Chengdu in 5 days, done properly. This winter family tour runs from the mystery of Sanxingdui to the giant pandas, from the Three Kingdoms stories of Wuhou Shrine to Du Fu’s thatched cottage, and from the 2,000-year-old Dujiangyan irrigation works to a night walk down Chunxi Road. The centrepiece is a 100-minute palace dinner show, the Jingong Liyan — six acts and ten performances while you eat, a living museum of Shu culture on the plate (a Shugong Banquet may substitute depending on the date).

The panda research is hands-on, not a photo stop: at the Dujiangyan panda base kids analyse panda “green dumplings” (dung) with a science teacher, make paper from bamboo fibre the old way, try movable-type printing, and cook panda “wotou” buns with a nutritionist — earning a Panda Guardian certificate. At Sanxingdui, kids dig in a 1:1 replica sacrificial pit, cast a small bronze vessel in a real workshop and try artefact restoration, earning a Sanxingdui archaeology certificate. Add a Sichuan-opera face-changing show, a Dujiangyan water-engineering class where kids build a traditional macha tool, and 7 included main meals across the trip.

Where you stay: 2 nights in Chengdu at the 339 Tianfu Panda Tower Lire Hotel (or the Shimao Chengdu Mao Yu, same 5-diamond level) by the Panda Tower, Chunxi Road and Taikoo Li; then 2 nights at Qingcheng Mountain Xikang Yun She (or the Minyue Qingchengshan Zun), where every room has its own private plunge pool, plus a giant panda-themed infinity pool, hot-spring pools, a kids’ castle and a pet park. Transport is by tour bus, a family tour leader travels with the group, and a photographer joins groups of 10+.

  • Cancellation: free up to 15 days before departure, then 20% (8–15 days), 60% (1–8 days), 100% within 1 day

  • Address/meeting point: Chengdu (exact meeting hotel confirmed after booking); the tour ends in Chengdu on day 5
