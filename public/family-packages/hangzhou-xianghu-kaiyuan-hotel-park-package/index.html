Inside Xianghu National Tourist Resort (Phase 3, Xiaoshan), the Hangzhou Xianghu Baoyi Kaiyuan Mingdu Resort Hotel is a 24,000㎡ Tang-dynasty-styled “art museum” of a hotel — staggered terraces, water-mirror courtyards, a rooftop garden — on the lake’s quiet edge. Its bundles are mix-and-match: a lakeside night plus your pick of kayaking or a Xianghu cruise for two, Senbo Water Park / Fantasy Island family tickets, Changqiao Polar Ocean Park + kids’ park tickets, the Xianghu Yayun show, afternoon tea, or dinner for two. Every tier also includes a free 2-hour hanfu experience, a kids’ DIY craft session, welcome fruit, the heated indoor pool, gym and indoor parking.

Rooms run 35–38㎡ (superior twin with two 1.2 m beds, or a king room with a 1.8 m bed, floors 3–5), and breakfast for two is in every bundle. Note the geography, per recent guests: the hotel sits at the scenic area’s third phase, so the parks are a short drive rather than a walk — the trade-off is a much quieter stay. Nearby: the Jinsha beach park and Xianghua Island’s flower fields.
