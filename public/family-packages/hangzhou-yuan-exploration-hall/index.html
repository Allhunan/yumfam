This is the rainy-day parenting heavyweight of Hangzhou: the Yuan Exploration Hall （元探索馆）, 8,000㎡ across four floors of the Wahaha Building podium in Xiaoshan (620 Mingxing Road, right by Metro Line 7’s Mingxing Road station), filled with 100+ German-imported interactive exhibits across 20 themed zones — and the house rule is the opposite of a normal museum: everything is meant to be touched, climbed, pumped, built and tested.

The headliners: a 16-meter climbing frame themed on Journey to the West; the Flying Car Factory, where kids assemble their own car and test how different slopes change its run (the hall also displays a real car taken apart piece by piece); the Water’s Journey play zone; an Air Maze of valves and flying scarves; Plato’s Cave for light-and-shadow experiments; a Sound Lab where kids do their own foley recording; a dress-up corner for stepping into famous paintings; and toddler zones (a soft under-3s climbing area, a “little Olympia” sports corner) so younger siblings aren’t sidelined. There’s a calm-down room and nursing rooms, a library-and-tea-bar on the 3rd floor where parents can actually sit down, and a restaurant on the 4th.

⚠️ Read the card mechanics before buying. Here “adult” means 18+ and “kid” means under 18. The 2-visit card works as 1 adult + 1 kid twice, or 2 adults + 2 kids once — but entry is by wristband and leaving the hall ends that visit (the restaurant is the one exception), and a partially used order can’t be refunded — the merchant’s own advice is to place separate orders if you want to split visits. Each phone number can buy only one card in this sales round. Extra adults join on a ¥138 companion ticket; babies under 6 months enter free as a companion. Hours: Tue–Fri 9:30–17:00, weekends & holidays 10:00–20:00, closed Mondays.
