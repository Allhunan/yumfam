This one is built around a single idea: skip the queues. Instead of the crowded Harbin–Snow Town circuit, it anchors in Yichun, in the Lesser Khingan Mountains — the snow forest from the Chinese primary-school textbook chapter The Beautiful Lesser Khingan Mountains — and keeps the group in the forest for four days. The pitch is that the snow is just as deep, the forest is quieter, and nothing on the itinerary involves standing in line. Harbin still bookends the trip (nights one and five at the Harbin International Convention Centre Hilton, opened July 2025), with free time at the start and end for Central Street, St Sophia Cathedral and the Ice and Snow World — which is running a Disney collaboration this winter, and whose ticket is not included.

The route, as listed on the live product page:

  • Jinshan Deer Park, the “Little Nara of the Northeast” — a full morning with the sika deer in the snow: hand-feeding, photos in the rime-frost forest, and no queue for a photo spot.
  • Riyuexia ski resort — a 4S-standard ski area with one coach per family, teaching from first steps to snowplough stops; skis, boots and helmet included, plus a snow ATV, a snow “long dragon” tube train and ice fishing with a local fisherman.
  • Xingyue Fairy-tale Village — a whitewashed “Santorini in the snow” guest village for one night: splash-freezing water in the morning, ice lanterns and fireworks around a bonfire at night, and an ice-play park outside the door.
  • Northeast Tiger Park — the “thrill vehicle” drives among adult tigers close enough to see their whiskers, with a research tutor explaining wild-tiger conservation on board.
  • Harbin Institute of Technology Aerospace Museum — real Long March rocket engines and re-entry satellites up close.
  • Forest walks with a ranger — tracking roe deer and squirrel prints in the snow, calling wild birds to land on your hand, and a red-pine adoption ceremony where each child hangs a nameplate on a tree.

What’s included: 5 nights with breakfast (2 nights Harbin Hilton, 1 night Xingyue Fairy-tale Village, 2 nights Yichun Yuenanshan Resort), 5 breakfasts, 4 lunches and 3 dinners, all first-gate tickets listed above, a “2+1” first-class coach throughout, one family leader, one research tutor, one photographer and one driver per group, plus a research handbook, travel photos and a red-pine adoption certificate. Travel insurance is included.

Good to know

  • This is a destination join-in tour — your transport to and from Harbin is not included.
  • Harbin Ice and Snow World is not included: visit it during the free time in Harbin and buy its ticket separately.
  • Minimum booking is 1 adult + 1 child; the child price does not include a bed — children must occupy a bed, so add the ¥700 room difference. If 1 adult travels with 2 children, one child pays the adult price.
  • Groups need 15 people to run and cap at 29; confirm with customer service after booking and before buying your transport.
  • Unused vouchers are refundable. Ages 5–15; pregnant travellers cannot join, and travellers over 60 must sign a waiver (over 70 cannot join).
  • The Xingyue Fairy-tale Village night is a non-standard guesthouse — rooms and service differ from a city star-rated hotel. Skiing is a high-risk activity: follow the coach, wear a helmet throughout, and bring your own goggles and gloves.
