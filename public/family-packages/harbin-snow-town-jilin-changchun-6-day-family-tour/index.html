This is a four-city loop through the Northeast’s ice season — Harbin, Snow Town, Jilin and Changchun — drawn as one route with no backtracking, so every day lands somewhere new. The headline stay is night one in Harbin’s Aoluguya Hyatt Centric, a forest-culture luxury hotel themed on the Ewenki reindeer tribe, about 10 minutes from the Ice and Snow World — the detail page notes it is the only hotel of its kind in China and that peak-season rooms there pass ¥2,000 a night. The Snow Town night is inside the scenic area itself at a 4-diamond hotel, a step up from the hard kang beds the town is notorious for: proper beds, private bathroom and heating, a 3-minute walk to Snow Rhythm Street.

The route, as listed on the live product page:

  • Harbin — free time for Central Street, St Sophia Cathedral and the Ice and Snow World (its ticket is not included — buy it separately during the free time), plus the Aoluguya hotel’s reindeer-forest theme.
  • Snow Town — a night walk down Snow Rhythm Street, the evening float parade and yangge dance, a snow disco, and a warrior-vehicle ride through “Dog Bear Ridge” to Yangcao Mountain, with a 1.5 km snow-raft ride back down through the forest.
  • Hengdaohezi — the “Little Kamakura of the Northeast”, a Russian-style railway town, with a research session at the Northeast Tiger Park (including the “thrill vehicle” that drives among the tigers) and a tiger-meal-making class for kids.
  • Jilin & Songhua Lake — 3 hours of skiing with gear included (skis, boots, poles, helmet), a 2-hour snow-play park, and one cable-car ride to the summit for the rime-ice views over the Songhua River.
  • Changchun — an ice carnival on the frozen lake and a stay at the Changchun Hyatt to close the loop.

What’s included: 5 nights with breakfast (Harbin Aoluguya, Mudanjiang Hawaii International, Snow Town Ruixue Yunting, Jilin Shimao Wanjin, Changchun Hyatt), 5 breakfasts, 3 lunches and 2 dinners, all first-gate tickets listed above, a “2+1” first-class coach throughout, one family leader, one research tutor, one photographer and one driver per group, plus a research handbook, travel photos and a souvenir.

Good to know

  • This is a destination join-in tour — your transport to Harbin and home from Changchun is not included.
  • Harbin Ice and Snow World is not included: visit it during the free time in Harbin and buy its ticket separately.
  • Minimum booking is 1 adult + 1 child; the child price does not include a bed — children must occupy a bed, so add the room difference (¥1,500 in the winter break, ¥2,500 over Spring Festival). If 1 adult travels with 2 children, one child pays the adult price.
  • Groups need 15 people to run and cap at 28; buy at least 3 days before departure and confirm with customer service after booking.
  • Cancellation: free 15+ days before departure, 20% loss at 8 days, 60% at 1 day, 100% within 1 day.
  • Ages 5–15; pregnant travellers cannot join, and travellers over 60 must sign a waiver (over 70 cannot join). Rime ice and frozen-river views are weather-dependent, not guaranteed.
