Yuehua is the five-star hotel with the big garden in Huli, and this deal pairs its buffet with the thing that makes it a family outing rather than just a meal: a plant treasure hunt through the hotel’s 70,000 m² Xinyue Garden, with a map to collect from the restaurant reception. Eat, then go and hunt — the garden has lawns, a playground and animals to look at, and the kids do not want to leave.

The buffet itself runs the usual Yuehua spread — a seafood counter with prawns, baby cuttlefish and crab claws on ice, a sashimi counter, hot dishes kept warm under lamps, salads, fruit, a flower-tea corner and a dessert cabinet with cakes, brownies and mousse — and the menu rotates with the season, so the dishes on the day are whatever the kitchen is serving.

Good to know

  • Book at least a day ahead, by 20:00 the day before — the restaurant asks guests to call to book, and it gets busy at mealtimes. Unbooked vouchers can be refunded, but once booked the voucher is non-refundable.
  • Each adult can bring one child under 1.3 m; extra or over-height children under 1.4 m pay ¥80 each on site, and children over 1.4 m pay the adult price.
  • Not valid on New Year’s Eve, Valentine’s Day, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day or Chinese New Year’s Eve; the price does not apply to group bookings.
  • For the treasure hunt, collect the map from the 2nd-floor Lüting restaurant reception, then enter the garden through the Huating north gate — security will let you in on showing the map.
  • Expired and unused vouchers refund automatically.
