Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child (under 1.2 m), lunch or weekday dinner — ¥219 (≈$31, was ¥298) · Valid July 15 – December 30, 2026 · Book at least 1 day ahead

The Portman Seven Star Bay （佰翔波特曼七星湾酒店） sits on the Tong’an coast in Xiamen, and its Le Wei all-day restaurant runs the hotel buffet: lunch 12:00–14:30 every day, dinner 17:30–21:00 (weekdays on this voucher; weekends need the weekend-dinner tier). The spread rotates with the season, but the shape stays the same — seafood, Chinese and Western dishes, and French-style desserts — and the restaurant will make baby food (steamed egg, white congee) for the smallest diners. One child under 1.2 m eats free with each adult; children 1.2–1.4 m take the child ticket, and anyone over 1.4 m pays the adult price.

Good to know

  • Book at least 1 day ahead by 18:00; show the booking code on arrival. Unbooked vouchers are refundable, and unused ones refund automatically after December 30, 2026. Once booked, the voucher is non-refundable.
  • Not usable on Dec 24–25, 2026 (and the earlier blackout dates in Sep–Oct have passed). Public holidays, festive dinners and the hotel’s special food festivals may need a top-up at the restaurant’s counter price — and the Christmas Eve dinner is excluded outright, so don’t plan the 24th around this voucher.
  • One practical tip from past guests: the hotel has several restaurants, and the booking page doesn’t name the venue — head for the Le Wei all-day restaurant. Address: Portman Seven Star Bay Hotel, No. 7 Guanzhou Road, Tong’an District, Xiamen, Fujian.
