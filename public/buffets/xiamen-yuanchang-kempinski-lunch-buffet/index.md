A five-star buffet at a neighbourhood price. The **Kempinski Hotel** is the 198-metre sail-shaped tower on Yundang Lake in central Xiamen, and its **2nd-floor café** has been serving its buffet for 12 years — the menu is refreshed every season and the regulars keep coming back. Expect 100+ dishes: a fresh seafood counter, European, Japanese and Southeast Asian stations and Chinese classics, all cooked to order at open kitchens. Dinner adds salmon, grilled oysters, teppanyaki foie gras and Peking duck, served course by course.

**Packages** — lunch and dinner, single or double; every 2 adults can bring 1 child under 6 free

- **Lunch for one — ¥156** (≈$22, was ¥278)
- **Lunch for two — ¥306** (≈$43, was ¥556)
- **Dinner for one — ¥218** (≈$30, was ¥394)
- **Dinner for two — ¥428** (≈$59, was ¥788)

**Good to know:** The buffet runs Wednesday to Sunday — Mondays and Tuesdays are not covered. Weekends and New Year carry no surcharge. Book by 20:00 the day before; unbooked orders are refundable and the voucher auto-refunds after expiry. Child policy: every 2 adults can bring 1 child under 6 free; children 6–12 pay half the walk-in price, and children 12 and over pay the full adult price. Menus rotate with the season, so the dishes on the day may differ slightly from the photos.
