Xiamen Beihaiwan — 'Crayfish Festival' Buffet, 1 Adult + 1 Kid from ¥118 (+ Free Playground Ticket)

Beihaiwan Howard Johnson Wanda Plaza Xiamen's 'Crayfish Festival' all-you-can-eat buffet: 10+ crayfish flavors done fresh to order, plus sashimi, grilled stations, sushi bar and dozens of desserts — with a free kids' trampoline park or waterpark ticket thrown in.

Deal price: from ¥118 (≈$16) for 1 adult + 1 kid (lunch) · 2 adults + 2 kids lunch ¥216 (≈$30) · Dinner from ¥138 (≈$19) · Xialv Money-Saver Card: ¥30 off this product + ¥50 welcome bonus on signup

The Beihaiwan Howard Johnson Wanda Plaza (Jimei District, Xiamen — ranked among the city’s Top 10 family resorts) is running a “Crayfish Festival” themed buffet, and the crayfish situation is gloriously over the top: 10+ flavors, all-you-can-eat, made fresh to order — iced/chilled crayfish, spicy, garlic, curry, steamed, braised, thirteen-spice, tom yum, bo-bo chicken style, even crayfish nigiri and hand rolls. Beyond the crayfish stations there’s chilled seafood, live grilling, a sushi bar, hot dishes and dozens of desserts (guests rave about the dessert spread enough to call it “an afternoon tea after the buffet”).

The kicker for families: every package throws in a free kids’ ticket to either the trampoline park or the waterpark — the ¥118 lunch for 1 adult + 1 kid (kid under 1.4m) effectively doubles as a half-day family outing. Lunch runs 11:30–14:00 on Saturdays and Sundays; dinner 17:30–21:00 Friday through Sunday — weekends and public holidays included, so it’s usable right through the National Day week. Reservations need to be made a day ahead, and the deal is refundable anytime, with automatic refunds on expiry.

⚠️ One timing note: the crayfish theme wraps up on Oct 7, 2026 — after that the buffet continues with its regular spread, but the 10-flavor crayfish stations go away. Book now if the crayfish is the draw.
