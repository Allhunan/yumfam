Xiamen Xianglu International Hotel Buffet — Chaoshan Beef Hotpot Feast, ¥108 (≈$15)

Xiamen Xianglu International Hotel's Four Seasons restaurant buffet, now on a Chaoshan beef hotpot theme for autumn and winter: fresh-cut beef hotpot plus all the sides, ¥108 for lunch or dinner, weekends and holidays included — or grab the splittable 10-visit card at about ¥100 a visit.
October 28, 2026 · YumFam

Deal price: single buffet, lunch or dinner interchangeable — from ¥108 (≈$15) (was ¥198, about 55% of list) · Weekends & holidays included · ⏰ valid through Mar 10, 2027

Xianglu International Hotel’s Four Seasons restaurant has switched its buffet to a Chaoshan beef hotpot theme for autumn and winter — and at ¥108 it’s one of the best-value hotel buffets in Xiamen right now. The star is fresh-cut beef, sliced to order and never held overnight: dip it for seconds in a clear southern-ginger beef-bone broth or a satay-spiced pot, with the classic Chaoshan dips (satay sauce, Puning bean paste, celery bits) ready to go. Alongside the beef: sliced chicken, pork and lamb rolls, mushrooms, tofu and vegetable sides, meatballs and fish balls, and a rotating cast of small eats.

Save room for the extras that make this buffet stand out: a DIY candied-fruit station (pick your fruit, drizzle it with plum juice, dust it with plum powder or chili) and a Guangzhou sweet-potato dessert station with adzuki, barley, longan, osmanthus honey and taro balls, served hot or cold. Menus rotate with the season, so the exact lineup follows the day’s kitchen.

⚠️ Booking & extras: Valid Friday to Sunday only. Lunch is served Saturday–Sunday 11:30–14:00; dinner Friday–Sunday 17:30–21:00. Reserve by 21:00 the day before; refundable any time before you reserve. Each adult may bring one child under 1.2 m free. The 10-visit card can be split across visits, but once partially used it cannot be refunded.
