Xiamen InterContinental Seaview Hotel — Weekend 1 Adult + 1 Child Buffet from ¥158 (≈$22)

InterContinental Xiamen's seaview buffet on Huandao Road: 9 food stations, 100+ dishes with a fresh seafood-season menu — 1 adult + 1 child weekend lunch ¥158, dinner ¥198, kids' playground free.
October 12, 2026 · YumFam

Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child (under 1.2 m) — weekend lunch ¥158 (≈$22) / dinner ¥198 (≈$28) · Lunch Sat–Sun 11:30–14:00 · Dinner Wed–Sat 17:30–21:00 · Valid Sep 2 – Dec 31, 2026 · Book by 21:00 the day before

The InterContinental Xiamen （厦门海景洲际酒店） sits on the quiet eastern end of Huandao Road, steps from the beach and the convention center — look for the giant whale sculpture floating above the lobby, the hotel’s beloved mascot. Its Straits all-day restaurant runs the buffet families keep coming back for: 9 stations and 100+ dishes, currently in seafood season. Dinner is where the big guns come out — king crab legs on weekend evenings, Alaska snow crab legs on weekday evenings, plus Taiwan oysters, razor clams and a rotating cast of Fujian-style seafood, all self-serve. The sashimi counter cuts tuna, salmon and octopus to order; the teppanyaki station does steaks, lamb chops and skewers with the occasional flame show; and there are noodle soups (wonton, Xiamen satay, chicken broth), German pork knuckle, BBQ ribs, a chocolate fountain, and red and white wine included at dinner.

Parents get the real perk: the InterContinental Kids’ Planet playground right by the restaurant is free with this deal — ocean-themed play zones, a trampoline and interactive games, plus a dedicated kids’ buffet corner — so children can burn off energy between courses while adults eat in peace. Walk it off afterward with a stroll down to the beach; the hotel is an easy wander from Huangcuo beach and Zengcuo’an village.

⚠️ Days and blackouts to note: lunch vouchers are Saturday–Sunday only; dinner vouchers run Wednesday–Saturday only. Blackout dates: Sep 25–27, Oct 1–7, Oct 31, Dec 24 and Dec 25, 2026. You must book by 21:00 the day before, and once a booking is confirmed the voucher becomes non-refundable (unused, unbooked vouchers refund anytime and auto-refund after expiry). The voucher can’t be combined with the hotel’s own promotions or used during buyout events — worth a quick call ahead on big convention weekends.
