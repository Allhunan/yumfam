Xiamen Thai Food Festival Buffet at Garden Hotel — 1 Adult + 1 Kid Lunch ¥148 (≈$21)

Thai food-festival weekend buffet at the historic Xiamen Hotel garden property: 1 adult + 1 kid under 1.3 m lunch for ¥148 (was ¥228) with a seafood counter, teppan grills, Minnan snacks and a dessert counter — weekends, no holiday surcharge.
October 10, 2026 · YumFam

Deal price: 1 adult + 1 kid under 1.3 m lunch — ¥148 (≈$21) (was ¥228) · Dinner ¥188 (≈$26) · Bookable Jul 22 – Dec 30, 2026 · Lunch Sat/Sun, dinner Fri–Sun, weekends and holidays at no surcharge · ⏰ Reserve by 19:00 the day before

The venue is half the story here: the Xiamen Hotel （厦门宾馆） on Huyuan Road in Siming District is a 60-plus-year-old garden property — Minnan red-brick courtyards, quiet grounds in the middle of the city, opposite Xiamen No. 1 Middle School and a short walk from Zhongshan Park metro station and the Wanshi Botanical Garden. Its Pearl of the Sea Café （海上明珠咖啡厅） is currently running a Thai Food Festival theme on top of its regular spread, and the format is an old-school Xiamen buffet done generously.

The seafood counter is the main event: fresh shrimp, stone crab, red conch and big clams on ice, plus a dressed-seafood （捞汁） selection with octopus and pearl clams. Open teppan and grill stations cook to order — roast lamb chops and steak are the signatures — alongside nostalgic Minnan dishes like the Xiamen oyster omelette. Dinner (17:30–21:00) adds oysters and the small individual hotpots guests keep mentioning. Desserts run to nearly ten kinds — durian cheesecake, matcha cherry cake, mango mousse, Napoleon — plus a chocolate fountain that keeps the kids occupied. Lunch runs 11:30–14:00.

⚠️ Dates and child pricing: vouchers run through Dec 30, 2026, but Dec 24–25 are blacked out. Each adult brings one child under 1.3 m free; a child of 1.3–1.4 m either tops up on site (¥89 lunch / ¥109 dinner) or uses the dedicated child ticket below; over 1.4 m pays the adult price. Unreserved vouchers refund anytime and unused ones auto-refund after expiry — but once you’ve booked a date, that booking can’t be refunded.
