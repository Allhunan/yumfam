Deal price: boiled live fish set for 3–4 people — ¥138 (≈$19, was ¥269, about 49% off) · Valid through Dec 31, 2026 · Delivery or pickup — no dine-in

Yubaofu （鱼宝福） in Wenzhao is one of Xiamen’s busiest boiled-fish restaurants — the kind of place locals queue for. This deal brings the signature dish to you: a whole fresh live fish, cooked in a bubbling spicy broth with plenty of sides, enough for 3–4 people at about ¥35 a head, with a big bottle of Coke included. It is delivery or pickup only — there is no dine-in on this deal. Delivery is free anywhere on Xiamen island (except Gulangyu), or pick it up from the Wenzhao shop yourself. Book 4 hours ahead; usable 09:00–13:30 and 16:00–21:00.
