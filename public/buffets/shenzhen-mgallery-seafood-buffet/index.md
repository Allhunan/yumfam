**Deal price: themed seafood buffet dinner for two — ¥358** (≈$50, was ¥1,099) · Valid through Dec 30, 2026 · Not valid Dec 24–25 · Dinner 17:30–21:00 · Book at least 4 hours ahead

Shenzhen's MGallery hotel in Longhua puts on a full seafood buffet that reads like a greatest-hits list: an ice pool of baby abalone, giant river prawns, New Zealand green-lipped mussels, sea snails and prawns; sashimi and sushi sliced to order; oysters served raw, garlic-grilled, black-bean-grilled or in an air pot; and a charcoal grill turning out Beef Wellington, lamb and Atlantic cod alongside stir-fried crab three ways and Singapore laksa and giant grouper soup from the noodle station. Desserts run to low-sugar Basque durian cake, canelés on weekends, six ice creams and seasonal fruit, with red and white wine, beer and soft drinks free-flow all evening.

The daily special is the part to plan around: **Fridays and Saturdays** each diner gets a portion of aged Huadiao wine drunken crab plus freshly opened durian, while **Sundays to Thursdays** the special is a serving of Buddha Jumps Over the Wall.

- Themed seafood buffet dinner for two, any day of the week — **¥358** (≈$50, was ¥1,099)
- Themed seafood buffet dinner for 2 adults + 1 child (1.2–1.4 m), any day of the week — **¥399** (≈$55, was ¥1,599)
- Second wave, dinner for two — **¥368** (≈$51, was ¥1,099); second wave, 2 adults + 1 child — **¥478** (≈$66, was ¥1,599)

**Good to know**

- Valid Aug 18 – Dec 30, 2026. Not usable Dec 24–25 (Christmas Eve and Christmas Day).
