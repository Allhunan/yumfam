Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child lunch from ¥168 (≈$23, was ¥299) · Lunch valid Sep 25 – Dec 27, 2026, weekends only · Dinner packages valid to Dec 31, 2026 · Book by 12:00 on the day of use · Refundable if unbooked

The Wanfu Hilton — a Hilton in the Wanda business district in Baiyun, right by the Guangzhou Children’s Activity Center — has put a crab theme on its buffet for the autumn: “Golden Autumn with Crabs”. The headline is the per-person crab dish: on weekdays you get drunken hairy crab or steamed hairy crab with ginger vinegar; on Friday to Sunday you get both. Around it, the buffet runs to 100+ dishes, and the hotel’s own rating on the booking platform is 4.8.

What’s on the spread: a crab station (Singapore black-pepper crab, salted-egg-yolk crab, Typhoon Shelter crab, Thai yellow-curry crab, rotating daily), a seafood section (cooked prawns, scallops, mussels, clams and baby octopus, plus tiger prawns and abalone Friday to Sunday), sashimi and sushi, grilled oysters and skewers, roast meats and Cantonese barbecue, hot dishes in Asian, Cantonese and Western styles, a cheese and salad section, and desserts — durian cake, Black Forest cake, coconut puddings and eight flavours of ice cream. Drinks include draught beer, red and white wine, soft drinks and sparkling water. At weekend lunch there’s a kids’ cooking class — DIY cakes, burgers and other hands-on food for children.
