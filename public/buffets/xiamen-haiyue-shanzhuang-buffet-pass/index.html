The Haiyue Shanzhuang (海悦山庄） at 3999 Huandao South Road is one of Xiamen’s grand old seaside resorts — a vast mountain-and-sea garden property on the Huangcuo beach side of the island. Its Mediterranean Café on the 2nd floor runs the buffet: window seats look over the blue infinity pool and the gardens, and the stations run ten-plus themes deep — grill, Japanese, Chinese hot dishes, seafood, noodles and pastries, desserts — with 100+ dishes in all, from fresh coastal seafood and sashimi to old-school Minnan snacks. This pass covers 5 visits and is usable on weekends and public holidays at no surcharge, which is what makes it a family staple rather than a weekday-only curiosity.

The passes split, so five visits can be five separate family lunches or one big group outing. The math: the lunch pass works out to ¥133 (≈$19) per visit, and every visit lets you bring one child under 1.2 m free.

⚠️ How the pass works: reserve by 21:00 the day before each visit (phone 0592-5023333). Lunch runs 12:00–14:00, dinner 18:00–21:00. The pass can be split across visits, but once it’s partially used the remaining visits can’t be refunded — and unreserved, untouched passes refund anytime. The single blackout date is Dec 24. Last usable day: Jan 31, 2027.
