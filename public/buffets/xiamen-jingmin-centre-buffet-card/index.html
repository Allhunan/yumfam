Deal price: 5-visit family buffet card — ¥699 (≈$99, was ¥1,255, about ¥140 per visit) · Valid Nov 11, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Friday–Sunday only · A single weekend visit is ¥168 (≈$24, was ¥251)

Jingmin Centre Hotel （厦门京闽中心酒店） is one of Xiamen’s long-established hotels, and its Yubo Garden buffet is the old-reliable kind: 100+ dishes spanning roast meats, sashimi and seafood, carved steak and lamb chops, crab, sushi, salads, teppanyaki, desserts, fruit, beer and soft drinks. Weekend lunch runs Saturday–Sunday 11:45–14:00; dinner runs Friday–Saturday 18:00–21:00 — the card and the single ticket are both valid for lunch or dinner.

The ¥699 card buys five visits, each for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.4 m. The visits can be split — five different families can each use one, or one family can come back five times. Each visit is limited to one family group and can’t be stacked. Children under 1.2 m eat free with an adult; children 1.2–1.4 m are ¥78; over 1.4 m pays the adult single price (¥168). The kids’ playground is free with the meal.

Good to know

  • Usable immediately after purchase; no advance booking needed, but calling ahead is recommended on busy weekends
  • Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year’s Eve, Chinese New Year’s Eve and Spring Festival may carry a top-up — check with the restaurant on those dates
  • Unused card is refundable; once partly used, the remaining visits can’t be refunded or converted to cash
  • Address: Yubo Garden buffet restaurant, Jingmin Centre Hotel, No. 158 Yuhou Nanli, Siming District, Xiamen
