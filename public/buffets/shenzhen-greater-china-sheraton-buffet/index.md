**Deal price: lunch for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.2 m — ¥138** (≈$19, was ¥298) · Valid Oct 15, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 · Weekends & New Year no top-up · Blacked out Dec 24, 25, 31 · Refundable if unbooked

The Sheraton's Yiju Xuan buffet in Futian is running a Taiwan night-market theme for dinner (until around Dec 15): oyster omelettes, salt-and-pepper chicken, braised beef offal with instant noodles, takoyaki, tempura, Taiwanese braised pork rice and curry fish balls — plus a weekend-only **grouper hotpot** (龙趸石斑鱼, Friday to Sunday dinner), roast meats, a teppanyaki station with garlic oysters and black-pepper steaks, sushi and sashimi, and free-flowing red and white wine. Lunch keeps the same spread at a lower price, and weekends and the New Year holiday carry **no top-up**.

**Packages**

- Lunch for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.2 m — **¥138** (≈$19, was ¥298)
- Dinner for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.2 m — **¥168** (≈$23, was ¥388)
- Lunch for 2 adults + 2 children (1.4 m + 1.2 m) — **¥308** (≈$43, was ¥598)
- Dinner for 2 adults + 2 children (1.4 m + 1.2 m) — **¥358** (≈$50, was ¥778)
- Child ticket, lunch or dinner — **¥68** (≈$9, was ¥188)

**Good to know**

- Valid Oct 15, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027, any day of the week; **not usable Dec 24, Dec 25 or Dec 31**. Lunch 12:00–14:30, dinner 18:00–21:30. Free parking for 3 hours.
- Book at least 1 day ahead — and at least 3 days ahead for weekends and holidays, when slots run out fast. Children under 1.2 m eat free; 1.2–1.4 m children use the child ticket; over 1.4 m pays the adult price.
- Dishes rotate with the season, so the spread on the day is what counts. The deal does not stack with Marriott Bonvoy member discounts or points, and it is not suitable for groups of 10 or more.
- Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded; once booked, no refund.
