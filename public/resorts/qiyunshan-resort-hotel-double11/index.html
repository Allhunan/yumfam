Qiyun Mountain （齐云山） in Xiuning County, Huangshan, is one of China’s four great Taoist mountains, and the Qiyun Mountain Resort Hotel sits inside the scenic area in Yanqian Village — you sleep in the attraction rather than commuting to it. Autumn is peak sea-of-clouds season, and every package bundles the room with the mountain: scenic-area admission, a bamboo raft ride on the Heng River, Xiaoyao Street entry and the Freehome family campsite — petting zoo, rope nets, sand treasure hunts, forest maze, mini golf and archery. Higher tiers add the Feiyue Qiyun flying experience, heritage craft sessions (papermaking, paper-cutting, iron painting, lacquer fans), night shows and shuttle transfers, and every tier includes a free room upgrade on a reservation note. Each room can bring one child under 1.2 m free.

⚠️ Good to know: Reserve by 20:00 on the day of use. Refundable if unreserved. The November under-18 free-admission offer applies to the scenic area ticket. Book early — rooms are first-come, first-served after purchase, and confirmed only once the hotel accepts the reservation.
