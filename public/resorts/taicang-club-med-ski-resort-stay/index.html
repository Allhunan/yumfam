Club Med’s Taicang resort sits inside the Alps Snow World indoor-ski complex — the “Oriental Alps” — about an hour’s drive from Shanghai or Suzhou, and a short ride from Taicang South railway station. This is a proper ski-in, ski-out style stay without the mountain weather: the beginner slope and snow-play areas are indoors, so the snow is guaranteed all winter, and the resort’s G.O. team runs the activities, evening shows and dance parties while you are there.

Both tiers cover two nights in a superior king or twin room with breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child every day (the child rate covers ages 4–11, or 1.2–1.4 m tall). The ¥1,698 tier adds two 3-hour beginner-slope ski passes (snow play included) for one session, one Amazing Family session for 1 adult + 1 child, and a two-person cycling or stretching class (pick one). The ¥1,898 tier swaps the 3-hour passes for two full-day beginner-slope passes and adds two 3-hour snow-play sessions. Gym and yoga room access, evening shows and interactive parties, and on-site parking are included on both tiers.

Good to know

  • Valid Nov 20, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027, any day of the week. Book by 18:00 the day before; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00.
  • Top-ups: Fridays in January +¥300 per night; Saturdays (except March), Dec 31, 2026 and Feb 5 & 11, 2027 +¥300 per night; New Year (Jan 1–2, 2027) and Spring Festival (Feb 6–10, 2027) +¥600 per night.
