The Beijing Junhe Howard Johnson （北京君和豪生酒店） is a European castle-style resort on the banks of the Chaobai River in Shunyi — the stretch locals call “Beijing’s Rhine” — next to the Hanshiqiao Wetland reserve and about 15 minutes from the Beijing International Flower Port. Its hot-spring season (Sep 1, 2026 – Mar 15, 2027) opens up more than 3,000㎡ of indoor and outdoor soaking pools, and every package here includes a spring pass with unlimited entry for the whole stay, so you can soak before breakfast, after dinner, and again the next morning.

The deluxe rooms are a proper 45㎡ (a 2 m king or two 1.35 m twins); family rooms pair a 2 m king with a 1.3 m single bed for the kid. Beyond the pools, the vouchers bundle in the neighboring fun: two tickets to the Sacred Deer Forest theme park (deer, alpacas and family rides), an alpaca-feeding pack, and a guide to the Yuemingjian scenic spot — plus the hotel’s own indoor pool, gym, Chinese and Western restaurants and a football play area. Breakfast for two is included on every tier, and little kids eat and soak free (under 1.2 m on the deluxe tiers; the family-room spring pass covers one child under 1.5 m).

⚠️ The voucher window is short: these packages can only be used through Nov 14, 2026, even though the hotel’s spring season runs into March — buy for a stay in the next few weeks, not as a winter stockpile. The only surcharge on the books (Sep 30 – Oct 3, +¥200/night) has passed, so weekends are genuinely flat for the rest of the window. Book by 23:00 the day before; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Unreserved vouchers refund anytime; once booked, the room is held for the day and is generally non-refundable.
