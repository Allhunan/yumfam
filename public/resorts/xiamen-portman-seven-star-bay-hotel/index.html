The Portman Seven Star Bay Hotel (No. 7 Guanzhou Road, Tong’an) is a French-style beachfront resort town — three hotel areas (Yihai, Xihai and Ruihai) around the Seven Star Park playground, with the beach right across the road. Its Double 11 bundles run to April 30, 2027, and every tier stacks the same family kit: daily buffet breakfast for two at the Lewei all-day restaurant (6:30–10:00 weekdays, to 10:30 weekends; up to two kids under 1.2 m eat free), Seven Star Park playground access (2 children under 12 plus 1 accompanying adult), 2 mini-club activity tickets, the first round of the in-room minibar free each day, a Fenghe photo-shoot session (30–60 minutes, 3 retouched digital photos — book at least 2 days ahead), free shuttle buses during concert periods, and 15%-off vouchers for the Haixing No.7 music beer restaurant set (¥233→¥198) and the Xiyuehui family set (¥369→¥313). Most tiers work as 1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night, and you can reserve as late as 23:59 on the day itself.
