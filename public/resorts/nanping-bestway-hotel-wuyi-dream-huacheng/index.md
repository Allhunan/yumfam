# Nanping Bestway Hotel + Wuyi Dream Huacheng Tickets — Garden-View Room from ¥439 (≈$61)

**Deal price: garden-view room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + 2 Wuyi Dream Huacheng tickets — ¥439** (≈$61, was ¥976) · Advanced standard room, 2 nights (or 1 night × 2 rooms) — **¥599** (≈$83, was ¥976) · Valid Nov 13, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 · Friday–Sunday only

Nanping Bestway Hotel sits in Jianyang, about 40 minutes' drive from Wuyi Mountain National Park, and this deal pairs the stay with the thing that makes the trip: Wuyi Dream Huacheng, an immersive Song-dynasty show village where you walk through ancient streets in costume. The tickets include a hanfu rental (3,000 sets to choose from), NPC immersive interaction and the Song-style buildings themselves — so the evening is a show, not just a room.

The hotel itself is a full-service star-rated stay with a fitness centre and an indoor heated pool, and both tiers include breakfast. The garden-view room is the one to pick if you want the show tickets bundled in; the standard-room tier skips the show but gives you two nights (or two rooms for one night) for ¥599, which works well for two families travelling together.

**Packages**

- Garden-view room, 1 night + breakfast for 2 + 2 Dream Huacheng tickets — **¥439** (≈$61, was ¥976): double or twin garden-view room, breakfast for 2, 2 show tickets with hanfu rental, NPC interaction and the Song-style buildings, plus fitness centre and indoor heated pool
- Advanced standard room, 2 nights (or 1 night × 2 rooms) + breakfast for 2 each night — **¥599** (≈$83, was ¥976): standard double or twin room, breakfast for 2 per night, fitness centre and indoor heated pool

**Good to know**

- Friday–Sunday only. Blackout dates: Feb 6–12, Feb 14 and Feb 20, 2027.
- Book by 23:59 the day before you stay. Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 the next day.
- Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
- Dream Huacheng tickets are collected at the hotel front desk.
- Extra breakfast is ¥89 per person on site, booked in advance.
- Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded, and expired unused vouchers refund automatically. Once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable.
