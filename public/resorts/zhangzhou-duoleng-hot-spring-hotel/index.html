Deal price: deluxe room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + unlimited hot-spring park for 2 for the whole stay — ¥299 (≈$42, was ¥999, about 30% of list) · Valid Nov 19, 2026 – Jun 30, 2027, any day of the week · No top-up on weekdays or weekends

Duoleng sits in Jiaomei, just across the water from Xiamen — close enough that metro Line 6 gets you there — and the deal is unusually simple for a hot-spring stay: one price covers the room, breakfast for two, and unlimited entry to the hot-spring park for the whole stay, with no weekend top-up at all. The park has outdoor pools, a swimming pool, a children’s area, stone-spring pools, a spa hall with bubble jets and high-pressure water massage, and a fish-therapy pool. Free parking and Wi-Fi are included, and the nearby Jiaomei Wanda mall gives you somewhere to wander in the evening.

The trade-off is the setting: this is a small-town hot-spring hotel, not a resort — the railway runs nearby and the area around the hotel is quiet, so come for the springs themselves rather than the neighbourhood.
