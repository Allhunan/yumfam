# Zhangzhou Duoleng Hot Spring Hotel — 1 Night + Breakfast + Unlimited Hot Springs from ¥299 (≈$42)

- Published: 2026-11-21

**Deal price: deluxe room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + unlimited hot-spring park for 2 for the whole stay — ¥299** (≈$42, was ¥999, about 30% of list) · Valid Nov 19, 2026 – Jun 30, 2027, any day of the week · No top-up on weekdays or weekends

Duoleng sits in Jiaomei, just across the water from Xiamen — close enough that metro Line 6 gets you there — and the deal is unusually simple for a hot-spring stay: one price covers the room, breakfast for two, and unlimited entry to the hot-spring park for the whole stay, with no weekend top-up at all. The park has outdoor pools, a swimming pool, a children's area, stone-spring pools, a spa hall with bubble jets and high-pressure water massage, and a fish-therapy pool. Free parking and Wi-Fi are included, and the nearby Jiaomei Wanda mall gives you somewhere to wander in the evening.

The trade-off is the setting: this is a small-town hot-spring hotel, not a resort — the railway runs nearby and the area around the hotel is quiet, so come for the springs themselves rather than the neighbourhood.

**Packages**

- Deluxe room (king/twin) for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + unlimited hot-spring park for 2 for the whole stay — **¥299** (≈$42, was ¥999): 1 night in a deluxe room, breakfast for 2, unlimited hot-spring park entry for 2 during the stay, free parking and Wi-Fi

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 19, 2026 – Jun 30, 2027, any day of the week. No top-up on weekdays or weekends; public holidays are charged extra per the hotel.
- Book by 23:59 on the day you stay. Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 the next day.
- Each room can bring 2 children under 1.2 m free for both the hot springs and breakfast. Taller children pay at the door: springs ¥38 (1.2–1.5 m) / ¥50 (1.5 m and over), breakfast ¥34 / ¥38.
- Room upgrades at the door: business hot-spring room +¥100 per night, deluxe hot-spring room +¥170 per night.
- Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book. Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded and auto-refund on expiry; once booked the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable.
