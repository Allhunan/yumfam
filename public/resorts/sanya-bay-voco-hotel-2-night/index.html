The voco Sanya Bay is IHG’s lifestyle brand on the Coconut Dream Corridor — the golden beachfront strip of Sanya Bay. Step out of the hotel and you are about 50 metres from the sand and the sea; the airport is roughly 15 minutes away and the high-speed rail station about 20, and Tianya Haijiao, West Island, Nanshan Temple, Dadonghai and Luhuitou are all easy day trips. The hotel has 233 rooms, each with a smart toilet and Elemis toiletries, and its all-day restaurant serves buffet breakfast and dinner plus Mediterranean, Spanish and Cantonese dishes and local Hainan flavours.

Every tier includes daily breakfast for 2 adults + 2 children, a first-day minibar, a photo shoot (5 e-photos), a shuttle to West Island and a shuttle to the cdf Sanya International Duty Free City, 10% off at the Malt Cave sea-view café-bar, a ¥30 voucher for the Sanya Eternal Love show and 20% off Yanoda (ticket + shuttle). The base 2-night tier adds a Hainan-style herbal dessert (清补凉) for two; the sea-view 2-night tier adds two cocktails at the rooftop bar; the 3-night sea-view tier adds a 糟粕醋 buffet dinner for two (swapped for a set menu on Dec 24, Dec 31 and Feb 5–12). The family room tier adds beach toys, a kids’ welcome treat and one airport pickup or drop-off; the suite adds a welcome fruit platter and a romantic Western set dinner for two; the duplex family suite adds the 糟粕醋 buffet dinner for 2 adults + 2 children, kids’ toiletries and beach toys.

Good to know

  • Check-in 16:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 22:00 on the day you want to use the voucher; rooms are first-come, first-served, so book early.
  • Surcharges apply on peak dates: about ¥80/night in November and ¥200/night in December, rising to ¥400 over New Year and up to ¥1,600/night over Chinese New Year (Feb 6–10, 2027) — the exact top-up shows at booking.
  • Unbooked and unused vouchers are refundable, and expire automatically with a refund after June 30, 2027. Once a booking is confirmed, the room is held for the full day and is generally non-refundable. Bring ID for every guest (children need their household registration booklet).
