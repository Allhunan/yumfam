Shili Fangfei 6-city hotel pass from ¥899 (≈$126, was ¥1,198) — 1 night at any of 6 garden resorts (Hangzhou Xixi, Huzhou, Ji'an, Ya'an hot springs, Shigatse, Huaying) with breakfast, afternoon tea and a free weekday room upgrade. Valid Sep 10, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027.

Deal price: 1 night at any of 6 resorts — ¥899 (≈$126, was ¥1,198, about 25% off) · Valid Sep 10, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027 · Book by 17:00 the day before · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

Shili Fangfei （十里芳菲） is a chain of six garden-village resorts, and this pass lets you pick any one of them for a night — or split a longer pass across different resorts on different dates. The six are Hangzhou Xixi (inside the Xixi Wetland), Huzhou (a herb-fragrance village), Ji’an (an ancient village in Jiangxi), Ya’an (a wild-rose hot-spring village — the winter pick), Shigatse in Tibet (an oxygen-supplied healing hotel) and Huaying (a mountain village in Sichuan). Every stay includes daily breakfast for two, a minibar on the first day, and a local experience at the resort you choose — a hanfu photo session and wetland boat ride in Hangzhou, a herbal-bath craft and temple walk in Huzhou, a village lantern ceremony in Ji’an, a stream hike and campfire film night in Ya’an, a scripture-copying set in Shigatse, or a private hot-spring soak in Huaying.

The ¥899 pass covers 1 night for two (breakfast, afternoon tea for two, minibar, and the resort experience), with a free room upgrade on weekdays where rooms allow — in Ya’an that can mean a private hot-spring villa. Longer options use the same pick-and-mix idea: ¥1,499 for 2 nights (was ¥2,298), ¥1,899 for 3 nights (was ¥3,399), or ¥2,999 for 2 nights in a family suite or villa with breakfast for four (was ¥3,388). The 2- and 3-night passes can be spent as consecutive nights at one resort or as one night each at different resorts on different dates.

Good to know

  • Valid Sep 10, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027, any day of the week; book by 17:00 the day before, and show your ID and booking code at check-in
  • Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; bookings are first-come-first-served, so reserve early for weekends
  • Spring Festival top-up applies Feb 4 and Feb 11 (+¥400/night) and Feb 5–10 (+¥800/night); other dates carry no top-up
  • Unbooked and unused vouchers are refundable; once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the full day and is generally non-refundable
  • Ya’an is the hot-spring stop of the six — if you only want one winter night, that is the one to pick; Hangzhou Xixi is the easiest to reach, 10 minutes from the wetland’s main entrance
