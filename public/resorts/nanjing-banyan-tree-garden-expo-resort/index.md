Banyan Tree's Nanjing resort is built into a disused quarry inside the Jiangsu Garden Expo Park at Tangshan — 115 rooms set into the cliff face above the old quarry valley, with a garden grown on the valley floor where the mine used to be. It is the most photographed resort hotel in Nanjing for a reason: the entrance sits at the top of the cliff, and the whole building reads as an extension of the rock.

Every tier includes a private hot spring in the room plus the indoor and outdoor public hot-spring pools, breakfast for two, welcome fruit and a first round of minibar soft drinks, two tickets each to the "That Is Nature" exploration park and the Yangshan Stele (Ming Culture Village) scenic area, a 60-minute photo shoot (5 digital photos), morning wellness sessions and children's craft activities, and access to the kids' club, pool, gym and yoga room. The dining voucher works at the Chinese restaurant, the Western restaurant and the lobby lounge (the ¥200 and ¥500 vouchers also cover the spa).

**Good to know**

- Valid Oct 15, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027, any day of the week. Book at least 2 days ahead for the ¥3,188 tier (by 17:00), at least 1 day ahead for the ¥3,999 tier; the suite tier can be booked the same day.
