Deal price: 2 nights for ¥699 (≈$97) · 1 night with breakfast + afternoon tea ¥399 (≈$55) · Valid through Feb 28, 2027 · No surcharge on weekends or public holidays

The Four Points by Sheraton Shanghai Qingpu New Town (Building A, Rongtai Zhihui Tiandi, Lane 100 Huake East Road) opened in September 2026 near Hongqiao station and the National Exhibition Center, and its grand-opening bundles run all the way to February 28, 2027 — valid every day of the week with no surcharge on weekends, Mid-Autumn, National Day, New Year or Spring Festival. The 149 rooms (30–39 m² superior rooms, 39 m² family-themed rooms, 56 m² business suites) pair a retro-industrial look with the brand’s signature Comfort Bed, a 65-inch TV and a standalone shower. On the 5th floor, The Mesh combines the all-day restaurant, lobby lounge and beer bar — breakfast runs 7:00–9:30, and evenings bring the brand’s Best Brews craft-beer program. There’s also a 24-hour gym, a self-service laundry, free parking and a big free slide on the ground floor.

The family hook is next door in the same complex: Guji Planet kids’ park on the 3rd floor — about 1,000 m² with a 7.5-metre starry dome, a ball pit, sand pool, a 6-metre devil slide, trampolines and a high-ropes challenge zone for ages 6–12. The two family-friendly tiers below include a 1-adult + 1-child park pass (¥128 value; collect it at the front desk, valid during your stay). The included afternoon tea is a proper spread, too: mini sandwiches and savory tarts, red velvet and tiramisu cakes, chocolate mousse cups, cranberry scones with jam and cream, seasonal fruit, and English black or green tea.

⚠️ Good to know: reserve by 19:00 the day before at the latest (check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00). Blackout dates during major exhibitions: Nov 3–8 and Dec 1–4, 2026. Every guest needs their own original ID — adults an ID card, children (infants included) the household register; driving licences aren’t accepted. Unreserved vouchers are refundable; once a booking is confirmed it’s non-refundable in principle.
