A light-luxury cultural resort run by the Delong hotel group in the foothills of Lingshan Mountain — “the 33rd blessed land” — about 20 minutes from Shangrao railway station and 12 km from Lingshan scenic area. More than twenty restored Huizhou heritage houses, a traditional tea-oil workshop and classical Chinese gardens are woven into 40,900㎡ of landscaped grounds, so the whole stay feels like stepping into a quiet Jiangnan painting. Shangrao itself sits where Jiangxi, Zhejiang, Fujian and Anhui meet, an easy high-speed-rail hop from Shanghai, Hangzhou, Xiamen or Fuzhou — and a handy base for Lingshan, Wangxian Valley and Gexian Village.

Every package includes breakfast, free gym, parking and Wi-Fi, plus one hanfu dress-up experience — dress up in the old courtyard and take your own period-drama photos among white walls, black tiles and red lanterns. Rooms run 40–58㎡: the Guantang rooms (40–45㎡, 2 m king or twin 1.35 m beds) on floors 1–3, and the Boya mountain-view or courtyard rooms (58㎡) for a bigger, more private feel.

⚠️ Booking & extras: Reserve by 23:59 the day before use; refundable any time before you reserve, and auto-refunded if unused past expiry. Lingshan tickets and the Wuyi Mountain train ticket must be booked a day ahead with names and ID numbers. Breakfast surcharges apply on site for children over 1.2 m.
