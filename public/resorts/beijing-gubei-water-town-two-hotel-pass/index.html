Gubei Water Town is the canal town under the Simatai Great Wall, about two hours from central Beijing — and this pass is the flexible way to stay there: one voucher works at either of two hotels inside the town, the Gubei Zhiguang or the Hangu, so you pick whichever has rooms on your dates. The price is built for three adults, which makes it a natural fit for two parents and a grandparent, or two adults and an older child.

Both hotels sit inside the scenic area, so you get multiple re-entries to the town and unlimited rides on the shuttle bus while you stay, plus the hotel pool and gym. The hot-spring tickets cover the town’s three bathhouses (Yudao, Chengshang and Yuda — whichever is open that day).

Packages

  • 1 night, two-hotel pass — ¥999 (≈$139, was ¥1,758): family room A at Gubei Zhiguang or senior suite at Hangu, breakfast ×3, Gubei Water Town tickets ×3, hot-spring tickets ×3, and one of: go-kart ride for 1 adult + 1 child, afternoon tea at Gubei Zhiguang, or afternoon tea at Hangu’s Weiyuan restaurant
  • 2 nights, two-hotel pass — ¥1,599 (≈$222, was ¥3,288): the same room for 2 consecutive nights (can’t be split), breakfast ×3, town tickets ×3, hot-spring tickets ×3, and a travel-pass book or a plaque fridge magnet

Good to know

  • The price is calculated for 3 adults; children 1.2–1.5 m pay a supplement based on the room and activities.
  • Saturday top-up is ¥550 per night on the 1-night package (Oct 2026 – Jun 2027) and ¥750 on Friday–Saturday nights in July 2027; on the 2-night package it is ¥900 per night, including July. Holiday top-ups apply: New Year (Dec 31 – Jan 2, ¥550–¥1,200), Spring Festival (Feb 5 – 11, ¥550–¥1,200), Qingming (Apr 3–4, ¥1,000–¥1,200), May Day (May 1 – 4, ¥550–¥1,200) and Dragon Boat Festival (Jun 8, ¥1,000–¥1,200) — the exact figure depends on the package and the date, so check the top-up page before you book.
  • Book at least 2 days ahead (by 23:59 two days before) — rooms are first-come, first-served, so book early for weekends and holidays.
  • Check-in is 15:00, check-out 12:00 the next day. Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
  • The go-kart ride is for children under 1.3 m riding with an adult. Bring your own swimwear and swim cap for the hot springs, or buy them on site.
  • Once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable; unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded.
