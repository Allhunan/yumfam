Hehui sits out by Baishuizhai in Zengcheng, in the forested hills north-east of Guangzhou, and the pitch is simple: every room has its own hot-spring pool on the balcony (two fills of water included), and the price covers the whole family — 2 adults + 2 kids under 1.2 m — for breakfast, the public hot springs, the infinity pool and the water playground. The public springs run a five-element medicinal menu with dry sauna, herbal steam and a Himalayan salt-steam session, and the hotel throws in a kids’ club with one hosted session, a squirrel park with pet interaction, and free parking and Wi-Fi.

The ¥699 tier is breakfast-only; the ¥899 tier adds the dinner buffet for 2 + 2 (if the buffet is not running that night, a ¥360 Chinese-restaurant voucher is given instead). The ¥1,299 buy-1-get-1 tier is the value pick for a longer stay — two rooms for one night or one room for two nights, with breakfast each night. The ¥1,099 family room is a themed kids’ room (a “Bugs Life” parent-child room, or an Ultraman room if that one is full).
