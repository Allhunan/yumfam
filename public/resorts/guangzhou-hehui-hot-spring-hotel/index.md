# Guangzhou Hehui Hot Spring Hotel — Pool Room for 2 Adults + 2 Kids from ¥699 (≈$97)

**Deal price: advanced pool room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids + unlimited hot springs + infinity pool — ¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,288) · With dinner buffet for 2 + 2 **¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,488) · Buy-1-get-1 (2 rooms or 2 nights) **¥1,299** (≈$180, was ¥2,188) · Family theme room with dinner **¥1,099** (≈$153, was ¥1,788) · Valid Nov 22, 2026 – Apr 29, 2027

Hehui sits out by Baishuizhai in Zengcheng, in the forested hills north-east of Guangzhou, and the pitch is simple: every room has its own hot-spring pool on the balcony (two fills of water included), and the price covers the whole family — 2 adults + 2 kids under 1.2 m — for breakfast, the public hot springs, the infinity pool and the water playground. The public springs run a five-element medicinal menu with dry sauna, herbal steam and a Himalayan salt-steam session, and the hotel throws in a kids' club with one hosted session, a squirrel park with pet interaction, and free parking and Wi-Fi.

The ¥699 tier is breakfast-only; the ¥899 tier adds the dinner buffet for 2 + 2 (if the buffet is not running that night, a ¥360 Chinese-restaurant voucher is given instead). The ¥1,299 buy-1-get-1 tier is the value pick for a longer stay — two rooms for one night or one room for two nights, with breakfast each night. The ¥1,099 family room is a themed kids' room (a "Bugs Life" parent-child room, or an Ultraman room if that one is full).

**Packages**

- Advanced pool room + breakfast + hot springs + infinity pool — **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,288): pool room for 1 night, private balcony pool (2 fills), breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids, unlimited five-element medicinal hot springs, dry sauna, herbal steam, 1 Himalayan salt-steam session, infinity pool, water playground and fitness centre, kids' club + 1 hosted session, squirrel park
- Advanced pool room + breakfast & dinner buffet + hot springs + infinity pool — **¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,488): everything above plus a dinner buffet for 2 adults + 2 kids (or a ¥360 Chinese-restaurant voucher if the buffet is not running)
- Advanced pool room, buy-1-get-1 + breakfast + hot springs + infinity pool — **¥1,299** (≈$180, was ¥2,188): 2 rooms for 1 night or 1 room for 2 nights, breakfast for 2 + 2 each night, same hot-spring and pool access
- Family theme room + breakfast & dinner buffet + hot springs + infinity pool — **¥1,099** (≈$153, was ¥1,788): themed parent-child room for 1 night, private balcony pool, breakfast and dinner buffet for 2 + 2, same hot-spring and pool access

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 22, 2026 – Apr 29, 2027, any day of the week. Blackout dates: Feb 5–12 and Apr 3–5, 2027.
- Book by 16:00 on the day you stay. Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00 the next day.
- Top-ups (Nov–Feb): Friday +¥100 per room per night, Saturday +¥300; New Year Dec 31 +¥300, Jan 1–2 +¥600. Mar–Apr: Saturday +¥200. Upgrading to a deluxe pool room costs +¥100 per night.
- Children under 1.2 m are free throughout (meals, springs and pools). Older children pay on site: breakfast ¥58 (1.2–1.5 m) / ¥108 (over 1.5 m), dinner ¥98 / ¥198, hot springs ¥48 / ¥108.
- Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book. Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded; once booked the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable.
- The private pool includes two fills of water; extra fills are charged by the hotel.
