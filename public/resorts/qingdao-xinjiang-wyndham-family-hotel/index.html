A family-friendly Wyndham in Qingdao’s Chengyang district with a genuinely unusual perk: a 50,000㎡ European-style garden behind the hotel, complete with fountains, courtyards, ponds and a pet area where kids can feed the animals — the listing’s own pitch is that a walk, a play session and a photo stop are all sorted in one garden. Inside, the deal covers breakfast for two, two vouchers for the neighbouring hot spring (third-party operated, 12 hours per visit), an hour of ball sports (tennis, billiards or table tennis, pick one), the first day’s minibar free, and unlimited use of the indoor and outdoor kids’ parks, gym and pool during your stay.

Two upgrades on the same room: add vegetable picking at the nearby Fahai Temple eco garden for ¥459 (≈$64, was ¥1,099 — this tier alone ends November 30, 2026), or add a two-person buffet dinner at Shuiqing Café for ¥699 (≈$97, was ¥1,299 — this tier ends December 30, 2026 and is blacked out December 24–25). Room upgrades are available online: +¥50 per night for a family king or family twin room, +¥500 per night for a space-themed supreme family room. Free timed shuttles run to Jiaodong Airport (book 4 hours ahead), Qingdao North Railway Station (2 hours ahead) and Metro Line 1’s Xianjiazhai station (30 minutes ahead).

Practical notes: check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Book by 19:00 the same day; rooms are first-come, first-served, and unreserved vouchers can be refunded. Children under 1.2 m eat breakfast free; 1.2–1.5 m is ¥40, over 1.5 m is ¥88. Small dogs under 8 kg are welcome; aggressive breeds are not accepted. Hot spring note: children under 1 m enter free, 1–1.4 m pay a child rate, over 1.4 m pay adult rate. Address: Xinjiang Wyndham Hotel, 220 Heilongjiang Middle Road, Chengyang District, Qingdao, Shandong.
