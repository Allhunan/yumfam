**Tangcheng Yupin Hot Spring Hotel （永春汤城御品温泉酒店）** is the "little Bali" an hour or so from Xiamen — a hot-spring resort in Yongchun, Quanzhou where the spring water is piped into the rooms themselves. Every room in this deal comes with its **own balcony hot-spring pool**, so you can soak in private while looking out over the hills, then head down to the **hot-spring park with 20+ garden pools** — flower-petal pools, a mahjong pool, private nooks — as many times as you like during your stay. There is also an infinity pool, a spa area that the hotel pitches as the cure for a stiff office back, and a kids' water park that takes over in the afternoons.

The rooms start at 55㎡ with a butler-style welcome, simple uncluttered decor, and a dry-wet separated bathroom stocked with the hotel's own toiletries. Pick from three room types at the base price; a suite with a big terrace and twin private pools is available as a paid upgrade.

**Packages**
- **Hot-spring room, 1 night — ¥428** (≈$59, was ¥738): choice of 3 rooms, all with a balcony private pool — Superior twin (2 × 1.2 m beds), Deluxe king (1.8 m bed), or Premium family room (1.8 m bed + 1.2 m bed); breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.2 m); unlimited hot-spring park access; free hotel shuttle and free parking

**Good to know**
- Valid Sep 23 – Dec 31, 2026, Monday to Sunday; book by 23:59 the day before and show your ID at check-in (check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00)
- Friday and Saturday stays carry a ¥100 per-package top-up; upgrades to the private-pool family room (+¥270, or +¥370 on Fri/Sat) or the family suite (+¥460, or +¥560 on Fri/Sat) are available
- Children 1.2–1.4 m pay top-ups on site (breakfast ¥39, hot-spring ticket ¥49 weekdays / ¥79–89); over 1.4 m counts as an adult
- Address: No. 759 Tangcheng Village, Penghu Town, Yongchun County, Quanzhou — about 15 minutes from the train station; nearby day trips include 4A-rated Jiuxian Mountain and Baizhang Rock
