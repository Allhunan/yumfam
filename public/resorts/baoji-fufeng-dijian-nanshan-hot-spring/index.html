Fufeng Dijian Nanshan Hot Spring Hotel （扶风地建南山温泉酒店） is a slow-travel, soak-and-stay deal in Baoji, Shaanxi — about 1.1 km from the 5A-rated Famen Temple, home of the famous finger-bone relic of the Buddha. The package covers 5 nights at either of two sister hotels (the Fufeng Dijian Nanshan Hot Spring Hotel by the Famen cultural scenic area, or the Dijian Nanshan Qianhe Hotel), and the nights can be split however you like within the validity period: 1 room for 5 nights, 5 rooms for 1 night, or any combination in between.

The draw is the water: the hotel’s 500㎡ hot-spring area draws natural spring water from 1,800 m underground at 58°C. The package includes 10 hot-spring entries on top of the 5 nights, plus 10 breakfasts and 2 camping spots on the hotel’s 1,000㎡ camping ground (room for 300 people, tents under the stars). The hotel itself has 212 rooms — luxury suites, business twins and deluxe kings — with high-speed Wi-Fi, a mini bar, safe and satellite TV, plus a restaurant serving local Shaanxi dishes alongside international food.

Good to know

  • Valid Jul 20 – Dec 31, 2026; book by 23:59 the day before; show your ID at the hot-spring front desk to check in
  • The 5 nights must be used within the validity period and cannot be partially refunded — if you plan to split them across separate trips, book them as separate orders
  • Two children under 1.2 m stay free; public holidays may carry a top-up — confirm the price when booking
  • Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00; the hotel also has meeting rooms and a restaurant
  • Address: No. 4 Damei Fotang City, Meiyang Avenue North, Famen Town, Fufeng County, Baoji — about 1.1 km from Famen Temple and 1.2 km from the Famen Temple Buddhist cultural scenic area
