Changtai Shanwai Yueju Villa from ¥289 (≈$40, was ¥558): a 1-night two-bedroom family suite with breakfast, unlimited kids' park play and hot spring park tickets, in the Mayangxi eco resort area about 42 km from Xiamen island — valid October 21 to December 25, 2026, refundable if unreserved.

Deal price: 1 night, Moyun two-bedroom family suite — ¥289 (≈$40, was ¥558) · Breakfast · Unlimited Luyuechuan kids’ park play · 1 adult hot spring park ticket (each adult ticket admits 1 child under 1.2 m free) · Valid October 21 – December 25, 2026 · ⏰ refundable if unreserved · Book by 23:59 the day before

A quiet, Chinese-style villa retreat in Changtai’s Mayangxi eco tourism area, about 42 km from Xiamen island and 2 km from Tianzhu Mountain National Forest Park — close enough for a weekend escape, far enough to feel like one. The resort grounds cover roughly 110,000㎡ of gardens modelled on Suzhou’s Lingering Garden, with pavilions, covered walkways and streams. The headline Moyun suite is 89㎡ with a 1.8 m double bed plus a 1.2 m tatami bed, and the deal bundles breakfast, unlimited play in the Luyuechuan kids’ park, and a hot spring park ticket — plus a ¥10 single-ride go-kart coupon usable multiple times during the stay.

The same sale scales up for bigger groups: a three-bedroom view suite for one night is ¥429 (≈$60, was ¥838) with 2 hot spring tickets and breakfast for 4; the two-bedroom suite for two nights (or one night in two rooms) is ¥559 (≈$78, was ¥1,116); the three-bedroom suite for two nights is ¥799 (≈$111, was ¥1,676); and a three-bedroom detached villa with a mahjong table is ¥1,099 (≈$153, was ¥1,978) with 3 hot spring tickets and breakfast for 4.

Practical notes: check-in from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. The hot spring park is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays. Extra hot spring tickets can be added on site (adult ¥68, child 1.2–1.4 m ¥48); extra breakfast is ¥16 per person. Book by 23:59 the day before; rooms are first-come, first-served, and unreserved vouchers can be refunded (auto-refunded after expiry). Address: Shanwai Yueju Villa, Jianfa Shanwaishan, Mayangxi Eco Tourism Resort, Changtai District, Zhangzhou, Fujian.
