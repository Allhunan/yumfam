A wetland-side base between Guangzhou and Shenzhen: the ¥399 tier buys a garden balcony room (floor-to-ceiling windows, viewing balcony, bathtub) for 1 night with breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.2 m) and a fruit-picking voucher, plus the indoor pool, a 24-hour lake-view gym, free parking and Wi-Fi. The hotel stands right on the Tonghu wetland greenway, so mornings can start with a lakeside walk before breakfast, and the surrounding flower fields — bougainvillea falls, firecracker-flower corridors, cosmos meadows — are free to wander.

Step up to ¥499 (≈$69, was ¥899) and dinner joins the deal: on Saturdays (and the listed holiday dates) the evening meal is a salmon seafood buffet for 2 adults + 2 kids, while Sundays and weekdays get a Chinese set dinner instead. Either way, weekends carry no surcharge.
