Riyuegu is Xiamen’s big Southeast-Asian-style hot-spring village out in Haicang, and this is its winter combo: a full day in the pools plus a beef hotpot meal, with the resort’s extras — a treasure hunt, a sound-bowl meditation, a game room and a very friendly Alaskan dog — thrown in. Every tier lets each paying adult bring one child under 1.5 m free, so a family of three can do the single-person set for ¥239 plus one free child, or the two-person set for two adults and two children.

The resort opens 10:00–22:00 and the hotpot is served 11:30–20:00. A free shuttle runs between the resort and Dongfu metro station, so you can get there without a car.

Packages

  • Single: hot-spring ticket + single beef hotpot + sound-bowl meditation + breathing-decompression session + game room + treasure hunt + pet playtime — ¥239 (≈$33, was ¥348); one child under 1.5 m free; Saturday–Sunday top-up ¥40 per set
  • Two-person: hot-spring tickets ×2 + double beef hotpot + the same extras — ¥399 (≈$55, was ¥686); one free child under 1.5 m per adult; Saturday–Sunday top-up ¥80 per set
  • VIP two-person (weekdays): hot-spring tickets ×2 + indoor heated water park “Kids’ Palace” ticket + VIP locker, bathrobe, unlimited towels, a swim cap, unlimited fish therapy, VIP pool and rest area, fruit at the leisure station, a ¥18 food voucher, unlimited Kids’ Palace play for one child, 1 hour of swim-gear rental and 1 hour of a private room — ¥388 (≈$54, was ¥568); Saturday–Sunday top-up ¥80 per set

Good to know

  • Bring your own swimwear, and a swim cap — caps are required in the pools and in the Huxinzhou hydrotherapy pool.
  • The sound-bowl meditation runs 15:00–15:30 and the breathing-decompression session 15:45–16:15; the treasure hunt runs 12:00–16:00 and the dog playtime 15:00–17:30 in the hot-spring area.
  • The game room on the second floor of Moonlight Pavilion has cards, chess, billiards, video games, table football, table hockey and mahjong.
  • Guests aged 70 and over need an adult with them at all times.
  • The ticket is used right after purchase — no exchange needed — and if ticketing fails the refund comes back within 3 working days.
