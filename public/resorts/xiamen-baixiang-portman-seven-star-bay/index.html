“Apart from the price, there’s nothing wrong with it” — that’s the running verdict on the Xiamen Baixiang Portman Seven Star Bay (No. 7 Guanzhou Road, Tong’an), and at these Double-11 rates the price objection mostly evaporates. The resort does palace-scale grandeur properly: European-style buildings across a buggy-connected park — the Yihai “town” blocks, plus the Xihai and Ruihai sea-view wings — Rainbow Beach at the door, indoor and outdoor pools, and a kids’ operation (playground, mini club, “Lumi Magic Academy” XR) that fills whole days.

Every tier includes buffet breakfast for two (up to two kids under 1.2 m free), the first minibar round daily, Seven Star Park playground access (2 kids under 12 + 1 adult), 2 mini-club activity tickets, a Fenghe photo shoot (book 2+ days ahead), and gym/swim-club access. The favorite is the 2-night Yihai bundle with a weekday buffet dinner for two; food tiers add a lobster candlelight dinner at Haixing No.7, or the Tong’an ginger-sesame fried-crab set at Xiyuehui.

⚠️ “No weekend surcharge” has peak fine print: standard weekends are flat, but on-site surcharges apply per room-night on Dec 31 (+¥200), Jan 1–2 (+¥100) and across Spring Festival, Feb 5–12 (+¥500 to +¥900). The buffet dinner in the ¥1,299/¥1,999 tiers is a weekday dinner (17:30–21:00) — weekend/holiday sittings cost extra, and holiday blackouts apply to the lobster/crab dinners. Book by 23:59 on the day of use.
