If Beijing’s winter weekends feel too familiar, this is the escape 1.5 hours south: the Huamei Shengdi resort area in Wen’an, a spread of 10,000 mu of forest and 1,000 mu of lakes with a ski resort (opened November 14, 2026), a tractor park, go-karts and a New Year fireworks season. The hotel at its heart is a Hilton Curio Collection property built around wine culture — a European-castle exterior, a lobby that greets you with red-wine cocktails, and 266 rooms in a clean modern style, some with lake views.

Packages — breakfast is for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.2 m

  • Lakeside room + afternoon tea, 1 night — ¥358 (≈$50, was ¥699): 1 night in a lakeside king or twin room, breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child, afternoon tea for one, plus the gym, the tractor park, weekend kids’ activities and the resort shuttle train. Free parking and Wi-Fi.
  • 3-night special, 3 rooms or 3 nights — ¥999 (≈$139, was ¥1,299): 3 nights in a selected king or twin room — book as 3 rooms for 1 night or 1 room for 3 nights — with breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child each night, plus the gym, tractor park, weekend kids’ activities and shuttle train.

The resort’s family play list is the reason to come with kids: go-karts and ATVs, laser shooting and archery, retro kids’ cars, a little kitchen where kids can cook, the park train, a sports field and a real-life “chicken dinner” game. Weekends bring organised hotel kids’ activities, and the fireworks season runs over New Year. The hotel also has Chinese and Western restaurants, a gym and a swimming pool, a lobby bar and meeting rooms.

Good to know: the hotel is in the Luneng Eco Zone, Wen’an County, Langfang, Hebei — about 1.5 hours’ drive from Beijing. Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 23:00 the day before; unbooked orders are refundable. Bring ID for every guest. The lakeside 1-night package is valid only through Dec 31, 2026; the 3-night package runs through Apr 29, 2027. Children under 1.1 m eat breakfast free, 1.1–1.4 m half price, 1.4 m and over at the adult rate. Some outdoor activities pause in bad weather — check the resort notice board on the day.
