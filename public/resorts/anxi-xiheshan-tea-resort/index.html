Deal price: 1 night in a balcony room + breakfasts for 2 adults & 2 kids + park admission — from ¥299 (≈$42) (was ¥999, about 30% of list) · Bookable Oct 29 – Dec 31, 2026 · Weekends at no surcharge · ⏰ valid through Dec 31, 2026

Xihe Mountain （溪禾山） sits in Anxi County, the home of Tieguanyin oolong, and the resort lies inside a 4A-rated tea-culture park — the same tea-country scenery used as a music-video backdrop. Late October is cockscomb-flower season in the park, and the stay bundles the room with the whole estate: breakfasts for 2 adults and 2 kids (kids under 1.2 m), park admission for the same 2 adults and 2 kids, free indoor playground for the children and free parking. Between outings, the resort’s tea rooms keep the pace slow — guests praise the on-site afternoon tea, where a pot runs as little as ¥30 for three infusions — and on rainy days the covered terrace keeps the conversation going.

⚠️ Good to know: Reserve by 23:59 the day before use; check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00. Refundable if unreserved, and expired orders auto-refund. Breakfast surcharges apply on site for taller kids (1.2–1.4 m: ¥19; over 1.4 m: ¥38). An optional autumn-tea study tour can be booked separately on site. Book early — rooms are first-come, first-served after purchase.
