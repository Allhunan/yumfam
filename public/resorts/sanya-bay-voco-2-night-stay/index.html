voco Sanya Bay — the IHG brand’s easygoing beachfront address on the Coconut Dream Corridor — sits about 50 metres from the sand: roughly 15 minutes from Phoenix Airport and 20 minutes from the high-speed-rail station, with Tianya Haijiao, West Island, Nanshan Temple, Dadonghai and Luhuitou all within an easy drive. The hotel has 233 rooms and suites, every one with a smart toilet, Elemis toiletries and a minibar, plus a two-floor, island-adventure kids’ club in Building 3 (slides, trampolines, arcade games, a ball pit), a 17th-floor pool bar and gym facing the bay, and the Lanting all-day restaurant serving Mediterranean, Spanish, Cantonese and Hainanese dishes alongside the breakfast buffet.

Every tier here covers breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids (children under 1.4 m eat free), the first-day minibar, a photo shoot with 5 free digital photos, and two shuttle buses — the hotel↔West Island round trip and the cdf Sanya duty-free mall shuttle — plus a 10% discount at the cave-style sea-view café bar, a ¥30 Sanya Romance Park voucher and 20% off Yanoda tickets. Higher tiers swap in sea views, set dinners or the family bunk room.
