Qiyun Mountain Resort Hotel — 2 Nights + Scenic Tickets, Bamboo Raft & Night Show, from ¥399 (≈$56)

Autumn 'shaiqiu' promo at Qiyun Mountain Resort Hotel near Huangshan: rooms from ¥399 a night (2 nights for two ¥869), bundling scenic-area tickets, bamboo raft, the Freehome family campsite, heritage craft sessions, hanfu dress-up and the Baiyue night shows — no weekend surcharge, free room upgrade on non-holidays.

Qiyun Mountain （齐云山） in Xiuning County, Huangshan, is one of China’s four great Taoist mountains, and the Qiyun Mountain Resort Hotel sits inside the scenic area in Yanqian Village — you sleep in the attraction rather than commuting to it. It’s autumn “shaiqiu” （晒秋， the harvest sun-drying season), peak sea-of-clouds time, and the packages bundle the room with the whole mountain: scenic-area admission, a bamboo raft ride on the Heng River, Xiaoyao Street entry, and the Freehome family campsite — 10 hectares of petting zoo, rope nets, sand treasure hunts, a forest maze, mini golf and archery. Add unlimited sports-centre use and the evening “Baiyue Night” shows — golden-dragon river show, molten-iron fireworks （打铁花）, fish-lantern parade, fireworks — and the days fill themselves.

The cultural layer is hands-on: mid and upper tiers add heritage craft sessions (papermaking, paper-cutting, iron painting, lacquer fans), every tier includes hanfu dress-up, and each room can bring one child under 1.2m free.

⚠️ Booking & upgrades: reserve by 20:00 on the day of use at the latest, and check-in is 14:00 / check-out 12:00. The headline perk — a free room upgrade on non-holiday stays — only works if you note your preferred upgrade in the booking remark; it’s subject to the day’s availability, not guaranteed. Weekends genuinely carry no surcharge; the only uplifts on the books were National Day dates, now past.
