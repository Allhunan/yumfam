Taihu Longemont （太湖龙之梦） in Changxing, Huzhou is a small city of a resort — zoo, ice-and-snow world, circus, ancient town and water shows around Taihu Lake — and this voucher is its most flexible way in. You choose your hotel from three: the Animal World Hotel (Y-shaped, animal-themed rooms on floors 3–26, breakfast in the 27th-floor restaurant, about 7 minutes’ walk from the zoo and snow world), the Diamond Hotel (2,000 rooms, a tiger garden on the ground floor, nicknamed the “little Iceland” of the Yangtze Delta), or the Ancient Town Hotel (3,000+ Chinese-style rooms, penguins paddling in a lobby display, front-row views of the ancient town’s fireworks, 5 minutes’ walk to the town). Every tier includes daily buffet breakfast for two and a pair of park tickets — details below.
