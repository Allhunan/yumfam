About 45 minutes from Xiamen island, in the hills of Xiang’an, Sujia Puyue is a Suzhou-garden-style villa park of detached three-bedroom houses, each with its own courtyard, living room, kitchen and private hot-spring plunge pool. One floor up, bedrooms sleep the whole group; downstairs, the villa’s entertainment room is either a mahjong parlour or a KTV room (the ¥678 luxury tier includes both), and a barbecue grill plate is free to use (bring your own ingredients). The park sits inside a landscaped estate with mountain trails behind it, and the 4A Beichen Mountain, Tong’an film studio and Fantawild are all nearby.

⚠️ Good to know: Breakfast is NOT included in these tiers — the included meal is an afternoon-tea set. A ¥500 deposit is collected on site per villa and returned at check-out. Saturday stays add ¥200 per villa-night; New Year (Dec 31–Jan 2) adds ¥600. The local specialty gift must be collected at the farm, and the farm-activity 10%-off voucher applies to the Sanwuqiu countryside experiences. Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Free WiFi and parking; hanfu dress-up is included. Address: 2-4 Xinzhen Road, Xiang’an District, Xiamen.
