Shanghai Haichang Ocean Park Resort Hotel is the stay-inside-the-park option at Haichang Ocean Park in Lingang, Pudong: you sleep steps from the gates, enter through a dedicated VIP channel, and get two days of unlimited park entry for two adults (family tiers add one child under 1.4 m). The headline tier adds breakfast for two the next morning, free parking and Wi-Fi, and the current Ocean Wonders Festival (魔都海洋万秀节, through Nov 15) — two brand-new haunted houses, a new Arabian-style riverside street, 150 roaming performers, a night-time orca show and a mermaid light show, plus six refreshed big ocean shows and 100,000+ marine animals across the park.

If you have an Ultraman fan in the family, there is a dedicated Ultraman-themed hotel wing with themed family rooms (¥1,499 for one night). The regular family room (¥1,199 for one night) sleeps two adults and one child with the same unlimited tickets and breakfast. Two-night tiers drop the breakfast but add a second day of park time at a lower per-night rate.
