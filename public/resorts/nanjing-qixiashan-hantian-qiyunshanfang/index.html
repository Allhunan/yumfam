Nanjing Qixia Mountain Hantian Qiyunshanfang Hotel — Zen Stay + Maple Mountain Tickets, ¥488 (≈$69)

Hantian Qiyunshanfang Hotel at the foot of Nanjing's Qixia Mountain: a Chinese Zen-style stay with breakfasts, vegetarian noodles, Qixia Mountain tickets and a choice of maple-scroll, incense or singing-bowl experience — from ¥488 (about half list price), valid all the way to June 2027.

Deal price: 1 night + breakfasts + vegetarian noodles + 2 Qixia Mountain tickets + Zen experience — from ¥488 (≈$69) (was ¥999, about 49% of list) · Autumn maple season + valid to Jun 2027 · ⏰ valid through Jun 30, 2027

“One Qixia Mountain, half the history of Jinling” — and this hotel sits right at its foot, next to the famous Qixia Temple, one of China’s four great temples. Hantian Qiyunshanfang is Chinese Zen through and through: the same flying-eaves rooflines as the temple, symmetrical courtyards, white space and water features, and a whole Heart Sutra wall greeting you at check-in. Every corner is photogenic, and the stillness is the point — a place to let a city-frazzled mind settle.

Your bundle: 1 night in a 28–30㎡ Zen room with breakfasts for 2, the hotel’s signature vegetarian noodles for 2, 2 Qixia Mountain tickets (perfect for maple season — though the hotel notes maple color depends on weather, so treat the red leaves as a bonus, not a promise), and one Zen experience of your choice: a maple-viewing scroll session, an incense ritual, or a singing-bowl session. The larger Wenchan garden-view rooms (70–75㎡) add a photo shoot.

⚠️ Booking & extras: Breakfast is served 7:00–10:00. Kids’ breakfast: free under 1.2 m, ¥44 for 1.2–1.4 m, ¥88 above 1.4 m. Zen rooms have a baby crib but no extra bed, and twin beds cannot be joined. The 2-night tiers can be split as 1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night. The hotel’s photos are file images for reference; the real maple display depends on the day’s weather.
