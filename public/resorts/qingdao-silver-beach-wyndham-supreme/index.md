**Qingdao Silver Beach Wyndham Supreme Hotel （青岛银沙滩温德姆至尊酒店）** is a seaside stay where the beach is genuinely part of the hotel: a VIP walkway gets you to **Silver Beach in 2 minutes**, Golden Beach is a 5-minute drive, and Tangdaowan Park is across the street. Every room has a private balcony — the 40㎡ sea-view rooms look out over the bay — and the hotel was designed with ocean touches throughout, from the high-ceilinged lobby to the in-house Bavarian-style beer house.

The **¥549 package** covers 1 night in a 40㎡ sea-view king or twin room, a buffet breakfast for two, and a **dinner buffet or Chinese set for two** (the buffet runs Friday and Saturday; on other days you get the Chinese set instead — tomato-braised beef, crispy pork, seasonal vegetables, egg fried rice and fruit). The kids' club, gym and indoor pool are free during your stay, as is parking. Two more tiers run on the same base: **¥899** for 2 nights with one dinner included (was ¥1,598), and **¥799** for 2 nights room-only with daily breakfast (was ¥1,798).

**Good to know**
- Valid Oct 20, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027; book by 18:00 on the day of use; show the booking code, phone number and ID at check-in
- No top-up on New Year's Eve, New Year or Spring Festival; the dinner buffet is served Friday–Saturday only — other days you get the two-person Chinese set at the Datang Palace restaurant (lunch 11:00–14:00, dinner 17:00–21:00)
- Breakfast: kids under 1.2 m free, 1.2–1.4 m ¥49, 1.4 m and above ¥89; the outdoor pool is closed in winter
- Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00; nearby: Tangdaowan Park across the street, the Chinese Courtyard garden, Qingdao Forest Wildlife World and the 68-metre Qindao Eye Ferris wheel
- Address: No. 178 Yinshatan Road, Huangdao District, Qingdao — about 22 km from Qingdao station, 65 km from Qingdao Jiaodong International Airport
