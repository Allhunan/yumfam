A Taihu Lake family stay with the season’s signature extra: Golden Riverside Hotel sits on Huantaihu Avenue in Suzhou’s Taihu Lake resort area, and the headline tier pairs a garden-view room with a pair of hairy crabs — autumn’s Suzhou delicacy, with the cooking fee waived — plus a ¥100 dining voucher. Children are well covered without leaving the hotel: the kids’ park, kids’ cinema, games room and DIY baking sessions are all included, and there’s an indoor heated pool and gym for adults. Breakfast is for two adults; children under 1.4 m eat free, older children pay the full ¥88 breakfast price.

The same room for two nights is ¥698 (≈$97, was ¥1,198) with the crab pair and dining voucher. If you’d rather skip the crab, the noodle-or-tea tiers swap it for two bowls of Suzhou-style noodles (braised pork or mushroom) or a two-person afternoon tea: ¥466 (≈$65, was ¥699) for one night, ¥666 (≈$93, was ¥1,398) for two nights. Note the split validity: the crab tiers run through December 29, 2026, while the noodle/tea tiers run through December 31, 2026. Check-in is from 14:00, check-out by 12:00. Room upgrades are available at the front desk: lake-view or family rooms +¥200, 3D sleep-themed rooms +¥300.

Practical notes: book by 20:00 the day before your stay; rooms are first-come, first-served after booking, and a confirmed reservation is generally non-refundable while unreserved vouchers can be refunded. Address: Golden Riverside Hotel, 5888 Huantaihu Avenue, Wuzhong District, Suzhou, Jiangsu.
