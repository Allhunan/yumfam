Shenzhen’s east coast is the city’s answer to a seaside weekend — and this is the most theatrical place to stay on it. The Luwan International is a French-chateau resort set between the hills and the sea in the Meisha Bay resort area: three sides mountains, one side ocean, about 800 metres from the water. With Metro Line 8’s phase 3 now open, you can genuinely take the metro to the beach — the hotel is a 10-minute walk from Xiyong station’s Exit C — and the coastal boardwalk, the sea-view road and the beaches are all within a short drive.

Deal price: mountain-view room, 1 night — ¥399 (≈$55, was ¥1,288): a mountain-view king (1.8 m bed) or twin (two 1.2 m beds) room for 1 night, with unlimited 3D cinema, the sea-view splash pool (about 500 sqm, 80 cm deep — seasonal, closes after the National Day holiday, so check before you go), the kids’ playground, free parking and free Wi-Fi. Breakfast is not included (adults ¥58, kids 1.2–1.4 m ¥38 at the hotel). Weekends, Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day carry no surcharge.

The hotel has 309 rooms in Mediterranean and tropical styles, plus a chess-and-cards room and KTV (both charged). The real draw is the location: Xiyong beach is a 5-minute drive (about ¥20 per person, parking extra) — a gentle, shell-strewn beach that’s good for kids hunting crabs and shells. The coastal boardwalk between Xiyong and Rose Coast is a 7-minute drive, Dameisha Beach and its 1,800-metre beach is about 16 minutes, the Dameisha Outlets (“Shenzhen’s Venice”, on a lake) about 14 minutes, and the Dameisha coastal art town — its houses painted with whales, sea lions and turtles — the same. Further afield: Nan’ao’s First Beach (24 minutes), the Meisha Peak hike (41 minutes) and Yangmeikeng, the Mermaid film location (38 minutes).

Good to know: the hotel is at Xiyong, Kuiyong Street, Dapeng New District (turn right after the Xiyong expressway exit, 200 metres on). Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 17:00 on the day of use; unbooked orders are refundable. Bring ID for every guest. Room type (king or twin) is assigned by the hotel — the package does not let you pick.
