**Beixi Tangjing Hot Spring Lodge （北溪·汤境温泉宿集）** is a soak-stay-and-eat combo in the hills outside Zhangzhou: a hot-spring park plus lodging in one, so the whole weekend happens without moving the car. The park has **17 outdoor pools and 3 indoor pools** fed by metasilicic-acid spring water (≥25 mg/L), plus a forest outdoor pool that stays open through autumn and winter. Then, as evening comes, the campsite BBQ takes over — a two-person set (worth ¥178) with Angus beef cuts, wagyu, Austrian black pork and vegetables, grilled at your own pitch in the woods with background music drifting through the trees.

The rooms are built for families: the private-spring rooms are spacious, open-plan, with their own in-room pool for a late-night soak once the kids are asleep. And the location makes a two-day loop easy — about twenty minutes' drive to **Zhangzhou Ancient City** for snacks and old streets between soaks.

**Packages**
- **Standard room, 1 night — ¥499** (≈$69, was ¥888): breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.2 m); outdoor hot-spring park tickets for 2 adults + 2 kids (open 13:00–22:00); campsite BBQ set for two (worth ¥178); swimming pool; free parking
- **Private-spring room, 1 night — ¥599** (≈$83, was ¥988): same inclusions, with your own in-room hot-spring pool

**Good to know**
- Valid Sep 3 – Dec 30, 2026, Monday to Sunday; **no top-up on weekends**; book by 23:59 the day before
- Extra guests pay top-ups on site (hot-spring park ¥98 weekdays / ¥118 weekends; children 1.2–1.4 m ¥58 / ¥68; breakfast over 1.2 m ¥20 a person)
- Address: No. 1090 Huangqing Village, Guokeng Town, Longwen District, Zhangzhou — about a 20-minute drive from Zhangzhou Ancient City
