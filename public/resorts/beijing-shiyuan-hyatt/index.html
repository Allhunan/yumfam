The Hyatt Regency Beijing Shiyuan sits across the road from the Shiyuan (Expo Park) International Resort in Yanqing — Beijing’s mountain-fringed northwest, near the Badaling Great Wall and the Gui River, in the district that hosted the 2019 World Horticultural Expo and events of the 2022 Winter Olympics. The hotel itself is a low-rise complex of 283 rooms (from about 40㎡, many with floor-to-ceiling garden-view glass, some with private balconies) linked by winding corridors. This package turns the stay into a full family day out: a Hyatt king or twin room, buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 12), and a pick-3 activity pass.

The six options (each for 2 adults + 1 child, one use, no repeats): ① Shiyuan Yueqin Hot Spring — a roughly 10,000㎡ spring center with 30+ pools across six zones including a kids’ water area (child under 1.3 m); ② iQiyi Qibabu kids park, the animation-IP indoor park just across the street (child under 1.3 m); ③ a Longqing Street intangible-heritage handicraft session for one person (tie-dye, beading, bamboo weaving or glaze painting); ④ the Expo Botanical Garden (no child height limit); ⑤ an XR experience of about 25 minutes (kids 6+); ⑥ the Paleontology study base (no height limit). Also included: the Sunday-morning Baduanjin session (9:00–10:00), gym, heated indoor pool, indoor children’s playroom, free parking and Wi-Fi.
