Shankouling （山口凌度假村） is a boutique stay in a century-old village about an hour from central Hefei — the village locals call a “little Jingdezhen” for its old kilns and craft shops. The deal is an all-in-one slow day out: a night in a light-luxury country room, breakfast, two bowls of handmade noodles at the village noodle shop, afternoon tea for two at the village bookshop, a hike up White Horse Mountain (walking sticks provided), camping on the village green, and the village art museum. No top-ups inside the validity period.

Packages

  • Sunshine king / deluxe twin room, 1 night — ¥369 (≈$51, was ¥1,388): 28–32 m², breakfast for two, 2 handmade-noodle meals, afternoon tea for two, White Horse Mountain access, camping, art museum
  • Family / kids’ room, 1 night — ¥469 (≈$65, was ¥1,788): 40–41 m² with a kids’ bed and toys, or a family room with balcony, breakfast for three, same meals, tea and activities
  • Breakfast is free for one child under 1.2 m; extra breakfasts are ¥20 a person

Good to know

  • Check-in from 12:00, check-out by 14:00 next day; reserve by 20:00 on the day you go, then show the voucher code and your ID.
  • The noodle meals are served at the village noodle shop (09:00–19:00 weekdays, to 20:00 weekends) and afternoon tea at the village bookshop — both close earlier on weekdays, so plan around that.
  • Free sights nearby: the former residence of General Zhang Zhizhong, Red Stone Mouth, Chaohu Reed Bay Wetland, Hongjiatuan old village and Changlinhe old town.
  • Bring ID for every guest — adults their ID card, children their household registration booklet (a driving licence doesn’t count).
  • Unused and unbooked vouchers can be refunded.
