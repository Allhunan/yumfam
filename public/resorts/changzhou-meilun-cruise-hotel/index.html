Deal price: ¥599 (≈$83, was ¥998 — 40% off) for 2 nights · ⏰ Valid October 17, 2026 – March 31, 2027 · Weekends & New Year’s Day no surcharge · Refundable if unbooked

The gimmick is the whole point here: the Meilun is a cruise ship that never sails, docked inside Changzhou’s Global Harbor mall complex — cabins instead of rooms, portholes and deck styling instead of corridors, and the mall’s eat-drink-play sprawl downstairs. The base tier is ¥599 for a sailor twin room for either 2 rooms × 1 night or 1 room × 2 nights, with daily breakfast for 2 (kids under 1.2 m free; 1.2–1.4 m about ¥45, taller kids and adults about ¥58), free parking and Wi-Fi. And the surcharge policy is the deal’s quiet superpower: weekends, New Year’s Day and even Chinese New Year’s Eve (Feb 5) carry no surcharge — only Feb 6–10 (the first five days of the New Year) add ¥100/room/night.

If you want the full mall weekend, the upper tiers add the good stuff: the Star Bar afternoon tea for 2, a Port Town ticket for 2 including the Ferris wheel, 20 Tom Bear arcade tokens and 5 laps of the Global karting track; the family-suite tier (four themes to choose from — Baltic, Tribal Chief, Harbour and Coral Sea) adds breakfast for 3 adults + 1 child and Happy Town super-sports-park tickets for 2 adults + 1 child. The mall itself is the rainy-day plan: dining, shopping, cinema and kids’ attractions under one roof, connected to the hotel without going outside.

The hotel’s cruise theme is full-on — if your kids are in a pirate/sailor phase, this is the cheapest way to feed it for a weekend.
