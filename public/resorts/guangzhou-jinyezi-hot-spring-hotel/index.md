**Deal price: 1 night from ¥399** (≈$55, was ¥988) · Valid Nov 18, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 · Any day of the week · Book by 17:00 on the day of use · Refundable if unbooked

They say summer is for water play and winter is for hot springs — but autumn is Jinyezi's best season. Tucked into the forested hills beside the 4A Baishuizhai scenic area, about 26°C year-round and wrapped in 400,000 mu of forest at the foot of the Nankun Mountains, this Country Garden–run resort does the whole family day in one place: swim in the morning, soak in the evening.

The draw is the **natural mountain hot spring** — a spread of pools in the hills with different flavours and temperatures — plus a **big spring-water swimming pool**, a **kids' naughty-castle playground**, and **sauna and stone baths**. The hotel sits on the edge of Baishuizhai, known as the "emerald on the Tropic of Cancer", and the surrounding half-hour is full of easy add-ons: the Baishuizhai waterfall trail, Dafengmen rafting, a jungle adventure park and fruit picking (all pay-as-you-go).

**Packages** — all for 2 adults + 2 kids under 1.2 m, with unlimited hot springs, kids' playground, spring-water pool, sauna and stone baths

- **Garden-view room + breakfast + hot springs — ¥399** (≈$55, was ¥988): 1 night in a garden-view king or twin room with a buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids
- **Garden-view room + breakfast + dinner + hot springs — ¥549** (≈$76, was ¥1,188): the same room and breakfast, plus a dinner set or buffet dinner for 2 adults + 2 kids
- **Mountain-view family suite + breakfast + hot springs — ¥798** (≈$111, was ¥1,988): a family suite with breakfast for 4

**Good to know:** the hotel is at 38 Baishuizhai Avenue, Zengcheng (about an hour's drive from central Guangzhou). Everyone staying must show ID at check-in — adults with an ID card, children with a household register. Book by 17:00 on the day you want to use it; unbooked orders are refundable. The buffet theme and dishes rotate with the season, so the menu on the day is what counts.
