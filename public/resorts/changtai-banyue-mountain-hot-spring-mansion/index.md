Banyue Mountain sits in a forested valley in Changtai, about an hour from Xiamen, built around **strontium-rich soda springs** and a hot-spring park with **50+ pools** you can soak in as often as you like during your stay. The draw of this deal is the room itself: a **50㎡ mansion room with its own private hot-spring soak pool**, so you can bathe without leaving your room, plus breakfast and park entry for **2 adults + 2 kids** (one child under 1.4 m, one under 1.2 m).

**Packages** — every package includes breakfast and hot-spring park tickets for 2 adults + 2 kids, unlimited use of the 50+ pools, free Wi-Fi and parking

- **50㎡ Hot Spring Mansion room (private soak pool), 1 night — ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,398): king (1.8 × 2 m) or twin (2 × 1.35 × 2 m) room with a private in-room hot-spring pool.
- **50㎡ Strontium-Rich Mansion room (private soak pool), 1 night — ¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,398): the same setup in the strontium-rich wing.
- **2 nights, Hot Spring Mansion room — ¥1,598** (≈$222, was ¥2,398): 1 room for 2 nights, or 2 rooms for 1 night.
- **2 nights, Strontium-Rich Mansion room — ¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,398): listed at the same price as a single night — this looks like a platform pricing quirk, so confirm the total at checkout before you pay.

**Good to know:** Saturdays carry a +¥100/night surcharge. Children 1.2–1.4 m pay ¥88 for the hot spring and ¥68 for breakfast; over 1.4 m pays the adult rate of ¥199 (breakfast + hot spring). Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; the hot-spring park runs 13:00–23:00 and breakfast 7:30–10:00. A shuttle runs from Xiamen North Station — book it a day ahead.
