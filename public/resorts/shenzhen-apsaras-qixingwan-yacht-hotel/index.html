Shenzhen Apsaras Qixingwan Yacht Hotel from ¥499 (≈$69): Sea-View Room + Breakfast + Kayaking at the Marina

Deal price: 1 night, 45㎡ deluxe sea-view room (king or twin) — ¥499 (≈$69, was ¥1,299) · Breakfast for 2 · 1-hour kayaking for 2 at Qixingwan Yacht Club · Free in-room minibar · Valid September 24 – December 31, 2026 · ⏰ weekends at no extra charge, refundable if unreserved · Book by 15:30 the same day

A sailing-themed hotel right on the Qixingwan marina in Dapeng — every one of its 80-plus rooms faces the sea with a viewing balcony, so the “sea-view room” here is not an upgrade lottery. The deal pairs the room with breakfast for two and an hour of kayaking for two at the yacht club, plus a free in-room minibar (drinks and snacks in the fridge), free entry to the Dapeng Geology Museum (closed Mondays) and the Hetang Wetland Park, and marina perks: yacht-harbour photo spots, a canopy campsite check-in point, a photo-ready sailboat and a kids’ sand pit. Free parking and Wi-Fi included.

The area is the draw for a 2-day escape: Jidiaosha beach (the “glass sea”) is about 5 minutes away by car, and Yangmeikeng (the Mermaid movie location), Luzui Villa, Dongchong beach and the old walled town of Dapeng Fortress are all nearby day trips. The buy-1-get-1 tier covers 2 consecutive nights or 1 night in 2 rooms, with a choice of a 1-hour sailing trip or 1-hour kayaking for two per room.

Practical notes: check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Book by 15:30 the same day; unreserved vouchers can be refunded. Extra guests: children under 1.2 m are free; 1.2–1.4 m pay about ¥38 for breakfast; over 1.4 m or adults pay about ¥68 for breakfast. Extra kayaking or sailing is about ¥100 per person on site. Self-drivers heading to Dapeng on weekends and holidays need to pre-book their car entry. Address: Apsaras Hotel, Qixingwan Yacht Club, Dadui Road, Dongshan Village, Nan’ao Town, Longgang District, Shenzhen, Guangdong.
