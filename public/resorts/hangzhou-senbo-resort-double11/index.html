Hangzhou Senbo is the Xianghu Lake family-resort institution: 415 rooms scattered through the valley, including the forest wooden cabins that feel straight out of a fairy tale, plus treehouses, castle rooms and family villas. Next door, the newly upgraded Fantasy Island kids’ park adds a ball pit, two-level ninja course, high-altitude expansion, aerial magic net, soccer field and three slides, alongside LEGO rooms, a submarine cabin, princess dress-up, role-play supermarket and kitchen, karting, grass sledding, trampolines and a climbing wall. The 9,200㎡ indoor water park runs all year at a constant temperature with 30+ water attractions under a transparent dome.

The base package covers a 38㎡ wooden cabin (king or twin), two buffet breakfasts at the Rainforest Restaurant plus a kids’ breakfast, free entry to the Senyiji lounge park (PS5, board games, pool table, movie zone, dress-up photo area and the global instant-noodle wall — indoor water park area and the ¥68 credit excluded), a pet-friendly hiking tour with the pet cleaning fee waived and a pet gift pack, welcome fruit and first-day perks.

⚠️ Good to know: Book by 17:00 on the day of use; unused bookings are refundable. Heads-up: the indoor water park closes for maintenance November 30 – December 25 — Fantasy Island and Senyiji stay open. Address: 1515 Xianghu Road, Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou.
