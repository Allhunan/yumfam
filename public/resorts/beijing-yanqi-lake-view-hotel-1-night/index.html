China State Construction Yanqi Lake View Hotel （中建雁栖湖景酒店） sits right on Yanqi Lake in Huairou, 60 km from central Beijing — the lake’s north gate is just 300 metres from the hotel, and the autumn colours on the water are the reason to go now. This Double 11 package covers a premium or deluxe king or twin room for one night, breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.4 m), two coffees, and use of the pool, kids’ castle and gym, with free parking and WiFi. A two-night option at ¥1,499 (≈$211, was ¥1,999) can be used as 1 room for 2 nights or 2 rooms for 1 night.

The hotel has 297 rooms in a grand Chinese-classical-meets-modern style, and the area is made for a slow weekend: Mutianyu Great Wall is 24 km away, Hongluo Temple 9 km, Qinglong Gorge 7 km, and the hotel also has a tennis and badminton hall, table tennis, billiards, KTV and chess rooms.

Good to know

  • No surcharge on New Year’s Day or Spring Festival; Friday and Saturday nights carry a ¥100 per room-night surcharge on site
  • Book by 23:59 the day before; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; all guests show ID at check-in
  • Breakfast covers 2 adults + 1 child under 1.4 m; the pool, kids’ castle and gym are included (subject to the hotel’s opening hours that day)
  • No lake-view room type in this package, and premium rooms do not take an extra bed; cribs and bed rails are limited — confirm with the front desk on arrival
  • Unbooked orders are refundable; once booked, the room is held for the day and is generally non-refundable
  • Address: No. 1 Yanxiu Road, Yanqi Lake, Huairou District, Beijing
