A national Top-100 family resort sitting on Xiamen’s Golden Coast (思明区环岛南路3999号) — the property is separated from the beach only by the marathon track, and an underpass leads straight to Huangcuo beach for sand play. Inside: 1,000,000㎡ of gardens with 20,000+ trees and 400+ rare plant species, 19 pools (outdoor pools open May–Oct), an ~800㎡ Little Dolphin kids’ park (ball pit, trampolines, wave slide), and 622 rooms.

The ¥799 entry tier is the family favorite: the themed “Laughing Bugs” family garden-view twin room with 2 breakfasts plus a go-kart experience. Two-night tiers from ¥1,599 add options like Xiamen Old Courtyard tickets, a picnic-basket afternoon tea, or a poolside villa with buffet afternoon tea. The 2nd-floor Mediterranean café buffet runs 100+ dishes.

What guests say#

94.8% of families with kids 10–12 recommend it (77 votes), 77.3% for ages 3–6. On the merchant’s reviews (43), one guest called it a repeat-visit spot: great greenery, two full days of play, the kids’ park, a little train, the indoor pool and afternoon tea — the beach is out the back gate with shuttles running between areas. Another 5-star reviewer praised the environment and service at state-guesthouse standard and the Ming-Qing-style bamboo-garden rooms, with the honest caveat that the buffet is just average — and a tip to go on a sunny summer day.
