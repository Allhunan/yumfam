Haiyue Shanzhuang is Xiamen’s garden grande dame — a state-guesthouse-standard resort spread across a million square meters of sea-and-mountain gardens on Huandao South Road: 20,000+ trees, 400+ rare plant species, a 700-meter boardwalk climbing to a sea viewpoint, and — almost unheard of for one hotel — 19 swimming pools, including Xiamen’s only standard-length infinity pool. The beach is right there too: cross the marathon track through the underpass and you’re on Huangcuo beach, soft sand and clear water, close enough for sunrise shell-collecting with the kids.

The ¥899 headline room is a Larva （爆笑虫子）-themed family mountain-view twin in the bamboo-courtyard, Ming-Qing-style villa zone, and the bundle is stacked: buffet breakfast for two plus one extra kids’ breakfast with no height limit (two children under 1.2 m eat free regardless), a day-one minibar and welcome fruit, a pony-cart or mini-train ride through the gardens, one professional photo-shoot session (book it several days ahead — guests warn it fills up), the garden plant-exploration trail, and free run of the pools and the ~800㎡ Dolphin kids park (ocean-ball pit, trampoline, wave slide). Indoors there’s a heated pool for winter; the outdoor garden pools and water park run May–October.

⚠️ Weekends cost nothing extra — but Chinese New Year does. For the ¥899 and ¥999 tiers, Feb 5 and Feb 10, 2027 add ¥600/room/night, and Feb 6–9 add ¥1,200/room/night, paid at the hotel; the 3-night sea-view tier adds ¥2,000/night across Feb 5–10. Every other date through April 30, 2027 — including ordinary weekends and the New Year holiday — is surcharge-free. Book by 23:59 the day before and bring your voucher code plus ID; once booked, the room is held all day but is generally non-refundable. Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00.
