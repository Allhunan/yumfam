Yingde Tianyue Kingdom Hotel （英德天樾王国酒店）, formerly the Hilton DoubleTree, is a castle-style hotel in Yingde, about two hours from Guangzhou — built around karst hot springs and a chocolate-themed kingdom that kids love. The ¥559 package covers a garden-view king or twin room for one night, a buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 2 children (one child under 1.4 m, one under 1.2 m — the first time the free child allowance has been widened to 1.4 m), an oyster-and-beer dinner buffet (all-you-can-eat oysters, free-flow beer), unlimited soaking in the karst hot-spring water world (30+ pools across seven themed zones, plus dry and wet saunas), and the indoor kids’ playground, table tennis and billiards. On Friday and Saturday nights the hotel runs its anniversary programme — a Wa ethnic bonfire party, stage shows and a castle fireworks display at 20:30.

Two upgrades are worth knowing: ¥999 (≈$141, was ¥2,388) buys the same package as buy-one-get-one-free — either 2 nights in 1 room or 1 night in 2 rooms; ¥699 (≈$99, was ¥1,688) adds two days of unlimited Chocolate Kingdom park entry (a 5-minute drive away).

Good to know

  • No surcharge on weekends or during the winter holiday; New Year’s Day Jan 1–2 carries a ¥100 per room-night top-up
  • Room upgrades on site: lakeside room +¥50, family room +¥200 per room-night
  • Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; bring ID for every guest (children need their household registration booklet); book by 17:30 on the day of use
  • The hot spring flows at 72 °C with 30+ pools; fireworks may be cancelled or adjusted in rain
  • Unbooked orders are refundable; once booked, the room is held for the day and is generally non-refundable
  • Address: No. 1 Aoyuan Avenue, Yinghong Town, Yingde, Qingyuan, Guangdong
