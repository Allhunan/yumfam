**Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child ¥59.9** (≈$8) · Valid Nov 19, 2026 – Jun 30, 2027 · Weekends & public holidays: no surcharge · Buy and use the same day · Refundable if unused

Forty-five minutes from Xiamen, with no weekend top-up. **Duoleng Hot Spring** in Jiaomei is a 20,000㎡ seawater hot-spring garden where every pool is carved from a single block of granite — dozens of tonnes of yellow granite, hollowed out one by one. The deal is a **day ticket for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.2 m for ¥59.9**, usable any day of the week including weekends and public holidays, with no surcharge at all. (The same venue also sells a hotel stay with unlimited springs — see our [Duoleng Hot Spring Hotel page](/resorts/zhangzhou-duoleng-hot-spring-hotel/) — this ticket is the day-visit version.)

**What the ticket covers**

- **The granite pools** — dozens of themed pools scattered through the garden, including the "General's Spring", the "Sky-Edge Spring" (a terraced infinity pool), the Tai Chi twin pools in a yin-yang layout and the "Butterfly Love Spring" carved with mandarin ducks
- **A 600㎡ swimming pool** with a large spa section — 17 functions and 57 jets — plus dry and wet steam rooms
- **A children's area** and family pools, so the ticket works as a half-day outing with kids

**Good to know:** The ticket is valid 10:00–23:00 and can be bought and used the same day; unused tickets are refundable and the voucher auto-refunds after expiry. Extra children are charged at the door: 1.2–1.5 m ¥38, 1.5 m and over the adult price of ¥50 — so the ¥59.9 ticket still beats the ¥88 walk-up price for one adult and one child. The platform lists ¥999 as the original price for this ticket; the door prices above are the more useful comparison. Expired tickets can still be used with a ¥30 top-up per ticket.
