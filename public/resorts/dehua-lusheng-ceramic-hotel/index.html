Dehua is the “Porcelain Capital of the World”, about two hours from Xiamen, and this hotel is built around that identity — a ceramic-themed stay where the factory and the museum are connected to the hotel itself, so you tour them indoors with a guide rather than going out in the sun. The deal includes the guided factory and museum visit, and if you book by 24:00 on November 25 you get a free ceramic DIY session (one per booking; the family room also gets a free mug).

The ¥499 city room covers breakfast for three and lunch for two, which suits a family of three; the Tao Bai family room (¥759) covers breakfast for four and adds the DIY session. There is a small rooftop open-air pool for the kids, and the rooms are the draw in themselves — ceramic design throughout, with guests singling out the big bathtubs, sofas and king beds.

Good to know

  • Valid Nov 18, 2026 – Jan 30, 2027, any day of the week, with no top-up.
  • The free ceramic DIY session (and the free mug on family rooms) applies only if you book by 24:00 on November 25, 2026.
  • Book by 23:59 the day before you stay. Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 the next day.
  • Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
  • The ceramic factory and museum are inside the hotel complex, with a guide for the tour. The rooftop pool is small — fine for kids splashing, not for laps.
