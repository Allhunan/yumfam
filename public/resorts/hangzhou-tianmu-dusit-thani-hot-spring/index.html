West Tianmu Mountain is Hangzhou’s “natural oxygen bar” — ancient-tree forest an hour from downtown — and the Dusit Thani Tianmu Mountain is the Thai-hospitality group’s quiet wellness outpost inside it: 160 villas and rooms in muted, return-to-nature tones, every room with a viewing balcony or private courtyard facing the mountain green. The room in this deal is a 50㎡ garden-view twin (two 1.35 m beds, floors 2–3, non-smoking) with the details Dusit regulars notice: King Koil mattresses, Kohler smart toilets, and Tianmu tea sets in the room. Breakfast for two is included at Yueshan Restaurant, whose floor-to-ceiling windows face the layered ridges.

The soak is the 2-night tier’s bonus: a 30-minute private outdoor wellness soak for two. The hotel’s spring area has 5 outdoor pools themed on the five tones of classical Chinese music （宫商角徵羽）, and its NAMM Thai spa (named for the Thai word for water) sits alongside — spa treatments are self-pay, as is the spring area beyond your included session.

⚠️ Read the tiers carefully: the ¥599 one-night tier is room + breakfast only — the private soak comes with the ¥999 two-consecutive-nights tier. Reserve by 17:59 the day before; check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00. A deluxe courtyard twin upgrade runs +¥100/room/night. Confirmed bookings are held all day and generally non-refundable; unreserved vouchers refund anytime. Every guest, children included, needs ID at check-in (household register for kids).
