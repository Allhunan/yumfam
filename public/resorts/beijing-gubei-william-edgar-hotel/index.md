# Gubei William Edgar Hotel from ¥899 (≈$125): 1 Night with Town Tickets, Hot-Spring Tickets & a Great Wall Day Trip

**Deal price: 1 night + breakfast for 2 + town tickets ×2 + hot-spring tickets ×2 — ¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,210) · Valid Nov 20, 2026 – Jul 15, 2027 · Book at least 2 days ahead · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

The William Edgar is one of the hotels inside Gubei Water Town, the canal town under the Simatai Great Wall — and this is its simplest deal: one night, breakfast for two, town tickets and hot-spring tickets for two, plus a pick-one extra. The standout choice in that extra is the Simatai Great Wall day ticket, which turns the stay into a wall-and-water-town weekend without a separate ticket purchase.

Like the other Gubei hotels, the stay includes multiple re-entries to the water town and unlimited rides on the shuttle bus while you are there. The hot-spring tickets cover the town's three bathhouses (Yuda, Yudao and Chengshang — whichever is open that day).

**Packages**

- Superior twin room B or superior king room B, 1 night — **¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,210): breakfast ×2, Gubei Water Town tickets ×2, a travel-pass book or a plaque fridge magnet, one of: Simatai Great Wall day tickets ×2, a DIY lamp, or afternoon tea for two, plus hot-spring tickets ×2

**Good to know**

- The price is calculated for 2 adults; children 1.2–1.5 m pay a supplement based on the room and activities.
- Saturday top-up is ¥300 per night (Nov 2026 – Jun 2027) and ¥350 on Friday–Saturday nights in July 2027. Holiday top-ups apply: New Year (Dec 31 – Jan 2, ¥300–¥600), Spring Festival (Feb 5 – 11, ¥300–¥600), Qingming (Apr 3–4, ¥600), May Day (May 1 – 4, ¥300–¥600) and Dragon Boat Festival (Jun 8, ¥600).
- Book at least 2 days ahead (by 23:59 two days before) — rooms are first-come, first-served, so book early for weekends and holidays.
- Check-in is 15:00, check-out 12:00 the next day. Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
- Bring your own swimwear and swim cap for the hot springs, or buy them on site.
- Once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable; unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded.
