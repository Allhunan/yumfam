A 210,000㎡ wellness resort tucked into Tong’an’s Tingxi valley — forest, hot springs, herbs and good food, far from the city noise. Shengzhixiang sits on natural sodium-bicarbonate springs from deep under Tingxi, nicknamed “beauty soup” water, and its signature is the cave hot spring: warm in winter, cool in summer, constant temperature all year. The outdoor spring park runs original-soup pools with free tea service, pools of every size hidden among flowers by an infinity pool, and water that flows and refreshes continuously, cleaned and disinfected daily. Between soaks, stroll the hillside boardwalk, and kids get a water park and an indoor playground on site.

Your bundle: 1 night with breakfasts for 2 adults and 2 kids (kids = under 1.2 m) and unlimited outdoor hot-spring entry for the whole stay — come and go as you please. Rooms are generous (46–62㎡ in the luxury tier) with big bathrooms; the deluxe king even has a bathtub, so you can soak in-room if you skip the pools. Free parking and Wi-Fi throughout.

⚠️ Booking & extras: Reserve by 23:59 on the day of use; refundable any time before you reserve. Weekend surcharges apply on site: +¥100/room/night on Fridays, +¥200 on Saturdays (higher on May Day and Mid-Autumn holidays — check the booking page for the exact date).
