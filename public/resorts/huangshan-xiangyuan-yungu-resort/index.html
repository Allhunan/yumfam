A ¥150-million Huizhou-style resort built from a cluster of five historic Anhui dwellings, right inside the Huangshan scenic area on the must-pass road to the Yungu cableway — so you wake up on the mountain instead of commuting to it. Autumn here runs mid-October to late November, when the forests turn gold and the air smells of osmanthus; sit in the courtyard with tea, then soak in the mountain hot springs as the mist rolls in. The hotel won the “Five Continents Diamond Award — Brand Resort Hotel of the Year” at the 2020 World Hotel Forum.

Your bundle: 1 night with breakfasts, mountain hot-spring soaks, first-day minibar, a Tangkou–hotel shuttle with luggage service, and Huangshan ticket booking service. The deluxe king (36㎡, 1.8 m bed) starts at ¥730; the 42㎡ deluxe view rooms add a terrace option. Xialv orders also get a free breakfast + hot-spring soak for one child under 1.5 m (worth ¥122).

⚠️ Booking & extras: Reserve by 20:00 on the day of use; refundable before you reserve. No surcharge on weekends or holidays within the validity period. Note: the package does not include the Huangshan scenic-area ticket itself (the hotel helps you book it), and private cars cannot drive into the scenic area — transfer to the scenic bus at the Tangkou transfer center, then the hotel shuttle meets you.
