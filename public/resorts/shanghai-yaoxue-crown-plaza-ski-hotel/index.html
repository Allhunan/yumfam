Shanghai Yaoxue Crowne Plaza （上海冰雪世界皇冠假日酒店） is the official hotel of Yaoxue Snow World, a 98,000㎡ indoor snow park in Lingang — walk through Xingyao Plaza and you are at the slopes. The hotel leans into the theme: snowflakes, snowboards and snowy-mountain motifs throughout, 370 rooms, and a spiral staircase that has become a photo spot in its own right. It is also a good base for the wider Lingang area — Haichang Ocean Park, the Shanghai Astronomy Museum, Dishui Lake and the Maritime Museum are all nearby (their tickets are not included in this deal).

The ¥999 snow-play package covers 1 night in a deluxe room, breakfast for two the next morning, snow-play tickets for 2 adults + 1 child, and a set lunch for 2 adults + 1 child at the hotel’s Jingyan restaurant, plus gym access and free parking. The ¥1,200 ski package swaps the play tickets and lunch for 2 full-day, unlimited ski passes. Both packages run 15:00 check-in to 12:00 check-out.
