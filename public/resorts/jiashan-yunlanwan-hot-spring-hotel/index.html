Yunlanwan is a 1,300-acre hot-spring resort in Jiashan, right in the middle of the Yangtze Delta — an easy weekend trip from Shanghai, Hangzhou or Suzhou. The hot-spring centre covers 30,000㎡ with 60 pools across nine zones, and the resort adds a flower-sea park with a mini train, a building-block park for younger kids, and a free shuttle inside the grounds, so a family can spend the whole stay without leaving.

The base tier is a 43–45㎡ deluxe king or twin room with breakfast for two, two hot-spring tickets (up to three entries during the stay), two flower-sea park tickets including the mini train, and two building-block park tickets. The family hot-spring room (60–70㎡) has its own private spring and stretches the breakfast and tickets to three people; the family room sleeps four with bunk beds and stretches everything to four. One tier adds a Chinese dinner set for two — the same set served on the resort’s variety show.
