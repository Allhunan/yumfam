Jinsuiyuan Hot Spring （同安金穗园温泉） is a 4A-standard hot-spring park in Tong’an, Xiamen — about 25 acres of gardens, pavilions and winding paths hiding 50+ wellness pools, a national-standard indoor hot-spring pool, a large spa area and a Jurassic-themed hot-spring water park for kids. The water is the draw here: a rare silicate spring drawn from 158 m underground, coming out at 65°C, famously silky on the skin — the old line about it is “spring water so smooth it washes away the dust.”

The park is built for a slow day out rather than a quick dip. Wooden boardwalks link the pools, there are shaded seats for resting between soaks, and you can bring your own snacks — soak, sunbathe, eat a little, soak again. The kids’ side has its own small water park with slides (warm water, so long play sessions don’t end in chills), and there is an indoor pool for rainy days. Note the kids’ water park closes for winter, so in the cold months the draw is the warm pools themselves.
