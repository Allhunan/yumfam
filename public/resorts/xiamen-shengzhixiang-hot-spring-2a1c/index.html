Deal price: 1 night + hot springs for 2 adults + 1 child — ¥588 (≈$81, was ¥1,699, about 65% off) · 2 nights from ¥799 (≈$110) · Valid Aug 15 – Dec 30, 2026 · Usable right after purchase · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

Shengzhixiang Hot Spring Resort （厦门盛之乡温泉渡假村） spreads over 210,000㎡ in Tingxi Town, Tong’an — Xiamen’s “ecological town,” tucked into forested hills far from the city noise. The draw is the water itself: natural bicarbonate springs from deep under Tingxi, nicknamed “beauty soup,” feeding a cave hot spring that stays a constant temperature all year, plus an open-air spring park of overflowing pools set among the trees, a wooden boardwalk up the hill, and free tea at the poolside.

The ¥588 package covers 1 night in a deluxe room (1.8 m + 1.5 m twin beds) with hot-spring passes and breakfast-or-lunch for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.5 m — take the breakfast, or swap the whole party’s meal to a Chinese set lunch (braised beef with radish, white-cut chicken or honey-glazed char siu, pick 3) if you tell the hotel a day ahead. A second ¥588 tier swaps the lunch for a buffet breakfast for two plus 2 set lunches. The ¥799 tier covers 2 nights in a premium king room (splittable) with 2 hot-spring passes per stay and no breakfast. One extra child under 1.2 m stays free on every tier.

  • Address: No. 1168 Chaoyuan Road, Tong’an District, Xiamen (southwest gate) — about 40 minutes’ drive from Xiamen island
