Tianmu Lake is one of the Yangtze Delta’s classic escapes — clear water, scattered islets, a winding shoreline — and Hantian Resort sits right on its peninsula, a private-lake-island-style property of 4,000+ mu backed by hills and wrapped in bamboo. The name says it all: “han” for water, nourishment and openness; “tian” for the fertile land that holds everything. Stand on the hillside terrace, breathe the bamboo air, and watch the lake wind away between layered mountains.

This Double-11 deal covers the resort’s Mengshe hotel and its sister properties, with tiers for every kind of trip: bring the dog, bring the kids, or just bring yourselves. Every tier includes breakfasts, Linghuwan park tickets, a hands-on craft activity, a resort photo shoot and the resort’s daily activities. The pet-friendly tier adds a pet bath and pet boarding; the family tier adds the 1,000㎡ indoor trampoline park, the outdoor Songguo playground and Hape park tickets; the top tiers add 180° lake-view hillside hot springs and lake-view rooms.

⚠️ Booking & extras: Weekends carry no surcharge on these tiers. Rooms cannot take an extra bed in the Mengshe and Songguo tiers (extra beds in the mountain-view and lake-view tiers are ¥300/bed/night including breakfast). Kids’ breakfasts in the family tier cover children under 1.4 m. Check the booking page for date-by-date availability before you buy.
