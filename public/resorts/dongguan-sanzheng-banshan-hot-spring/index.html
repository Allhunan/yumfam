Built into a hillside above a natural lake, Sanzheng Banshan is the hot-spring resort Shenzhen and Guangzhou families default to when the weather turns: 210,000 square metres of grounds wrapped in protected forest, with a 11,500-square-metre outdoor pool winding between the room blocks and a hot-spring zone of 38 pools across 8 areas and 2 gardens. The base tier covers 2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.2 m) for 1 night in a garden-view room (lake-view rooms also available) with Western buffet breakfast, unlimited Banshan Spring Water World, the elf kids’ water park (a warm-spring pool with a giant tipping bucket and slides), the coconut-shade white-sand beach, indoor pool, sauna and fish therapy, plus the brand-new outdoor non-powered kids park, the indoor time-travel play corridor, the Tianchi spa house and the youth-park lawn — all unlimited during your stay.

Getting here is easy from either side of the border region: about 40 minutes’ drive from Shenzhen, 25 minutes from Zhangmutou, 10 minutes from Dongguan South station (16 minutes by high-speed rail from Shenzhen North, and 40 minutes onward to Hong Kong West Kowloon). The hotel has 558 rooms in several styles, and the grounds add a gym, kids’ club, an indoor heated pool and lake activities (motorboats, electric boats, sailing — some at extra cost).
