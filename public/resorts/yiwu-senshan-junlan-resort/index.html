Senshan Junlan is a brand-new Junlan wellness resort that began trial operation on November 10, 2026, inside the 6,000-acre Senshan health town in Yiwu — Song-dynasty styling, white walls and dark tiles, built around dendrobium (tiepi shihu) herbal culture, facing a lake and backed by forested hills. It is a short trip from Yiwu International Trade City, Fotang Ancient Town and Hengdian World Studios, with high-speed rail, light rail and expressway connections.

Every tier includes breakfast for two, welcome fruit on the first day, a hanfu dress-up experience, an AI traditional-Chinese-medicine body scan at the in-house Senshan pharmacy, and access to the Lan Jingling kids’ club, the heated pool, the children’s pool, the gym and the yoga room. The two-night tiers can be used as one room for two nights or two rooms for one night — handy for two families travelling together or for bringing grandparents along.
