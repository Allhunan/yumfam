# Yiwu Senshan Junlan Resort — New Wellness Retreat from ¥499 (≈$69), No Top-Up on Weekends or Holidays

**Deal price: 1 night in a lake-view room + breakfast for 2 + hanfu experience + AI body scan — ¥499** (≈$69, was ¥699) · 2 nights in a garden king room **¥599** (≈$83, was ¥999) · 2 nights in a lake-view room **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,299) · Valid Nov 12, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 · No top-up on weekends, New Year or Spring Festival

Senshan Junlan is a brand-new Junlan wellness resort that began trial operation on November 10, 2026, inside the 6,000-acre Senshan health town in Yiwu — Song-dynasty styling, white walls and dark tiles, built around dendrobium (tiepi shihu) herbal culture, facing a lake and backed by forested hills. It is a short trip from Yiwu International Trade City, Fotang Ancient Town and Hengdian World Studios, with high-speed rail, light rail and expressway connections.

Every tier includes breakfast for two, welcome fruit on the first day, a hanfu dress-up experience, an AI traditional-Chinese-medicine body scan at the in-house Senshan pharmacy, and access to the Lan Jingling kids' club, the heated pool, the children's pool, the gym and the yoga room. The two-night tiers can be used as one room for two nights or two rooms for one night — handy for two families travelling together or for bringing grandparents along.

**Packages**

- 1 night in a lake-view king or twin room + breakfast for 2 + welcome fruit + hanfu experience + AI body scan + kids' club — **¥499** (≈$69, was ¥699)
- 2 nights in a garden king room (1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night) + breakfast for 2 per night + welcome fruit + hanfu experience + AI body scan + kids' club — **¥599** (≈$83, was ¥999)
- 2 nights in a lake-view king or twin room (1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night) + breakfast for 2 per night + welcome fruit + hanfu experience + AI body scan + kids' club — **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,299)

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 12, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027, any day of the week. No top-up on weekends, public holidays, New Year or Spring Festival — the price you see is the price you pay. Book by 17:00 the day before; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00.
- Breakfast for children: under 1.2 m free, 1.2–1.4 m half price, 1.4 m and over full price. Welcome fruit is only for the first night.
- The resort is in trial operation (opened November 10, 2026) — some facilities may still be bedding in; the heated pool, kids' pool, gym, yoga room and kids' club are all listed as available.
