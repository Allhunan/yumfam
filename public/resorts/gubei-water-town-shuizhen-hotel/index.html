One price that bundles the whole Gubei Water Town weekend: the ¥919 tier buys a deluxe room (twin or king, just outside the water town) for 1 night with breakfast for 2, 2 Gubei Water Town tickets, 2 hot-spring tickets, plus a choice of afternoon tea for 2 or a Great Wall day trip, and a DIY craft (lotus lantern, New Year woodblock print, or a painted kite). While you stay, you get unlimited re-entry to the water town and unlimited shuttle rides inside it, plus free pool and gym access at the hotel. Saturdays work too, with a ¥200 per room-night surcharge on the 1-night tier (and December 31 at the same surcharge).

Gubei Water Town is Beijing’s canal-town escape at the foot of the Simatai Great Wall — grey-brick lanes, lantern-lit canals at night, and hot-spring bathhouses to warm up in after walking the walls. Staying at the Shuizhen Grand Hotel by the visitor centre puts you a short shuttle ride from the lanes, and the bundled tickets mean you don’t pay entry and hot springs on top of the room.
