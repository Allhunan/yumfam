# Zhangzhou Southeast Flower City Hot Spring Hotel — 1 Night for 2 Adults + 1 Child from ¥298 (≈$41)

**Deal price: 1 night for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.4 m) + breakfast + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets — ¥298** (≈$41, was ¥518) · 2 nights **¥558** (≈$78, was ¥1,036) · Garden villa room **¥358** (≈$50, was ¥618) · Bamboo house with fishing **¥498** (≈$69, was ¥828) · Valid Nov 18, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 · No weekend top-up

Southeast Flower City is a hillside hot-spring resort in Zhangpu, about an hour from Xiamen, built in terraces up the mountain with shuttle carts running everywhere. The deal is priced for the family unit — 2 adults + 1 child under 1.4 m — and every tier includes breakfast for the three of you, hot-spring tickets and tickets to the Flower Expo Park next door, which runs a light show in the evening. The headline ¥298 tier is the 25 m² Yingbin Building room; the garden villa (¥358) and the 60 m² bamboo house (¥498, with a fishing session and its own private spring) step up the room, and every tier has a 2-night version that works out cheaper per night.

At ¥298 with no weekend top-up, this is one of the cheapest hot-spring stays on the platform this winter — the listing's own pitch is "eat, stay and soak for one price". The trade-off guests mention is food: the resort is out on its own, so dinner means the small restaurants nearby (about 15 minutes away) or the hotel's own dining.

**Packages**

- Yingbin Building room, 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets — **¥298** (≈$41, was ¥518): 25 m² double or twin room
- Yingbin Building room, 2 nights (or 1 night × 2 rooms) + breakfast + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets — **¥558** (≈$78, was ¥1,036): same inclusions per night
- Garden villa room, 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets — **¥358** (≈$50, was ¥618): 25 m² garden villa room
- Garden villa room, 2 nights (or 1 night × 2 rooms) + breakfast + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets — **¥658** (≈$91, was ¥1,236): same inclusions per night
- Bamboo House king room, 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets + fishing session — **¥498** (≈$69, was ¥828): 60 m² room with its own private spring
- Bamboo House king room, 2 nights (or 1 night × 2 rooms) + breakfast + hot-spring tickets + Flower Expo Park tickets + fishing session — **¥888** (≈$123, was ¥1,656): same inclusions per night

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 18, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027, any day of the week, with no weekend top-up.
- Book by 23:59 the day before you stay. Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 the next day.
- Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
- The resort is spread over a hillside — shuttle carts run throughout, and the hot-spring pools, kids' water playground and Flower Expo Park are all on site. Bring swimwear and a swim cap for the springs.
- Dining is limited on site; small restaurants nearby are about 15 minutes away.
- Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded, and expired unused vouchers refund automatically. Once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable.
