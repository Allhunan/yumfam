Kaiyuan Resort sits on a peninsula in Qiandao Lake (Thousand Island Lake), and it is built for families who want to stay put: the 3,000㎡ Fantasy Island car town, a petting zoo, the Sensen sports field, a fish-print craft workshop, a photo shoot, and an indoor heated pool are all inside the resort, and the lake is right outside. The rooms are 35㎡ superior twins or lake-view kings/twins on the upper floors, and every tier includes breakfast for two at the Hawaii Western restaurant.

The car town ticket covers 1 adult + 1 child, and the top lake-view tier adds the resort’s signature fish-head dinner set (¥388 value) at its Chinese restaurant — except on Chinese New Year’s Eve, when the set dinner is not available.
