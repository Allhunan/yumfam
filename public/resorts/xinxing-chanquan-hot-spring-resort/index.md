**Deal price: deluxe room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + unlimited Zen hot springs + dinner for two — ¥999** (≈$139, was ¥1,599) · Valid Nov 12, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Saturday +¥200 · Refundable if unbooked

South China's first Zen-culture hotel sits right beside Guoen Temple — the home temple of Huineng, the Sixth Patriarch of Zen — in the Liuzu Hometown resort area of Xinxing, Yunfu. The whole stay is built around slowing down: unlimited soaks in the Zen hot springs, indoor and outdoor pools, and a choice of **six Zen activities** (tai chi, Zen music, yoga, seated meditation, sutra copying or Go). The package also includes a set dinner for two (three dishes, a soup and pastries, served on arrival day or the next lunch), a visit to the Zen culture art gallery and its rosewood exhibition hall, plus billiards, a kids' play area, a film centre, a gym and catch-your-own-fish fun by the stream.

**Packages**

- Deluxe room (king/twin) for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.2 m + unlimited Zen hot springs + indoor/outdoor pools + dinner for two + 1 Zen activity of your choice — **¥999** (≈$139, was ¥1,599)

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 12, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027, any day of the week. Saturday carries a +¥200 per room per night top-up; upgrading to a lake-view deluxe room is +¥100; public holidays float with hotel pricing.
- Book by 18:00 the day before use; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
