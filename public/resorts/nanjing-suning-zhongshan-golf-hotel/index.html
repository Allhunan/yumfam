A spacious, quiet base on Nanjing’s green east side: Suning Zhongshan Golf Hotel stands on Huanling Road in the Xuanwu District, by the Zhongshan (Purple Mountain) scenic area. The headline room is a generous 52㎡ with a balcony and a 1.81 m king bed, and the tier bundles breakfast for two, a seasonal two-person afternoon tea in the lobby lounge (14:00–17:00), a free first round from the minibar, and unlimited play in the hotel’s 200㎡ castle-style indoor kids’ park — the kind of setup where a rainy day still works out.

The same ¥999 (≈$139, was ¥1,499) also buys the 70㎡ courtyard family room for one night (breakfast, minibar and kids’ park, without the afternoon tea). Two nights in the 52㎡ garden balcony room run ¥1,599 (≈$222, was ¥2,999). The top tier, a 100㎡ garden suite for two nights, is ¥2,698 (≈$375, was ¥4,598) — note this tier alone ends December 31, 2026, while the other tiers run to March 31, 2027.

Practical notes: there is no surcharge on weekends or holidays — the only surcharge in the whole sale is ¥300 per room-night over February 7–9, 2027 (Chinese New Year period). Breakfast for children: up to two children under 1.2 m eat free per room; 1.2–1.4 m is half-price ¥99, over 1.4 m is full-price ¥168. Extra beds are ¥300 per bed per night (breakfast not included). Check-in is from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Book by 20:00 the day before; rooms are first-come, first-served, and a confirmed reservation is generally non-refundable while unreserved vouchers can be refunded. Address: Suning Zhongshan Golf Hotel, 9 Huanling Road, Xuanwu District, Nanjing, Jiangsu.
