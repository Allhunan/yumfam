One island, one hotel: the Free InterContinental owns the whole of Xianshan Peninsula on Qiandao Lake, ringed by water on three sides, so the “resort” and the “scenery” are the same thing. The base tier is 2 nights in a 49-square-metre forest room (king with a 2-metre bed, or twin — either 1 room × 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night) with breakfast for 2 each morning and 2 coffees (Americano or latte), plus a long shared-perks list that is the real value here: a couples’ session at the new mountaintop “Dead Sea” hot spring, wellness experiences (morning wake-up, sound-healing lunch break, family yoga, gong-release show), workshops (pin-bean crafts, board-game parties, DIY sun hats and heritage cloth-paste art), a swan-garden feeding session, Xianshan Botanical Garden (0.45 km away), exploring Xianshan’s trails and viewpoints, an 1800-square-metre indoor kids’ park (unlimited), a high-speed-rail shuttle for 2 adults + 1 child, a 15% Free Island discount coupon and a Moon Gate photo session (3 prints) — most bookable with a day’s notice.

The rooms lean into the setting: nearly every room has dual balconies — watch the sunrise colour the lake from one, the sunset from the other — with Oriental-Zen styling in pale wood and grey, and the higher tiers add lake views, Bulgari toiletries and a deep soaking tub. Days here run slow by design: lakeside cycling, hiking the peninsula trails, a yacht cruise from the hotel jetty (extra cost), coffee on the rocky beach by day and the Free Island blues live show with BBQ and mojitos by night.
