**Deal price: 1 night with breakfast + any 3 of 6 activities — ¥699** (≈$96, was ¥1,938, about 64% off) · Valid Nov 13, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027 · No weekend top-up · Book by 23:59 the day before · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

**Beijing Hyatt Place Shiyuan （北京世园凯悦酒店）** sits right across the road from the Beijing Expo Park in Yanqing — the capital's "back garden" of mountains, gorges and wetlands, home to the 2019 World Horticultural Expo and the 2022 Winter Olympics. The hotel's 283 rooms (from about 40㎡) look out over the Expo gardens, and the whole stay is built around indoor play: in winter you never have to brave the cold to get from one activity to the next.

The **¥699 package** covers 1 night in a Hyatt king or twin room, breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 12), and **any 3 of 6 activities** (no repeats): hot-spring tickets for 2 adults + 1 child at the Shiyuan Yueqin Hot Spring, iQIYI Qibabu kids' park tickets, a non-heritage craft workshop ticket on Longqing Street, Botanical Pavilion tickets, a 25-minute XR experience (kids 6+), or a palaeontology research-base visit. You also get the heated indoor pool, gym, indoor kids' play area, free parking and Wi-Fi, plus a Sunday-morning Baduanjin (eight-brocade exercise) session.

Two more tiers run on the same base: **¥799** swaps the pick-3 for a winter package — hot-spring tickets plus either 4 hours of skiing for two at Shijinglong Ski Resort (expected open Nov 30, 2026 – Feb 26, 2027) or a hotpot dinner for two — and **¥1,299** covers 2 nights (or 2 rooms for 1 night) with daily breakfast, a hotpot dinner for two and the same pick-3 activities. The ski tier is only valid through Feb 26, 2027.

**Good to know**
- Valid Nov 13, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027 (ski tier through Feb 26, 2027); book by 23:59 the day before; show the booking code, phone number and ID at check-in
- Top-ups: ¥500/night on Jan 1–2, 2027 and ¥600/night on Feb 5–11, 2027; the ski tier also adds ¥100/night on Fridays and Saturdays
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; the hot-spring centre is a 10,000㎡ complex with 30+ pools, and the Qibabu kids' park is across the street
- Unbooked vouchers are refundable; once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the full day and is generally non-refundable
- Address: Building A, No. 1 Fukang South Road, Yanqing District, Beijing — about 90 minutes from central Beijing by car
