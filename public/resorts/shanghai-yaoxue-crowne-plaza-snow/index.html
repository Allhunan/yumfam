Shanghai Yaoxue Crowne Plaza （上海冰雪世界皇冠假日酒店） is the official hotel of Yaoxue Ice & Snow World — walk through Xingyao Plaza and you are at the snow park. The park is a 98,000㎡ indoor snow world kept at −3°C to −5°C all year, with a real-snow ski slope and a Nordic-themed snow-play zone of 10+ activities: the Snow Country Train, ice-cave exploring, snow climbing, speed sledding, ice bikes, snow-house building and more. The hotel itself leans into the theme — 370 rooms with ski and snow-mountain details (even the tissue box is shaped like a snow cabin), a spiral staircase that has become a photo spot, and a 24-hour gym.

The ¥999 snow-play package covers 1 night in a 32㎡ deluxe room (king with a 1.8 m bed, or twin with two 1.2 m beds), a buffet breakfast for two the next morning, snow-play tickets for 2 adults + 1 child (single entry, unlimited time that day), and a lunch buffet for 2 adults + 1 child at the Jingyan restaurant (11:30–13:00, book at least 1 day ahead). The ¥1,200 ski tier swaps the snow-play tickets and lunch for 2 full-day, unlimited ski tickets and is valid Nov 13, 2026 – Mar 30, 2027.

Lingang is a full family weekend on its own: Shanghai Haichang Ocean Park, the Shanghai Astronomy Museum, Dishui Lake and the China Maritime Museum are all nearby (tickets extra).
