Deal price: from ¥428 (≈$59) a night (was ¥668) · Private hot-spring room + breakfast for two + 2 hot-spring passes · Valid Nov 4, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 · Book at least a day ahead · Unused vouchers are refundable

Longjia Hotel （漳州龙佳大酒店） sits in its own eco hot-spring villa in Jiaomei — the town Xiamen locals call the city’s “back garden” — about 40 minutes out on Metro Line 6. The deal is a room with its own private soaking pool: you get the room, breakfast for two, and two passes to the hotel’s hot-spring pools, so you can soak in your room and then again in the shared springs. It’s an old, established hot-spring hotel rather than a new resort, and the reviews read like a regulars’ favourite.

Packages

  • Elegant private hot-spring room, 1 night — ¥428 (≈$59, was ¥668): 45 m² room (double or twin), breakfast for two, 2 hot-spring passes, private in-room pool
  • Deluxe private hot-spring view room, 1 night — ¥498 (≈$69, was ¥798): same inclusions, view room
  • Deluxe private hot-spring view room, 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night — ¥888 (≈$123, was ¥1,596): breakfast and 2 passes per room-night
  • Themed family room with private pool, 2 nights or 2 rooms × 1 night — ¥1,111 (≈$154, was ¥1,936): Alice/XR-tech, little-yellow-duck or bear themes, breakfast and 2 passes per room-night
  • Breakfast and the hot springs are free for up to 2 children under 1.2 m per room

Good to know

  • Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00 next day; reserve by 23:59 the day before you go, then show the voucher code and your ID at check-in.
  • Extra charges apply on public holidays and their connected days off; children 1.2–1.4 m pay ¥40 for breakfast and ¥50 for the hot springs on site.
  • Bring ID for every guest — adults their ID card, children their household registration booklet (a driving licence doesn’t count).
  • The hotel is a short walk from the old Tianyi Post Office, a century-old landmark worth a look while you’re there.
  • Unused and unbooked vouchers can be refunded, and expire unused automatically after the validity period.
