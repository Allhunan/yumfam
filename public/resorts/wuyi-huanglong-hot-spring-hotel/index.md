**Deal price: 1 night in a 47–58㎡ room — ¥399** (≈$55, was ¥584, about 32% off) · 2 nights from ¥748 (≈$103) · Valid Sep 22 – Nov 30, 2026 · **Weekends: no top-up** · Book by 12:00 on the day · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

**Wuyi Huanglong Hot Spring Hotel （武义温泉黄龙饭店）** opened at the end of 2025 in Wuyi Hot Spring Town — a county certified by China's Ministry of Land and Resources as a "Hot Spring Town," set on volcanic rock. The hotel is run by the Huanglong Hotel Management Group (the same group behind Hangzhou's landmark Huanglong Hotel, founded 1988), and it shows: a French-style resort manor with crystal chandeliers, Roman columns and fountains, and rooms dressed in a quiet "fluorite green." The 47–58㎡ Senyue rooms (1.8 m king or two 1.35 m twins) come with a small balcony, and the whole property sits a 3-minute walk from the Wuyi Hot Spring Fluorite Museum.

The **¥399 tier** covers 1 night with breakfast for two, two lobby-bar coffees, a welcome fruit plate on the first night, minibar drinks, and free use of the gym, indoor heated pool, basement kids' playground, billiards and table-tennis rooms. The **¥748 2-night tier** adds a hot-spring pass for two (the indoor spring area has original-spring, whirlpool, hot-cold alternating pools, rock baths and a sauna). Other tiers add horse riding at the OKK Equestrian Club (4 laps, ¥499 for 1 night with springs) or a family set meal (¥549 for 1 night, ¥849 for 2 nights with springs). For multi-family trips there is a 500㎡ villa — 4 bedrooms, private hot-spring pool and private swimming pool — from ¥3,788 a night.

**Good to know**
- Valid Sep 22 – Nov 30, 2026, any day of the week; **no weekend top-up** (National Day top-ups have passed); book by 12:00 on the day of arrival
- Getting there: about 2 hours by high-speed train from Shanghai to Wuyi North Station, then ~30 minutes by car (19 km); about 2.5 hours' drive from Hangzhou
- Nearby on foot: Wuyi Hot Spring Fluorite Museum (3 min), the hot-spring resort area (4 min), Wuyi Hot Spring Town (8 min); Jingyuan ancient-folk-house museum is 2 km away
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; show your ID at check-in (one valid ID per adult, household register for children)
- Unbooked vouchers are refundable; once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the full day and is generally non-refundable
- Address: Wuyi Hot Spring Town, Wuyi County, Jinhua, Zhejiang
