A brand-new hot-spring opening near Xiamen: the Jiulongshuizhen Resort’s Taoyuan Kou Ancient Hot-Spring Wellness Center （漳州九龙水镇度假区， No. 666 Taikouzhai, Shajian Town, Hua’an County — about 40 minutes from Xiamen North station and 40 minutes each from Zhangzhou Ancient City and the Eryi Lou tulou) is a full Song-dynasty daydream. The resort covers some 2,500 mu of forest parkland; the spring center alone runs to about 100 mu with 60+ ancient-style pools, an infinity pool, a family fun pool, stone-slab springs and a “black forest” therapy corridor, shaded by camphor trees up to 860 years old. The water carries 11 trace minerals — fluoride, metasilicic acid and radon among them — and next door stands the Taoyuan Kou ancient fort, a Southern Song stronghold rebuilt as lanes of food stalls, costume rental, tea houses and spa rooms.

The bundles are built for one adult + one child: entry from ¥269, then add-ons stack up — a ¥68 “Song-style Fujian” set meal, perilla drinks, hot-spring eggs, an hour of costume dress-up (10:30–18:00), fish therapy, a craft session (10:30–16:30, book at the front desk), a 60-minute herbal foot soak, or a five-element traditional spa. Multi-packs go up to 10 paired tickets.

⚠️ Read the weekday rule before buying: vouchers are for Monday–Friday use (hours 13:00–24:00; last entry 23:30). Weekend visits are possible but carry an on-site surcharge of ¥50 per adult / ¥30 per child (Sat–Sun hours 10:30–24:00). Book by phone — 0596-702 6666 — by 20:00 the day before. Child rules are height-based: under 1.3 m free with a paying adult (one per adult); 1.3–1.5 m pays the front-desk child rate (¥89 weekdays / ¥129 weekends); above 1.5 m it’s the adult rate. Unreserved vouchers refund anytime.
