Inside Hangzhou Senbo Resort by Xiang Lake, Koala Jungle is a 4,000-square-metre treetop adventure park: 40+ crossings strung through the forest — suspension bridges, a Tarzan jump, high swings and a 70-metre Andes zipline — plus a 12-metre climbing wall. The equipment is French-imported, and coaches supervise every course. Three routes run by difficulty: green (1.2 m+), blue (1.2 m+) and red (1.4 m+), so families can start easy and work up; children 1.2 m and under do the green and blue routes with a parent alongside. A free resort shuttle runs from the hotel lobby to the course.

Practical notes: buy by 23:59 the day before; swap your voucher for a wristband at the ticket window and keep it on — one wristband per person, no re-entry without it. Riders must weigh under 90 kg. The park does not operate in bad weather (rain or snow), so pick a clear day; all climbing is coach-supervised and you must not climb without your harness on. Unused vouchers are refundable any time. Address: Koala Jungle Adventure Park, inside Senbo Resort, Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang.
