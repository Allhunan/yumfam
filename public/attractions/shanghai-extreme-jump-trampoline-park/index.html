Extreme JUMP （极限JUMP蹦床馆） is a 3,000㎡ three-floor indoor trampoline park in Kangqiao, Pudong — part trampoline park, part indoor playground, part de-stress zone. One ticket covers all-day unlimited play across dozens of activities: free trampolines, the signature 90° devil slide, an 8-metre jump platform, the sticky spider wall, trampoline basketball, a climbing zone, a big slide, plus VR, racing games, table tennis, baseball and a brain-teaser challenge course. It works for both kids and adults — and on a rainy or freezing day, it is entirely indoors.

The park’s star attraction is the 90° slide: a near-vertical drop that looks intimidating and delivers exactly the adrenaline it promises. The 8-metre platform is three storeys up — plenty of people climb it, far fewer jump. The parents’ rest area overlooks the kids’ routes, so adults can keep an eye on things while recharging.

Good to know

  • Open 09:30–21:00 daily; buy and use the same day, no advance booking needed
  • Grip socks are required — bring your own rubber-soled grip socks or buy a pair on site for ¥10 (ordinary socks are not allowed in)
  • Children under 1.1 m need a ticketed adult with them inside
  • Unused vouchers are refundable
  • Address: Room 202, Zone B, No. 518 Kangshan Road, Kangqiao Town, Pudong New Area, Shanghai
