Changlu Holiday Park （顺德长鹿旅游休博园） in Shunde, Foshan is the kind of park where one day is genuinely not enough — which is why this ticket is good for three days. One ¥99 ticket covers a whole family group — 2 adults + 2 kids, or 2 adults + 2 seniors, or 3 adults — and packs in entry, the Fairy Tale Animal Kingdom, the land-sea-air theatre, 3 rides of your choice from 12, ¥100 of amusement credit and ¥20 of animal-feeding credit, plus vouchers for the Earth Village magic show (2 adult + 2 child) and the ice-snow world (4 × ¥75), and four viewing tickets for Changlu Farm.

Families who have been coming for years call it the value king of the area: the park is big and mostly shaded, with mist fans and rest spots everywhere, so even grandparents and small kids can last a full day. The beef noodles at the gate get mentioned a lot, and there is food all through the park at reasonable prices.
