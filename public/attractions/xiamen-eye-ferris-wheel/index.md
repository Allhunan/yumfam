**Deal price: child ticket (1–1.4 m) from ¥39.9** (≈$6, was ¥78) · Valid Nov 9 – Nov 30, 2026 · Buy-and-use, no reservation needed · Refundable anytime, auto-refund after expiry

The Xiamen Eye is the new landmark wheel at **Haishang World** in Huli — 62 metres up, with 32 spherical glass cabins that each hold 6 people, air conditioning and free WiFi inside, and a full 360° panoramic window. One slow revolution takes about 13 minutes, and the view changes completely with the light: Gulangyu in the daytime, the sunset over the bay, and the harbour lights after dark. Go in the evening if you can — that's when the cabins light up and the night view is at its best.

From the top you can pick out **Gulangyu** across the water, the spiral **mountain-sea health trail** winding up Fox Tail Mountain, and **Haicang Bay** on the far side. The wheel is built on a sail-shaped frame with a spoke-style rim — it reads as a landmark even from the ground, which is why families treat it as the centrepiece of a Haishang World afternoon rather than a standalone ride.

**Good to know:** one full-price adult ticket can bring one child under 1 m free. Children 1–1.4 m need a child ticket; students, seniors over 65, military personnel and police get the concession rate. Every Tuesday the wheel is closed for maintenance in the morning and opens at 14:00. All tickets in one order must be used together — if your group wants to ride separately, buy them as separate orders.
