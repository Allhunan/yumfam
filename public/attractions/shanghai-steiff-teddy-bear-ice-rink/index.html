A giant Steiff teddy bear has moved into Shanghai’s Jiuguang Center for the winter: a 12-metre-tall bear from the 145-year-old German toy maker towers over a real-ice rink that just opened on the mall floor. The early-bird deal is the cheapest ice time on the platform this season — ¥39.90 for a single 90-minute session, or ¥69.90 for two people — with skates, helmet, knee pads and elbow pads all included in the price.

This is a pop-up rink, not a full-size arena, so think of it as a festive skate-and-photo stop rather than a training session: glide for 90 minutes, take the photo under the bear, and be back in the mall. The rink runs 10:00–21:30 (last entry 20:30) every day, and the ticket works on weekends and public holidays with no top-up.

Packages

  • Early-bird single ticket — ¥39.90 (≈$6, was ¥89): 90 minutes on the ice, any age, skates/helmet/pads included
  • Early-bird two-person ticket — ¥69.90 (≈$10, was ¥189): two people skating together, 90 minutes, same inclusions; the pair must enter together and can’t be split across two visits

Good to know

  • A ¥2 accident-insurance policy per person is compulsory and bought at the rink desk on arrival — budget ¥2 extra per skater.
  • Wear long trousers; the rink supplies skates, helmet, knee pads and elbow pads, but not clothing.
  • Children aged 3–4, or under 1.2 m tall, must be accompanied by at least one adult who has also bought a ticket.
  • The dessert displays around the rink are decorations — they are not edible, and children should not climb on them or touch them.
  • In rain or special weather the rink may adjust its opening hours depending on the ice — check the on-site notice on the day.
  • The ticket is used by showing the booking code at the rink — no exchange needed — and unused codes can be refunded, including after expiry.
