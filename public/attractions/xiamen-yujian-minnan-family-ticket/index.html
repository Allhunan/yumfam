Yujian Minnan （屿见闽南） is an immersive theme park in Huli District that drops you into old coastal Fujian: Minnan-style streets and courtyards, costumed NPC characters wandering the park to chat and play, live performances, and a stamp-collection game — gather the stamps and the kids can redeem a souvenir popsicle. It is all indoors, so it works in any weather, and it photographs beautifully in traditional dress. This autumn family ticket covers 1 adult plus 2 children (one aged 16 or under, one aged 6 or under).

Good to know

  • 1 adult + 1 child aged 16 or under + 1 child aged 6 or under; open 09:30–18:30, last entry 30 minutes before closing
  • Book at least 30 minutes ahead; tickets are real-name — enter with your ID (or the QR code from the booking message)
  • The Minnan Fengyun Theatre show, costume experiences, food and shopping are not included
  • Unbooked orders are refundable, and expired orders auto-refund
  • Address: No. 15 and 17–19 Donggang North Road, Huli District, Xiamen
