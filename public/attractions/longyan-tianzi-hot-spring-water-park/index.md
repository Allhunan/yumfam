Tianzi is a forest hot-spring resort in Yongding, Longyan — about a two-hour drive inland from Xiamen — built around more than 100 spring pools scattered through the trees, plus a 50,000-square-metre water park on the same site. One ticket covers both: soak in the forest pools, then the water park for the kids. The springs run 12:00–22:00 and the water park 12:00–18:00, per the listing. In November the hot springs are the main draw — the water park is a summer feature, so check its current opening status on the day if that is what you are coming for.

The ticket is the flexible kind: buy it and use it the same day, no advance booking, refundable any time if unused, and it auto-refunds on expiry. Up to 3 tickets per phone number.

**Packages**

- 1 adult + 1 kid (under 1.2 m) hot-spring + water park day ticket — **¥128** (≈$18, was ¥288): entry for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.2 m to the hot-spring pools and the water park
- 1 adult + 1 kid (under 1.5 m) hot-spring + water park day ticket — **¥175** (≈$24, was ¥376): entry for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.5 m to the hot-spring pools and the water park

**Good to know**
