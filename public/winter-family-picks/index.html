Harbin ice and snow#

Harbin’s Ice and Snow World — the giant ice city on the Songhua River — is expected to open in late December 2026, with its opening ceremony expected on January 5, 2027. It is not open yet, and the booking platform has no Ice and Snow World ticket on sale at the moment, so the two tours below are the closest bookable way into the north’s ice season: both are verified on sale now, with fixed departures and prices as listed. The Yichun tour spends two nights in Harbin with free time for the Ice and Snow World (its ticket is not included — buy it separately), while the Keshiketeng tour is the only December-departure northern snow tour available.

2 nights in Harbin with free time for the Ice and Snow World (ticket not included), 1-on-1 ski lessons, ice fishing, a jade hot spring at −30°C and the Northeast Tiger Park. Departures Jan 19 – Feb 21, 2027.

Outdoor skiing on the platform comes as guided multi-day family tours instead, and the Keshiketeng tour listed above already opens with a day of ski lessons in Chongli. The tours below are the ski-inclusive ones we verified on sale on November 17, 2026; the Changbaishan tour was verified on sale on November 18, 2026, the morning it opened for booking. Spring Festival week (February 5–13, 2027) is peak season — expect crowds and book early; the Spring Festival departures printed on the pages are listed with each tour.
