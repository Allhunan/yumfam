**Deal price: 5-day / 4-night tour, child — from ¥3,380** (≈$470; adult from ¥3,980) · Departures Nov 11–21, 2026 and Jan 23 – Feb 11, 2027 · One family minimum to run

This is a proper expedition, not a sightseeing bus: five days across **Guizhou** built around the **FAST radio telescope** — the world's largest single-dish telescope — plus a real **caving descent on ropes**, a **Miao village** visit and hands-on geology, led by an astronomy professor, cave coaches and a geology guide. Days run from Guiyang to Pingtang (FAST) and Kaili (caves and Miao culture), with a professional family-tour leader and a photographer along.

**The trip (5 days / 4 nights)**
- Day 1 — Guiyang: meet at the museum for the opening ceremony (15:00), welcome banquet in the evening; overnight at a Huazhu luxury hotel
- Day 2 — Pingtang: FAST astronomy experience centre, climb to the FAST observation deck, lecture from an astronomy professor; overnight at a starry-sky hotel
- Day 3–4 — Kaili: cave exploration with ropes and flat-belt descent (helmets, headlamps and full caving suits provided), Miao village and intangible-heritage crafts; overnight at an Atour hotel in Kaili
- Day 5 — Return: group drop-off at Longdongbao Airport, then Guiyang North station
