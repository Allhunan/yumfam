A "singing Chinese-character culture class": the story opens with a crisis — the character spirit "Zi" discovers that Chinese characters are disappearing. Modern schoolboy Zhang Xiaoyuan stumbles into the museum's world of writing and teams up with Zi to travel through time, meeting a Shang-dynasty shaman, a Qin general, a Tang girl, a Tang calligrapher, a Ming scholar and a Republican-era farmer on a quest to recover the characters. Along the way children pick up how oracle-bone script was born, how Qin "unified the script," how calligraphy developed, and how traditional and simplified characters evolved. The staging draws on oracle-bone rubbings, steles and classical calligraphy for a museum-time-travel feel; the music mixes Chinese-style children's songs, pop and rock, with the theme song "Square Chinese Characters" and a dance, "One Horizontal, One Vertical," that acts out strokes with the body. Interactive Q&A and call-and-response keep children thinking and reading along rather than just watching.

Practical notes: the show runs about 90 minutes with no intermission and suits ages 6 and up. After booking, seats are locked in order of purchase and the order is confirmed once tickets are issued; on the day, collect your paper ticket at the theatre's ticket centre with your booking voucher (morning show: exchange 09:00–10:30; afternoon show: 09:00–15:00). Getting there: Metro Line 2 to Dushu Lake South, exit 1, then a 200-metre walk; or bus 59, 515 or 138 to "Yinshan Lake Songlin Park." Address: Yinshan Lake Grand Theatre, 886 Qinghe Road, Guo Xiang Subdistrict, Wuzhong District, Suzhou.
