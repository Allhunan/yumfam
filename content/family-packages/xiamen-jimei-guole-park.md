**Guole Park** （集美果乐园， 3535 Tongji South Road, Jimei, Xiamen) is a 40,000㎡ outdoor kids' park built around animals and open space. The **14-item family ticket** bundles gate entry with one run each on the park's rides and animal experiences. The animal side is the heart of it — **30+ species** across themed zones: a **petting garden** (rabbits to stroke, geese splashing, marmots to feed, parrots to teach, ostriches and camels to pose with), a **sika deer park** pitched as Xiamen's "mini Nara," and a **starry-sky pasture** where kids hand-feed goats, alpacas and deer. The **Pine Cone amusement area** adds 10+ rides — a luxury carousel, pirate ship, flying swing chairs, toy race cars and a sky-walk car — plus a physical-play zone with climbing nets, swings and obstacle runs, a Hobbit-house play cottage, and a **fruit-and-veg farm** (picking is self-paid). The piglet races are the unlikely crowd favorite, per the reviews below.
