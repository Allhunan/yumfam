**Deal price: from ¥59** (≈$8) · ⏰ Valid Oct 9–Dec 31, 2026 · ⏰ Usable on weekends and public holidays, no reservation or ticket exchange needed · ⏰ Refundable anytime

**Brightland （耀心岛）** is Shanghai's immersive indoor kids' play-park brand — not the usual ball-pit template, but miniature real-world cities where children check in for a "flight" (real security screening, boarding and meal service), bake with real ovens, run a supermarket checkout, staff a police station, hospital and fire crew. The **LCM Pudong store** (3F, LCM Place mall) packs 3,000+㎡, 100+ activities and a 200,000-ball ocean pit; the **Qingpu store** (Yuanzu Dream World, Zhao Xiang) is a ~5,000㎡ fairy-tale castle inside a "tree hole," with 24 themed scenes over three floors. This offer pairs it with the **Four Seasons Ice & Snow World** in the same Qingpu mall — real snow, slides and snow tubes — so one card covers both parks in one trip. Suggested play time is 4–6 hours per park.

The tickets are low-friction: buy-and-use, no booking or gate exchange, valid any day including holidays, unused vouchers refundable anytime. "Adult" = 18+, "kid" = under 18 (suggested ages 0–12); each child must stay with the same accompanying adult.

⚠️ **Two entry quirks:** the **Qingpu Brightland store is single-entry and closed on Mondays**; the LCM Pudong store allows same-day re-entry. At the snow world, same-day re-entry is fine, but tickets can't change hands, children can't stay unaccompanied, and no food is allowed inside. The ¥59 companion ticket can't be used alone — it must ride along with a full ticket; each extra adult pays the ¥80 gate companion rate, and each extra child needs another full ticket.

**Packages**
- Brightland companion ticket (adult 18+, must accompany a ticket holder) — **¥59** (≈$8, was ¥80)
- Dual-park card: 1A1C all-day at Brightland + 1A1C all-day at the snow world — **¥168** (≈$23, was ¥446)
- 3-visit card: 3 × 1A1C all-day Brightland tickets + a ¥30-off snow-world voucher — **¥399** (≈$56, was ¥1,014)
- Not included: in-park food, retail, crafts and some classes

**Good to know**
- Hours: LCM Pudong 10:00–20:30 (Mon–Thu & Sun), 10:00–21:00 (Fri–Sat); Qingpu 10:00–20:00
- Nursing rooms, rest areas, lockers, free Wi-Fi; bibs, tableware, blankets, diapers and bottle warmers lent free
- Some rides have height/weight limits — follow staff guidance on the day
- Addresses: LCM Place 3F, Pudong · Yuanzu Dream World, No. 6200 Jiasong Highway, Qingpu (snow world: 3F, No. 6088 Jiasong Middle Road)
