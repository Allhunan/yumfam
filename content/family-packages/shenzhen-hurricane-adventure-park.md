In Shenzhen's Nanshan district, Hurricane Adventure is a full-sensory immersive park built around two original shows that loop all day: **The North Sea Kraken** and **Ne Zha Stirs the Sea**. A giant immersive screen, multi-sensory effects and motion-adventure staging put you inside the story rather than in front of it — and one ticket covers both shows with **unlimited same-day re-entry**, so you can step out for a meal and come back for another round.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Buy and use the same day — no ticket exchange needed, just show the booking code at entry. Hours 10:00–21:00. Refundable anytime before use. Address: 340 m west of the Baishi Road / Haiyuan 2nd Road intersection, Nanshan District, Shenzhen.
