**Deal price: ¥69** (≈$10, was ¥109) for 1 adult + 1 child · 2 adults + 1 child ¥89 (was ¥129) · ⏰ Valid through Nov 30, 2026 · Refundable if unreserved

The Korean teddy-bear phenomenon lands in Pudong: **TEDDY ISLAND's Teddy Bear Jungle Park** (L3, Meida Commercial Plaza, Lane 3-8, No. 5 Mingfei Road, Zhuqiao Town) builds an indoor playground around the Jeju Teddy Bear Museum's beloved IP — plush bears in costume tucked through painted jungle scenery, equal parts playdate and photo shoot. The **9 play zones, all included in the ticket**, are: a **kids' theater** with interactive games, a **snake slide**, **interactive projection games**, a **craft workshop zone** (DIY makes to take home), an **ocean-ball pit**, a **sand-play area**, a **baking zone**, a **reading house**, and **spinning chairs**. It's a contained, soft, photo-friendly half-day for the under-school-age crowd — the Zhuqiao location also puts it on the Pudong airport side of the city, an easy add-on around a Disney or flight day.

⚠️ **Days differ by ticket:** the 1-adult + 1-child ticket runs **Wednesday to Sunday only** (closed Mon–Tue); the 2-adults + 1-child ticket is usable **any day of the week**. Both are **usable immediately after purchase** — no advance reservation needed. Children **under 1.3 m must be accompanied by an adult**, and everyone needs **anti-slip socks** to enter — bring your own or buy a pair at the front desk.

**Packages**
- 1 adult + 1 child, all 9 zones — **¥69** (≈$10, was ¥109) · Wed–Sun only
- 2 adults + 1 child, all 9 zones — **¥89** (≈$13, was ¥129) · any day

**Good to know:** the themed event days earlier in the season (Mid-Autumn lantern riddles and DIY lanterns, a National Day program Oct 1–3) have passed; the standard 9-zone play runs through the end of November. Check the day of the week against your ticket tier before heading out — that's the one trap in an otherwise simple deal.
