In Lingang, Pudong (Lane 888, Qiqing Road, next to Haichang Ocean Park), the **Ice-Snow Mingcheng Hotel** is the south-gate partner hotel of **Yaoxue Snow World** — a ~350,000 m² indoor snow resort whose 98,000 m² of real snow is held at −3 to −5 °C all year: ski slopes for every level plus a Nordic snow-play zone (snow-house building and a rainbow snow village on the 3rd floor, then the Snow Country Train up to the 5th-floor Blanc base camp for speed sleds and snow-ladder adventures). The hotel itself is built like twin snow peaks, with superior king/twin rooms decorated with skiing artwork, viewing windows and wet/dry-separated bathrooms — and it's a short walk to the snow park entrance. Every bundle below includes **gift hot-spring tickets** alongside the snow passes, and unreserved vouchers refund anytime.
