This is the closest bookable way into Harbin's ice season on the platform right now: the tour spends **2 nights in Harbin** (Steigenberger, or Crowne Plaza as the alternative), then heads 4 hours east into the snow forests of Yichun in the Lesser Khingan Mountains. One thing to know upfront: **the Harbin Ice and Snow World visit is on your own time during the Harbin free time — its ticket is not included in the tour price.** What is included is the Northeast Tiger Park, the ski lessons, the ice carnival and the hot spring below. The Harbin nights give you the time to visit the Ice and Snow World, St Sophia Cathedral and Central Street yourself — the hotel is about 15 minutes from the Ice and Snow World.

Highlights, as listed on the live product page:

- **2 nights in Harbin** — Steigenberger (or Crowne Plaza Harbin), inside the Sunac cultural tourism city, with free time for the Ice and Snow World, St Sophia Cathedral, Central Street and the Baroque old street.
- **Riyuexia ski resort** — an SSS-standard ski area in the forest, with a **1-on-1 coach for 1 hour** of beginner instruction, then free skiing on three graded slopes plus a half-pipe; ski gear (skis, boots, helmet) included.
- **Baxian Lake ice carnival** — ice curling, ice dragon boat, ice sledges and spinning ice tops, plus ice fishing and a traditional winter-net fishing show with fresh fish soup on the ice.
- **Jade hot spring at −30°C** — a natural mineral hot spring at Taoshan, for the classic ice-and-fire contrast: snow falling on your head while you soak.
- **Ewenki tribe** — dress in Ewenki clothing with reindeer-antler hats for snow-forest photos, visit the Ewenki folk village, ride a horse-drawn sleigh through birch forest, and meet the reindeer up close.
- **Northeast Tiger Park** — feed the tigers from the special feeding vehicle, with fresh meat strips provided.
- **Stays** — 2 nights Steigenberger Harbin, 1 night Taoshan Jade Hot Spring hotel, 2 nights in a Russian-style castle hotel in the forest; 7 set meals included (northeastern dishes: iron-pot stew, crispy pork, chicken stewed with mushrooms).
