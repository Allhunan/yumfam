One of the best-value family days out in Qingdao right now. **Sports Town Fun World** sits inside the 200-mu Citizen Fitness Center on the city's north coast (Metro Line 8, Citizen Fitness Center station; near the international expo center and the outlet mall), and a single ticket unlocks **10 attractions unlimited all day**: space walk, peace mission, rainbow slide, lakeside train, bumper cars, dream carousel, bay racing, dynamic flying chairs, high-altitude rafting and a water yacht — plus **3 themed parks** (dinosaur park, cute-pet park and a non-powered play park) and the brand-new **QIQI indoor trampoline** zone. Lifelike dinosaurs — T. rex, the meat-eating giant Niu Long — walk you straight into a Jurassic jungle, and the bouncy-cloud zone suits kids of all ages. You can even bring your own tent and camp on the lawn for an outdoor day.

The park blends sport, leisure, rides, sightseeing and study trips in one place, with room for 20,000 visitors, and it's built for family interaction: climbing, orienteering-style challenges, a big wooden adventure zone and fitness equipment alongside the rides.

⚠️ **Booking & extras:** Buy and use the same day, **no ticket exchange needed**; refundable any time. All rides are unlimited on the day, subject to what's actually open that day.
