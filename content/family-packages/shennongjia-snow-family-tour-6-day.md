Shennongjia is the quiet alternative to the big-name ski resorts. The pitch on the live page is blunt: Xinjiang and Changbai Mountain are crowded this winter, Shennongjia is not — and it is much closer to central China, reachable by high-speed rail or flight via Yichang. The ski field sits on the "Roof of Central China" at over 3,000 metres: a 100-plus-day snow season, soft powder the page compares to the Alps, and 15 runs from beginner slopes to wild-snow areas, so the whole family finds a run at its own level.

The difference from most ski tours is the time on snow. Where other itineraries allow half a day, this one blocks out **a full day at Shennongjia International Ski Resort** — ski ticket, skis and boots included, with **two hours of beginner coaching at a one-coach-per-family ratio**, and both skis and snowboard available. The rest of the trip is built around the place itself: the golden snub-nosed monkeys that survived here since the Ice Age, the Shennongding summit, the Three Gorges Dam, and the legend of Shennong, the mythical emperor who first tasted a hundred herbs in these mountains.

The route, as listed on the live product page:

- **Shennongjia International Ski Resort** — a full day of skiing on 15 runs, with a 2-hour beginner lesson for the family and snow-play extras (snow tubes, snowmobiles, snow frisbee, snowball fights and snowmen). The page calls it the "ceiling of southern skiing".
- **Golden snub-nosed monkey research area** — a guided nature watch among the 80-plus monkeys living in the forest, with a science talk on biodiversity and the monkeys' Ice Age survival story.
- **Shennongding, the 3,106-metre summit** — the highest peak in central China, with snow forest, alpine meadow and karst scenery on the climb.
- **Shennong Altar and the herbal garden** — the birthplace legend of Emperor Shennong, the 24 solar terms garden and an introduction to traditional Chinese herbs.
- **Three Gorges Dam study course** — a guided visit to the dam with a study session for the kids.

**What's included:** 5 nights in 5-diamond hotels (3 nights at the Yichang Hengda, 2 nights at the Kangdi Junlan in Muyu town), 5 breakfasts and 5 main meals, first-gate tickets for the sights listed above, the ski ticket with skis and boots plus the 2-hour family coach, and a one-day snowfield photo shoot. Travel insurance is included.
