**Deal price: Xishan room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + Super Wings playland ticket — ¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,688) · Valid Nov 20, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027 · New Year & Spring Festival zero top-up on the dinner tier · Refundable if unbooked

A well-known Beijing family hotel at the foot of the Western Hills, Wukuang Junlan is built for winter weekends with kids: the **Super Wings playland** (one child + one accompanying parent per ticket), the free **Lanjingling kids' activity programme** (book ahead — slots fill up), the free kids' play centre, an indoor pool with a warm shallow pool for little ones, a gym and free parking. The headline tier pairs all of that with breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child; the other two tiers swap the playland for the **Cloud Exploration Park + Nanwang's noodles** set meal, or for a **weekend buffet dinner for 2 adults + 1 child** — the dinner tier carries zero top-up over New Year and Spring Festival.

**Packages**

- Super Wings playland package: Xishan room (king/twin) for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.5 m + 1 Super Wings playland ticket + free kids' activities + free play centre — **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,688)
- Cloud Exploration Park + noodles package: Xishan room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + Cloud Exploration Park + Nanwang's noodles set meal + free kids' activities — **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,299)
- Weekend buffet dinner package: Xishan room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child + buffet dinner for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.5 m + free kids' activities — **¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,299), zero top-up over New Year and Spring Festival

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 20, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027, any day of the week. Top-ups float with occupancy — check the live top-up price before booking.
- Super Wings playland hours: weekdays 09:00–12:00 and 14:00–17:00; weekends and public holidays add a night session 18:00–21:00. Bring your room card — one child + one parent enter together, unlimited entries on either day of the stay.
- Breakfast child pricing: under 1.2 m free, 1.2–1.5 m ¥69, over 1.5 m ¥138 (adult price). Book by 23:59 the day before use; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00.
- Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded; once booked the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable.
