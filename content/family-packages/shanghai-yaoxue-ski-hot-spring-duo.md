Ski in the morning, soak in the afternoon. **Yaoxue Snow World** in Lingang is a 98,000㎡ indoor snow park held at **−3°C to −5°C all year**, with five slopes — a green run (81 m), a beginner run (75 m), a blue run (460 m), a red run (314 m) and a black run (340 m), 1,270 m of slopes in total. This duo deal pairs a **4-hour intermediate/advanced ski pass for two** with **hot-spring tickets for two** at the Crowne Plaza hotel next door, so you warm up properly after the cold.

**Packages** — both include a 4-hour intermediate/advanced ski ticket for two (all slopes, magic carpet and indoor gondola; gear rental included: snowwear, skis or snowboard, boots, poles for twin-tip skis only, and helmet — no deposit) plus a hot-spring ticket for two (bring your own swimwear; hot spring on the 3rd/5th floor of the Shanghai Snow World Crowne Plaza)

- **Weekday duo — ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,196): Monday to Friday only.
- **Weekend duo — ¥899** (≈$125, was ¥1,196): Saturday and Sunday only.

Overtime on the slopes costs ¥40 per 30 minutes on weekdays (2 hours of overtime upgrades you to unlimited time). Lockers and the snow-play area are not included. Both tickets must be used on the same day, and the hot-spring ticket is a free gift — if you don't use it, it isn't refunded.

**Good to know:** skiing runs 10:00–20:00 (last entry 16:00); the hot spring runs 13:00–23:00 on weekdays (last entry 22:00) and to 00:00 on weekends (last entry 23:00). Tickets are real-name and for your own use only; under-18s must be accompanied by an adult. It is properly cold inside — bring a hat, gloves and a mask, and a waterproof pouch for your phone (the rental gear has no pockets).
