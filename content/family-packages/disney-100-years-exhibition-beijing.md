The China premiere of *Disney 100 Years of Wonder* is now open at Beijing Chaoyang Joy City (10F, 101 Chaoyang North Road): a walk-through century of Disney drawn from the **Walt Disney Archives** — the studio's own vault of original props, costumes and artwork, most of it never previously shown in China. The route moves decade by decade through the stories: early animation treasures, Cinderella's glass slipper, Marvel artifacts, a full-size BB-8 from the Star Wars sequels, and immersive music-and-story galleries that build to a cinema-style finale hall. For a parent who grew up on these films, half the fun is watching your kids meet the same characters in the same rooms — it's a genuinely three-generation exhibition, not a kids-only play space.

**How a visit runs:** tickets are **buy-and-use with no exchange step** — book through the exhibition's WeChat account and enter with your booking QR code plus the purchase QR. Entry is one-time per ticket. Day sessions run **10:00–22:00 (last entry 21:00)**; the cheaper night session runs **18:00–22:00 (last entry 21:00)**. Give it 1.5–2 hours at a family pace — the archive galleries reward lingering, and the finale hall is worth timing your exit around rather than rushing. Chaoyang Joy City is a full mall, so the natural family plan is dinner downstairs first, then the night session upstairs after 18:00 when the galleries are calmest.

**Getting there:** the mall sits on Metro **Line 6 (Qingnian Lu station)** in Chaoyang District — from central Beijing it's a straight subway ride with no transfers from most of the east side, and there's mall parking if you drive (weekend evenings fill up; the subway is the calmer option for a night-session visit).
