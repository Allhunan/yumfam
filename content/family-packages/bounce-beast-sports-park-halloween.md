**Bounce Beast Super Sports Center （弹力猩球·超级运动中心）** turns its indoor sports arenas over to a month-long Halloween party — the **"Beast Halloween Carnival Month" （怪开猩狂欢月）**, a parent-child festival opening on Halloween day itself, Oct 31, running through Nov 29. The seasonal programme layers **a trick-or-treat parade, pumpkin battles, a zombie maze, broomstick flying races, a scarecrow relay and eyeball bowling** over the usual trampolines, climbing and aerial courses, with a cauldron-fishing side quest and Beast Coins to win and swap for limited surprise gifts. Three cities take part: **Shanghai** (three stores — Qibao Link, Sun Palace and Nanxiang Impression City MEGA), **Hangzhou** (Joy City) and **Chengdu** (Zhaoshang Damofang).

⚠️ **Read the timing and entry rules before you buy:**
- **Nothing works before Oct 31.** A ticket bought today activates on Oct 31 — and it can't be used on the day you buy it, so order by 23:59 the day before any visit.
- One ticket covers **1 adult (18+) + 1 child (age 3+, under 150cm)** for a single timed session — 3 hours in Shanghai and Hangzhou, 4 hours in Chengdu. Kids aged 3–8 must be accompanied inside the arena; 8+ may play unaccompanied.
- Entry is by wristband after front-desk check-in. You may step out and re-enter on the same wristband, but **the clock keeps running** and it can't be passed to someone else.
- **Grip socks are mandatory and not included** — bring your own or buy a pair at the front desk for ¥10. Lockers are free.
- Who can't take part: under-3s, over-50s, guests over 90kg, pregnant guests, and anyone with hypertension or heart conditions. Limit: 2 tickets per phone number all season; at peak times expect a queue number for entry.
