Guangzhou's "Elf Forest"-themed indoor caving park — over a kilometre of cave passages plus a giant maze cave — inside the Six Elements complex in Pazhou. One ticket covers 2.5 hours of caving plus a castle quest game across five zones: the Experience Zone (15 minutes, climb through the elf tree trunk, cross the high rope net and push through an ice world of snow blocks and stalactite columns), the Maze Zone (25–35 minutes, a karst labyrinth of stalactites, rubble and rope nets — watch out for the skeleton pit), the Science Zone (15–25 minutes, a Jurassic fossil world where kids dig for dinosaur, fish and trilobite fossils and hunt for a hidden treasure chest), the Magic Zone (20–25 minutes, a kaleidoscope ocean-ball mirror house and double-decker secret rooms) and the Challenge Zone (three routes: the 77-metre Ghost Cave Trail, the high-difficulty Extreme Challenge with slanted boards and yoga-ball chambers, and the Wind Cave where you push against winds from level 3 to level 7). A competition zone opens when matches are running.
