A proper outdoorsy family escape in Guangzhou's Conghua hills: the **Tianrenshanshui Valley Camp** （天人山水度假区·溪谷营地） Double-11 promo packs one night in your choice of **sailing tent, safari tent, Chinese-style RV (bunk beds) or wooden cabin**, plus breakfast and a proper dinner for two (fish-head hotpot with Xufu braised rice at 清集市）, **Earth Art Park tickets for 2 adults + 1 kid, a Shaxi reservoir sightseeing cruise and the shuttle bunny cart**. Mornings come with free mindfulness exercise (yoga / baduanjin), middays with a free sound-bowl meditation session, and **weekends and holidays add a nature-study activity (3 options, for 2 adults + 1 kid)**.

The camp itself is half the reason to go: **260,000㎡ of valley floor with 157 standalone units in 20 different formats**, threaded between century-old lychee trees and mountain streams. The four formats in this deal are all properly plumbed — private bathroom, air-conditioning and a mini-fridge, no shared-shower camping compromises. The sailing tent and safari tent （满庭欢） are both 45㎡ with two 1.2m beds; the Chinese-style RV is the compact adventure pick at 21㎡ (two 0.9m beds plus a bunk, which kids fight over); the 瑞龙吟 wooden-cabin courtyard is 45㎡ with two 1.2m beds and its own little yard. Beyond the camp, the wider Tianrenshanshui park is a designed landscape worth the walk: the Dengke Bridge woven from 8,040 bamboo poles, the 121-meter Longmen Feidu bridge over the water, Huadai Bay's 500-plus flower varieties rotating by season, boardwalks along Qingxi Valley, and egrets over the Shaxi reservoir. Dozens of Chinese and foreign landscape designers worked on the gardens, and it shows — this is a "poetic dwelling" art park that happens to let you sleep in it.
