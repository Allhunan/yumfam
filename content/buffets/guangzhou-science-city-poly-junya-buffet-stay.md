Poly Junya Guangzhou Science City （广州科学城保利郡雅酒店， 99 Lanyue Road, Huangpu) is a 30,000㎡ hotel wrapped in greenery next to the Baiyun ecological reserve — guests keep describing it as a "city forest," and the rooms (36–40㎡, floors 5–12, King Koil mattresses) face the green. The bundle is built around the **Moonlight Western Restaurant**, a garden-style buffet room on the ground floor: on buffet nights the theme is BBQ and beer, with **sashimi, freshly grilled oysters, skewers, free-flow beer, unlimited salmon — and unlimited Häagen-Dazs**. The hotel says the two buffet meals alone are worth ¥700+, which checks out against local pricing (a 2-adult-1-kid buffet dinner here normally runs about ¥338 on its own).

Between meals, the stay covers the **heated indoor pool and gym**, the **kids' playground** and the hotel's leafy "forest-oxygen back garden." Getting there is painless: metro lines 6, 7 and 21 are each about a 10-minute walk, Tianhe's business district is ~20 minutes by car, and Xiangxue Park and the South China Botanical Garden make easy add-on outings.

⚠️ **Read the calendar before you book:** the two-buffet tiers (¥539/¥588) are usable on **Fridays and Saturdays only** — that's when the buffet dinner runs (plus public holidays); the ¥518 tier works any day but includes breakfast only. The hotel also warns the deal **may not apply during the autumn Canton Fair period**, so check the booking calendar if your dates fall in mid-October to early November. Reserve by 16:00 on the day of your stay; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00, and bring your voucher code, phone and ID.
