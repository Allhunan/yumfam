For its 20th anniversary, the **Xianglu International Hotel** （翔鹭国际大酒店， 18 Changhao Road, Huli) is running a **Southeast Asia Food Festival** at its Four Seasons Restaurant — "Nanyang flavours on a Xiamen table." The lineup is properly regional rather than generic "Asian fusion": **Singapore laksa, tom yum seafood, bak kut teh, Indonesian fried rice and gado-gado salad, Malaysian fried flower cakes, Indian chicken, curry-onion meatballs and satay patties**, with Thai green papaya salad and a sour-spicy beef vermicelli salad on the cold side.

The desserts are the Instagram trap, in a good way: a **seven-colour coconut sticky rice** (mango, dragon fruit, pandan, purple sweet potato, strawberry) and the signature **bubur cha cha** — coconut-milk dessert soup loaded with toppings, icy and silky. And during the festival, **every dining adult is served one hairy crab** on top of the buffet — autumn crab season colliding with tropical flavours. Each adult ticket also covers **one child under 1.2m free**, which makes the family maths hard to beat at this price.

⚠️ **Dates & booking:** lunch is **Saturday/Sunday only (11:30–14:00)**; dinner runs **Friday–Sunday (17:30–21:00)**. The festival vouchers are **not valid Oct 31, Dec 24 or Dec 25**.
