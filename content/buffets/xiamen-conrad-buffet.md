The Conrad sits in one of Xiamen's Twin Towers, and its buffet restaurant is on the 38th floor — the view is the point as much as the food, with the sea, Gulangyu and the Xiamen University campus spread out below the windows. Go at sunset if you can: the room is best in the last light. The food leans on the classics done well — salmon sashimi that guests say never runs out, oysters and lamb, a dessert counter guests single out — rather than sheer volume, and the room itself is on the small side, so the trade-off is intimacy against choice.

Seasonal extras: dinner includes hairy crab until December 14, and a winter hotpot joins the dinner spread from December 15 through January 31, which covers Christmas and the New Year season. Each adult can bring one child under 12 free, so a family of two adults and two children eats on the double tier.
