**Deal price: 1 adult + 1 child buffet lunch — ¥138** (≈$19, was ¥298, about 54% off) · Valid Nov 13 – Dec 30, 2026 · Not usable Dec 24–25 · Book by 23:00 the day before

**Xiamen Ruiyi Hotel （厦门瑞颐大酒店）** sits on Lujiang Road by the ferry terminal, a short walk from Zhongshan Road's shopping streets — easy to pair with a morning wander and an afternoon of eating. Its 2nd-floor café runs a buffet of **200+ dishes across 10 stations**: a seafood counter with freshly sliced salmon, tuna, Arctic surf clams, mussels and crab; carved roast beef and French lamb chops; a Japanese counter (sushi, sashimi, miso soup, oden); sha cha noodles, Cantonese roast meats, a teppanyaki station, dim sum, Western desserts, Häagen-Dazs ice cream, seasonal fruit and drinks. Lunch runs **12:00–14:00**.

The deal covers **1 adult + 1 child under 1.4 m**, and includes **3 hours in the kids' playground** on the day you dine — useful if the adults want a second, slower pass at the dessert counter. Unused vouchers are refundable, and expired ones refund automatically.

**Good to know**
- Book by 23:00 the day before; show the booking code at the restaurant — no advance phone booking needed
- Saturday, Sunday and public holidays carry a ¥30 per person top-up on site; a ¥80 on-site top-up upgrades lunch to the dinner buffet (dinner runs Friday–Sunday)
- The child in the deal is under 1.4 m; extra adults or children are charged at the restaurant's top-up rates
- Free parking for 3 hours
- Address: 2nd floor, Xiamen Ruiyi Hotel, No. 12 Lujiang Road, Siming District, Xiamen
