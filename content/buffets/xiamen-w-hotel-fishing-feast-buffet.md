A proper five-star-hotel seafood splurge at a fraction of the usual price. W Xiamen's **NINE restaurant** （五魁九里西餐厅， 5th floor, 1599 Luling Road, Siming) runs themed buffets that rotate with the season — the current German-themed rotation serves **hairy crabs and ring sausages as table-delivered specials** alongside the standing stations. The seafood counter piles up **big prawns, mussels, scallops and emerald snails**, with **garlic-grilled oysters** as the crowd favorite; sashimi is **cut to order** at the Japanese station next to a spread of sushi; and the live grill does steaks and German sausages with proper theatre — sizzling, aromatic, hard to walk past. Chinese and Western hot dishes, daily soups (including a cream pumpkin), salads and a full drinks bar (juices, sodas, coffee) round it out.

The desserts deserve their own sentence: W's pastry team folds Xiamen's own **博饼 (mooncake-gambling) dice culture** into the cakes and mousses, so the signature local festival game shows up on your plate — a playful touch no other Xiamen hotel buffet bothers with. And the family math is kind: **every voucher includes one child under 1.4m free per adult**, so two adults and two small kids eat on a double voucher.
