Le Méridien sits up the hill in Huli, and its weekend buffet voucher is the flexible kind: one voucher covers lunch or dinner, Saturday or Sunday, for one adult plus a child under 1.4 m. Dinner is the better value — it unlocks the teppanyaki herb lamb chops and a free-flow bar of signature cocktails, red and white wine, beer and soft drinks. The food runs the usual five-star buffet range — grilled oysters, sashimi, desserts and pastries guests single out — and there's a kids' play area, so children have somewhere to go between courses. The price already includes service charge, and parking is free for four hours.

**Packages**

- 1 adult + 1 kid (under 1.4 m) lunch-or-dinner voucher — **¥158** (≈$22, was ¥298): buffet lunch (12:00–14:30) or dinner (17:45–21:00) for 1 adult + 1 child under 1.4 m, Saturdays and Sundays; dinner includes teppanyaki herb lamb chops and free-flow signature cocktails, wine, beer and soft drinks
- 2 adults + 1 kid (under 1.4 m) lunch-or-dinner voucher — **¥310** (≈$43, was ¥498): same for 2 adults + 1 child under 1.4 m
- Kids' set menu (weekdays only) — **¥68** (≈$9, was ¥168): a children's set for one child — Western option (mini beef burgers, fries, fried chicken wings, ice cream, apple or orange juice) or Chinese option (abalone braised pork rice, stir-fried broccoli, garlic fried pork ribs, ice cream, juice); Monday–Friday only, valid to Dec 31, 2027

**Good to know**

- Weekend vouchers valid Nov 12, 2026 – Feb 28, 2027, Saturdays and Sundays only. Blackout dates: Dec 24 and 25, 2026, and Feb 5, 2027 (Chinese New Year's Eve). The kids' set menu is valid to Dec 31, 2027 but blacked out Jan 1–3, Feb 5–13, Apr 3–5, May 1–5, Jun 9, Sep 15 and Oct 1–7, 2027.
