**Deal price: ¥178** (≈$25, was ¥298 — 40% off) for 1 adult + 1 child · ⏰ Saturday evenings 17:30–21:00 · Valid September 12 – December 31, 2026 · Refundable if unbooked

A Saturday-night buffet built for the season: the headline **¥178** tier covers **1 adult + 1 child under 1.4 m** at the Renaissance Xiamen's dinner buffet, with **seasonal hairy crab, freshly sliced Hetian chicken, seafood sashimi and unlimited red and white wine** among the spreads. Menus rotate with the season, so the exact dishes follow what the restaurant is serving that night.

The Renaissance sits in Tong'an, on Xiamen's northern side — an easy taxi from the island and a good excuse to pair dinner with Tong'an's old-town streets or the nearby film-studio park. If Saturdays are your family dinner night, the **5-visit card (¥799, ≈$111 — about ¥160 per visit)** splits across separate Saturdays and works out cheaper than five single bookings.

**Packages**

- Saturday dinner buffet, 1 adult + 1 child (under 1.4 m) — **¥178** (≈$25, was ¥298)
- Saturday dinner buffet, 2 adults + 2 children (under 1.4 m) — **¥346** (≈$48, was ¥596)
- Saturday dinner buffet 5-visit card, 1 adult + 1 child per visit (visits can be split across Saturdays) — **¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1490)

**Good to know:** valid **Saturdays only**; book by **20:00 the day before**. Dinner runs **17:30–21:00**. One adult may bring one child under 1.4 m; extra children 1.2–1.4 m pay half price (¥149) at the restaurant and children over 1.4 m pay the adult price. Public holidays may carry an on-site surcharge — check with the hotel. Unbooked bookings are refundable; once booked, a single visit is non-refundable, and the 5-visit card cannot be part-refunded after use starts.
