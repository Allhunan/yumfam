**Deal price: ¥598** (≈$84, was ¥912) · ⏰ Valid Oct 24 – Dec 31, 2026 · 🦀 4 visits × (1 adult + 1 kid under 1.4m), lunch or dinner · 🎠 4 kids' playground tickets included · Refundable if unreserved

Four weekend feasts for the price of one-and-a-half: this **4-visit card** for **Xiamen Le Méridien's 新食谱 (Xinshipu) all-day restaurant** works out to just **¥149.50 per visit** — each visit covering **1 adult + 1 child under 1.4m** for either lunch or dinner. Every sitting comes with **hairy crab** (the seasonal headline), **iced seafood and salmon sashimi**, **all-you-can-eat crawfish and grilled oysters**, rotating **steak, chicken steak and fish**, Cantonese roast meats, hand-pulled noodles, the chef's signature pizza, plus free-flow **red/white wine, beer and soft drinks**. The dessert corner runs from cakes and candied hawthorn skewers to stir-fried ice, four-fruit soup and an ice-cream counter.

The restaurant itself is a bright ground-floor space wrapping an open kitchen, but the real family kicker is what comes bundled: **4 tickets to the hotel's hillside kids' playground** — a 600㎡ outdoor park with slides, swings, climbing ropes, trampolines and seesaws, a short stroll from the lobby via the hotel's boardwalk. One lunch, one long afternoon of kids burning it off, zero extra spend.

⚠️ **Fine print that matters:** usable **Saturdays and Sundays only**, excluding **Dec 24–25, 2026**. Reserve by **20:00 the day before**; unreserved bookings are refundable. Each visit admits 1 adult + 1 child **under 1.4m**. The 4 visits can be used any time within the validity window — they don't need to be consecutive weekends. Menus rotate seasonally; the exact lineup on your visit may differ from the photos.
