**Deal price: buffet for 1 adult + 1 child — ¥128** (≈$18, was ¥240, about 47% off) · Weekends only, lunch or dinner · Valid Oct 23, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Book by 19:00 the day before

**Xiamen Pan Pacific Hotel （厦门泛太平洋大酒店）** on Hubin North Road runs a weekend buffet at its Caesar's restaurant that covers the full spread: a seafood and sashimi counter (sashimi at dinner), a live teppanyaki grill, sushi and cold dishes, hot dishes from the hotel's chefs, a fresh noodle station, salads and a dessert counter. The buffet is lunch or dinner, Saturday or Sunday, and the chefs send out a surprise dish on weekend services.

The deal covers **1 adult + 1 child under 5 years old or under 1.3 m** for ¥128; **2 adults + 1 child** is ¥218 (was ¥240 per adult). Each adult can bring one free child under 5 (including 5-year-olds) or under 1.3 m. Note that menus rotate with the season, so the exact dishes vary — what is on the counter the day you go is what you get.

**Good to know**
- Weekends only (Saturday and Sunday), lunch or dinner — book by 19:00 the day before; show the booking code at the restaurant
- Children 1.3–1.4 m pay ¥99 on site; children 1.4 m and above pay the adult price
- Unbooked vouchers are refundable, and expired ones refund automatically
- Address: Xiamen Pan Pacific Hotel, No. 19 Hubin North Road, Siming District, Xiamen
