A Marriott-grade family dinner by **Qiandeng Lake** in Foshan's Nanhai district. The **Zhen Chu Western Restaurant** （臻厨西餐厅） sits on the 5th floor of the Lizhi Hui Plaza （丽致汇广场， 38 Haiba East Road) inside the Foshan Marriott — a European-castle-styled hotel that has become a landmark on the lake. The dinner buffet runs around **100 land-and-sea dishes**, and it leads with seafood: swimming crab, emerald snails, flower snails, mussels, scallops and big prawns laid out by the plate, plus a **lobster course served to your table**. Around that core there's a sashimi and sushi counter, a long run of hot dishes, and a dessert-and-drinks finish. The value jumps on the 2-adult-2-kid bundle, which prices a family of four's dinner at barely more than one adult's door price elsewhere.

Getting there is part of the draw: the hotel is about **800 meters from Financial High-Tech Zone station** on the Guangfo metro line, and roughly an hour's drive from central Guangzhou — close enough for a school-night treat or a Sunday family dinner without a road trip.
