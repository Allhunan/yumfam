**Deal price: ¥128 (≈$18) for 1 adult + 1 child** (was ¥188) · 2 adults + 1 child ¥236 (was ¥396) · Lunch or dinner, weekdays and weekends, no top-up · Valid Oct 23, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Book by 19:00 the day before · Unused vouchers are refundable

**Haitianlou （海天楼）** is the Cantonese restaurant on the 4th floor of the Pan Pacific Xiamen, and this is its all-you-can-eat dim sum deal: not a buffet line but order-as-you-like — shrimp dumplings, xiao long bao, lava buns, cheung fun, fresh shrimp spring rolls, mochi, durian puffs, ginger chicken and the rest of the trolley, brought to the table as you order. Lunch runs 11:30–14:30 and dinner 17:30–21:30, every day of the week, and the price is the same on weekends — no top-up. With 259 reviews in the hotel's pool on the platform, it is one of the best-reviewed dim sum deals in Xiamen.

**Packages**

- 1 adult + 1 child — **¥128** (≈$18, was ¥188): lunch or dinner, weekdays and weekends
- 2 adults + 1 child — **¥236** (≈$33, was ¥396): lunch or dinner, weekdays and weekends; can't be split across visits
- "1 child" means a child under 5 years old (5 included) or under 1.2 m tall; children 1.2–1.4 m pay half the on-site child price, children 1.4 m and up pay the adult price

**Good to know**

- Reserve at least a day ahead (by 19:00 the day before) — the hotel asks you to call to book, and the restaurant gets busy at mealtimes.
- Not usable on Chinese New Year's Eve or the first three days of Chinese New Year; a ¥10 per-person top-up applies on other public holidays.
- The menu rotates with the season, so the dishes on the day are whatever the kitchen is serving — the dim sum above is what guests keep naming in reviews.
- The hotel is a short walk from Bailuzhou Park and the metro, which makes it an easy lunch stop on a family day out.
- Unused and unbooked vouchers can be refunded, and expire unused automatically after the validity period.
