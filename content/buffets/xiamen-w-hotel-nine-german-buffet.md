**Deal price: 1 adult buffet lunch, Fri–Sun — ¥158** (≈$22, was ¥268, about 41% off) · Valid Nov 7 – Dec 31, 2026 · Each adult brings 1 child under 1.4 m free · Refundable if unreserved, auto-refund on expiry

**NINE restaurant at W Xiamen** is marking the hotel's 5th anniversary with a buffet deal built around a rotating food theme — and right now that theme is **German food, running through November 30**. Expect the live grill station (steak, German sausages), a seafood spread (big prawns, mussels, scallops, oysters — the garlic-grilled oysters get singled out), sashimi and sushi cut to order, Chinese and Western hot dishes, fresh soups, and a dessert table that riffs on Xiamen's dice-game (博饼) tradition. Dinner adds hairy crab and the house "circle sausage", and an autumn/winter hotpot option has just joined the menu.

The theme rotates — past editions have been Thai, Sichuan and Mexican — so the German spread is the draw if you go in November, and the menu will change with the season after that. Lunch runs 12:00–14:00, Friday to Sunday; dinner runs Thursday to Sunday. Every adult can bring one child under 1.4 m free, which makes this one of the better-value five-star buffets in Xiamen for a family lunch.

**Packages**
- 1 adult buffet lunch, Friday–Sunday (free: 1 child under 1.4 m per adult) — **¥158** (≈$22, was ¥268)
- 1 adult buffet dinner, Thursday–Sunday (free: 1 child under 1.4 m per adult) — **¥178** (≈$25, was ¥328)

**Good to know**
- Book by 20:00 the day before use; unreserved vouchers refund anytime and auto-refund after expiry
- Dinner is not available on Dec 24–25, 2026
- Extra children: under 1.4 m at half the door price, 1.4 m and above at the adult price (paid on site)
- Dishes change with the theme and the season — what is served on the day is what the kitchen puts out
- Address: 5th floor, W Xiamen, 1599 Luling Road, Siming District, Xiamen

## What guests say

45 merchant reviews for this restaurant on the booking platform. Guests keep coming back for the rotating themes — a note on the quotes below: they describe earlier themes, not the current German one, which is exactly how the restaurant works.

*"The five-star service and setting held their usual standard — the whole experience was superb. With a Michelin-starred chef in charge, the Sichuan theme really brought Chongqing and Sichuan to the table. Thoughtful service, very satisfied."* — May 2026

*"We came in June for the Thai theme. The room is beautiful, the range is generous — curry chicken soup and sour-spicy tom yum, plus a fresh coconut per person. A family of four ate well and happily; we'll come back to try another theme."* — August 2026
