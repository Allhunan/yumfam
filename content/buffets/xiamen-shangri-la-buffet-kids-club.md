Shangri-La's Guanyinshan hotel pairs its buffet with the thing that makes it a family deal rather than just a cheap lunch: the kids' club. The base tier covers one adult and one child under 6 for the buffet plus four hours of play in the club on the same day — the club has its own staff who run the games, so parents get an actual meal while the kids are looked after. The 2-adult tier stretches the free-child age to under 12 (with two hours of play instead of four), and the 5 + 5 tiers can be split across separate visits, which makes them the practical pick for a family gathering or two families going together.

The buffet itself is the usual five-star spread — seafood, a carving station, desserts — and the menu rotates with the season, so the exact dishes change with what's in season. Lunch runs 11:30–14:00 and dinner 17:30–21:00.
