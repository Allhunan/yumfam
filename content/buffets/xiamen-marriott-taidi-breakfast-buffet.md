Xiamen Marriott Haicang (厦门泰地万豪酒店) sits on Haicang Avenue in the Haicang CBD, across the water from Xiamen Island. Its ground-floor Gongchu restaurant serves a full breakfast buffet every morning: hot dishes cooked several ways, freshly steamed dim sum and mixed-grain items, crispy youtiao (great dunked in soy milk or noodle soup), a bread corner with European-style loaves, toast and scones, plus congee, soy milk, milk and noodle soup — Chinese and Western tastes both covered, in a bright, spacious dining room with an open kitchen.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Reserve at least a day ahead (call the hotel's western restaurant to book) and show your booking code before dining. Each adult may bring one child under 6 free; children aged 6–12 pay half the walk-in price, and children over 12 pay the adult price. Dine-in only, no takeaway. Unreserved vouchers are refundable anytime. Free parking in the hotel's underground car park. Address: 839 Haicang Avenue, Haicang District, Xiamen.
