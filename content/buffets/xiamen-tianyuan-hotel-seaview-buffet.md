Ask Xiamen locals which hotel buffet punches above its price and the **Tianyuan Hotel** （厦门天元酒店） keeps coming up. Its Taste all-day restaurant (1F, 1813 Huandao East Road, in the AVIC Zijin Plaza by the convention center) runs nearly ten stations with two claims to fame: **salmon sashimi and pan-seared foie gras, both unlimited** — the foie gras seared crisp outside, molten inside, finished with a sweet-tart blueberry sauce. Add fresh-cut sashimi (salmon, sea bream, vinegared mackerel), grilled steak and squid skewers on the teppanyaki, Turkish kebab you slice yourself and wrap in flatbread, roast duck, Minnan specialties like 土笋冻 (sea-worm jelly), a made-to-order noodle bar with chicken or satay broth, and a dessert counter anchored by the hotel's **signature cheesecake** (one or two varieties daily, famously not too sweet), Black Forest cake and crème brûlée. Dinner adds red wine and a seasonal served dish for adults.

The family centerpiece is the **live-seafood hot pot**: every table gets its own mini pot (clear or spicy broth), and a long tank holds 10+ kinds of live seafood — crabs, prawns, clams, scallops — that you pick and cook yourself. The room itself is a pleasure: lake-blue tones, Nanyang tiles and Minnan arcade motifs, floor-to-ceiling glass looking over Huandao Road toward the sea — on a clear day you can see the Kinmen islands from the upper floors. The hotel has been a designated venue of the Golden Rooster & Hundred Flowers Film Festival five years running, and after lunch the beach is right across the road for a walk.
