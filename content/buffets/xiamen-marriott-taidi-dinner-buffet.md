Xiamen Marriott Haicang (厦门泰地万豪酒店) sits on Haicang Avenue in the Haicang CBD, across the water from Xiamen Island. Its ground-floor Gongchu buffet restaurant runs an open kitchen serving a rotating seasonal spread: a seafood section with live-caught steamed seafood and crayfish, a sashimi and Japanese counter, a hot-dish range including fragrant clay-pot (啫啫煲) dishes, and a dessert station — with the menu changing by season, so the day's actual dishes are what the restaurant serves that evening.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Reserve at least a day ahead by 20:00 (call the hotel's western restaurant to book) and show your booking code before dining. Each adult may bring one child under 1.2 m free; children 1.2–1.4 m pay half the walk-in price, and children over 1.4 m pay the adult price. Dine-in only, no takeaway. Unreserved vouchers are refundable anytime. Free parking in the hotel's underground car park. Address: 839 Haicang Avenue, Haicang District, Xiamen.
