**Last updated: September 30, 2026**

YumFam (yumfam.com) is a deals listing site. We keep data collection to an absolute minimum.

## What we collect

**Almost nothing.** We do not require accounts, and we do not ask for your name, email, or payment details. Booking and payment happen on the partner platform's site, not ours — we never see or store that information.

What may be collected automatically:

- **Basic analytics:** We may use privacy-friendly analytics to see which pages are popular (page views, approximate region). This data is aggregated and never tied to an individual.
- **Server logs:** Like every website, our host (Cloudflare) keeps standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and performance. These are retained per Cloudflare's policies.

## Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies of our own. Embedded content (such as product images loaded from our partner platform) may be subject to that platform's cookie policy.

## Affiliate links

Deal buttons on this site are affiliate links. If you book through them, our partner platform may record that the visit came from YumFam so they can credit us a commission. This does not change the price you pay. We disclose this because transparency matters — see our [About page](/about/) for more on how YumFam works.

## Third-party sites

Clicking a deal takes you to our partner's booking platform, which has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for how they handle your data once you leave yumfam.com.

## Children's privacy

Our content is family-oriented, but the site itself is intended for parents and adult travelers. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.
