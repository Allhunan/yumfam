A note on the four famous resorts first: we searched the booking platform for standalone ski products at Chongli, Yabuli, Beidahu and Altay — none exists there. Outdoor skiing on the platform comes as guided multi-day family tours instead, and the Keshiketeng tour listed above already opens with a day of ski lessons in Chongli. The tours below are the ski-inclusive ones we verified on sale on November 17, 2026; the Changbaishan tour was verified on sale on November 18, 2026, the morning it opened for booking. Spring Festival week (February 5–13, 2027) is peak season — expect crowds and book early; the Spring Festival departures printed on the pages are listed with each tour. The two tours with confirmed Spring Festival departures — Shennongjia and Yichun — are compared side by side on our [Spring Festival ski tours page](/spring-festival-ski-tours-2027/). There is no departure over the New Year holiday (January 1–3) on any of them. Helmets are included on the Yichun and Northeast tours below and mandatory throughout on the Yichun Lesser Khingan tour; neither the Shennongjia nor the Changbaishan page mentions helmets, so bring your own or check with customer service.

**2026–27 opening dates, checked November 20, 2026:** Koktokay opened October 20; Wanlong October 23; Yabuli November 6; Beidahu November 13; Genting in early November (November 6 per the local schedule); Changbaishan Huamei Shengdi November 14; Lake Songhua around November 20; Changbaishan Wanda opens November 21.
