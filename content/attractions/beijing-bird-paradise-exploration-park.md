Opened in September 2026 inside Wenyu River Park's Chaoyang demonstration area, the Bird Paradise Exploration Park blends native forest, bird shows, petting animals and wooden playgrounds into one family day out. The centrepiece is the parrot stunt show — trained parrots ride tiny bicycles, play basketball and bowl, presented by a team with 20+ years of bird training — followed by close-up feeding and photos with macaws and cockatoos. The petting farm has alpacas, raccoons, sika deer and rabbits to feed under keeper supervision, and the forest playgrounds are all wooden and unpowered: a giant grasshopper slide and butterfly climbing frame for ages 3–6, a shallow stream area for splashing and catching fish, and a giant bird-nest climbing tower with rope bridges and spiral slides for ages 6+.
