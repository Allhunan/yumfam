**Deal price: unlimited intermediate/advanced skiing for two + hot spring visit for two — ¥999** (≈$141, was ¥1,396, about 28% off) · Valid Nov 3 – Dec 31, 2026 · Book one day ahead

**Yaoxue Snow World （耀雪冰雪世界）** in Lingang is a 98,000㎡ indoor snow park held at −3 to −5°C all year — real snow, indoors, in Shanghai. There are five slopes: two beginner runs (the green run and the practice slope) plus blue, red and black runs for intermediate and advanced skiers, with a magic carpet and an indoor cable car. The headline ¥999 ticket covers unlimited time on the intermediate/advanced slopes for two people, with one gear rental each (ski suit, skis or snowboard, boots, poles and helmet, no deposit), plus a hot spring visit for two at the Crowne Plaza hotel next door (bring swimwear).

Two more Double 11 options on the same page: a beginner package at **¥720** (≈$101, was ¥1,320) — two 3-hour beginner-slope visits for one person, or one visit for two, with a 1-hour beginner lesson; and a 5-visit card at **¥1,699** (≈$239, was ¥2,696) — five 4-hour intermediate/advanced visits plus two hot spring visits.

**Good to know**
- Book at least one day ahead (by 23:57 the day before); tickets are real-name and you enter with your ID — both people on the two-person ticket enter together
- Ski hours in November: 10:00–20:00 daily; enter the intermediate/advanced slopes by 16:00 (4 hours before closing)
- Under-18s must be accompanied by a ticket-holding adult (one adult per child); ages 6–60 on the beginner package
- Unused tickets are refundable, and expired orders auto-refund
- Address: 2088 Hucheng Ring Road, Lingang, Pudong New Area, Shanghai
