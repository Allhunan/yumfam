Bounce Town (弹力小镇) in Jiading is a 4,000-square-metre trampoline park that plays more like a whole indoor playground: rows of trampolines, a near-vertical giant slide and gentler wave slides for younger kids, a high-altitude walkway, go-karts, plum-blossom poles, archery and shooting, a climbing net and a rock wall — plus a 300-square-metre soft-play toddler zone on the second floor with a pretend supermarket. One ticket covers the whole day with unlimited replays, and parking is free for 4 hours.

Practical notes: open Tuesday to Sunday, 10:00–20:30 (closed Mondays). Buy and use on the spot with your voucher code; unused vouchers are refundable any time. Everyone entering needs grip socks — bring your own or buy a pair at the front desk for ¥10. One wristband per person, checked at entry, and wristbands cannot be swapped mid-visit. The family ticket covers 1 adult + 1 child under 1.4 m. Address: Bounce Town Trampoline Park, 1018 Huiping Road, Jiading District, Shanghai.
