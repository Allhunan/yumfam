**Deal price: child play pass — ¥99** (≈$14, was ¥228) · Adult pass ¥168 · Valid September 1 – December 20, 2026 · Buy and use the same day

**Wind Valley （风之谷）** is 3,000 mu of open grassland in the hills of Lin'an, about 90 minutes' drive from central Hangzhou — the place guests keep calling "Hangzhou's Altay". One pass covers entry plus the **grassland train and mini train** ("the train to autumn") and **10+ rides**: the jungle flyer, New Zealand scooter, rainbow slide, bamboo raft, grass flying, bumper cars, tank battle and more. Two rides — the sky flyer and the super slide — need riders 1.4 m and up. Tents dot the grassland if you want to slow down between rides, and the site runs interactive activities through the day.

**Packages**
- Child play pass (1.1 m – under 1.4 m) — **¥99** (≈$14, was ¥228)
- Adult play pass (1.4 m and up) — **¥168** (≈$24, was ¥228)
- Child play pass + BBQ set (book the BBQ in advance) — **¥149** (≈$21, was ¥228)

**Good to know**
- Open 8:30–17:00, last entry 15:30. Swap your voucher for a ticket at the ticket office first (bring the QR code and the booking text message), then enter.
- Children under 1.1 m enter free; children 1.1–1.4 m need a child ticket and must be accompanied by a ticket-holding adult; anyone 1.4 m and up needs an adult ticket. Children under 16 must be accompanied by an adult throughout.
- Rides open depending on weather, temperature and season — check conditions before you go, and expect queues at peak times. Not included: the water sports centre, go-karts and horse riding, which are paid separately. Unused vouchers are refundable any time before use. Address: Wind Valley, Shuangta Village, Changhua Town, Lin'an District, Hangzhou, Zhejiang.
