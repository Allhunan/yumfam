**Deal price: single ticket with 20 activities + 4 rides — ¥39.90** (≈$6, was ¥128, about 69% off) · Valid Nov 10 – Dec 31, 2026 · Any day of the week · Refundable if unused

**Luyewan Forest Ranch （山里人家·鹿野湾森林牧场）** is a forest playground in Xiaoshan, Hangzhou — the kind of place where a family can easily spend 4–6 hours. One ticket covers **20 included activities**: the lakeside swing, petting farm, climbing nets and frames, trampolines, pink sand beach, rope courses, tree slides, seesaws, a mini roller coaster, rocking horse, food plaza and more. On top of that you pick **any 4 of 8 rides**: archery, the mini train, the little bee ride, the sightseeing boat, beach buggies, the rainbow slide, go-karts, and the jungle crossing.

One adult can bring **1 child under 1 m free**. Buy by 16:00 on the day you visit and exchange the code for a ticket at the gate; the ranch opens 09:00–17:00 on weekdays (last entry 16:00) and 08:30–17:30 on weekends.

**Good to know**
- The rainbow slide is limited to ages 10+ and 40–80 kg; children under 1.2 m can't ride the off-road buggy alone, and under-2s can't ride go-karts
- Feeding the animals costs extra for feed (¥30 a portion); some facilities may close in bad weather or for maintenance
- Max 5 tickets per phone number / per account for the whole sale period
- Unused tickets are refundable anytime; partly used orders can't be refunded, so split orders if families want to use tickets separately
- Address: No. 17 Dawan Road, Xiaoshan District, Hangzhou
