**Lingling International Circus City （灵玲国际马戏城）** in Jimei is Xiamen's big family day out: a zoo and a world-class circus in one park. The Animal Kingdom is home to giant pandas, giraffes, brown bears, white tigers, alpacas, parrots and ring-tailed lemurs — the animals head indoors at 17:00, so go earlier in the day. The circus show, *Forest Secrets*, plays in a Guinness-record big-top theatre with a water-and-land dual stage, fountains and fire effects, and the Kenyan Death Wheel act, newly upgraded from 4 wheels to 6 and raised to 17 metres. The ¥159 ticket covers the circus show and includes same-day entry to the Animal Kingdom, so one ticket does the whole park.

**Good to know**
- On sale Nov 7–20, 2026 (sale dates as listed); book at least one day ahead, then use on any day up to Dec 31, 2026, including public holidays
- Children under 1 m enter free with a paying adult (one free child per paying adult); tickets are single-entry and all tickets on one order must enter together
- Animal Kingdom hours 10:00–16:30; the animals go off show at 17:00 — note the night circus show does not include zoo entry
- Unused tickets are refundable, and expired orders auto-refund
- Address: No. 366 Xingjin Road, Xinglin, Jimei District, Xiamen
