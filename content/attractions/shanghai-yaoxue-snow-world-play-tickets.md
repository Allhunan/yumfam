**Deal price: snow-play child ticket — ¥129** (≈$18, was ¥248, about 48% off) · Double 11 pricing, on sale now · Gear rental included

**Yaoxue Snow World （耀雪冰雪世界）** is a 98,000㎡ indoor snow park in Lingang, Shanghai — real snow, indoors, all year round. The Nordic-themed play zone packs in **10+ snow-play activities**: the Snow Country Train, snow gliders, snow-house building, ice-cave exploring, snow climbing, speed sleds, ice bikes, the seven-colour snow village, space balls and more. The third floor suits younger kids (snow-house building, snow village, space ball), while the fifth-floor Base Camp is where the faster rides live — take the Snow Country Train up.

Every ticket includes a **single gear rental (snow suit + snow shoes), no deposit needed**, and once you are through the gate there is no time limit on play that day. The child ticket (¥129) and the pair ticket (¥358) also include a **hot-spring ticket** for the hotel's 3rd- and 5th-floor hot springs — bring swimwear, and a ticketed adult must accompany children.

**Good to know**
- Snow play hours: 10:00–16:00 on weekdays (last entry 15:00), 10:00–17:00 on weekends (last entry 18:00); hot springs 13:00–23:00 on weekdays
- The child ticket is for ages 3–11 and needs a ticketed adult along; the pair ticket is same price for adults and children
- The single ticket + ice bumper car option (¥199, was ¥290) adds one 10-minute bumper-car ride on the champion ice rink
- Dress warm: bring a hat, gloves and a mask — it is properly cold inside, and the rented suit and trousers have no pockets, so bring a waterproof pouch for your phone
- Buy by 23:57 the day before; tickets are for the date you pick and are refundable if unused
- Address: 1–5, Lane 2088, Hucheng Ring Road, Nanhui New Town, Pudong New Area, Shanghai
