**Changtai Banyue Mountain Hot Spring Hotel （长泰半月山温泉酒店）** sits in a forested valley where Xiamen, Zhangzhou and Quanzhou meet — about an hour from Xiamen, and a Fujian Top 20 family resort for 2025. The water is the draw: a **self-flowing strontium soda spring** emerging at 58 °C, mildly alkaline and rich in metasilicic acid (53.8 mg/L). The **¥659 package** covers a 50㎡ residence room (king with one 1.8 m bed, or twin with two 1.35 m beds) with its **own private soaking pool on the balcony**, **breakfast for 2 adults + 2 children** (one under 1.4 m, one under 1.2 m) served until **12:30**, and **hot-spring park tickets for all four** with unlimited soaks in **50+ pools** during your stay. The pools are open 13:00–23:00.

Longer and larger options: **¥1,299** (≈$183, was ¥2,688) for 2 nights in the same room type (or 2 rooms for 1 night); **¥759** (≈$107) for 1 night in the tatami private-pool family room (1.8 m bed + 1.2 m tatami bed), or **¥1,499** (≈$211, was ¥2,999) for 2 nights in it.
