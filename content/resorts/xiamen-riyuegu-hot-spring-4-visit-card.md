**Riyuegu Hot Spring Resort （日月谷温泉渡假村）** is Xiamen's old favourite — a 20-plus-year-old resort village that regulars describe as a slice of Bali tucked into the hills of Haicang. This card covers **4 visits**: each visit is one adult weekday hot-spring ticket plus one **Kids' Fun Palace** ticket, and each visit can bring **one child under 1.2 m free**. Use all four in one family day out, or spread them across the season — the ticket and the Fun Palace entry can't be split from each other, but the four visits are independent.

There are **100+ pools** scattered through the gardens — more than 40 themed springs, from the rose-petal pool to the wine spring, the mist pool hidden among the palms, and the big hydrotherapy pool at Hexinzhou. Beyond the soaking, the card throws in a generous bundle: four ¥10 dining vouchers, four ¥20 fish-therapy vouchers, the games room, a duck treasure hunt (13:00–16:00), a play session with the resident Alaskan dog (15:00–17:30), and a free 90-minute **singing-bowl and forest-sound therapy session** (14:30–16:00). The free shuttle runs from Dongfu Metro Station, so the trip from central Xiamen needs no car.
