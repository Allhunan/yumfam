Sleep next to the penguins. The **Tianjin Polar Ocean Hotel** sits right beside the Polar Ocean Park in Binhai New Area — the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region's ocean-themed family hotel — and the deal is a **polar-themed room for 2 adults + 1 child with breakfast and park tickets that allow unlimited re-entry over 2 days**. The park itself has 11 zones — Antarctic penguin island, a mysterious Arctic village, an undersea tunnel, a shark harbour, a coral sea, a beluga bay and a jellyfish hall — with penguins, polar bears, sea lions, walruses and seals. The hotel adds a kids' castle play fort, a "Barty magic" science class, a bedtime story session and its own VIP entrance into the park, so you skip the main queue.
