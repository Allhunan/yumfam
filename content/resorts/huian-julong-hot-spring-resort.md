Tucked inside the Julong eco-park in Huian, Quanzhou, the **Julong Hot Spring Park Hotel** （聚龙温泉公园酒店） is a new-Chinese-style garden hotel — Hui-style architecture, pavilions, covered corridors and streams running between the buildings — where the rooms sit directly above the spring park: walk downstairs and you're soaking. This one-price package covers **2 adults + 2 kids (under 1.4m)** for everything: a **private in-room hot-spring tub**, breakfast, **unlimited entries to the hot-spring park** for the whole stay, and the **Pinecone Tribe** kids' play zone — plus a free in-resort shuttle and free parking. Weekends carry **no surcharge**.

The park is the main event: its water is **metasilicic fluorinated spring water drawn from rock strata 1,200 meters underground**, spread across **30-plus pools in seven zones** — a kids' splash zone, a Chinese-herbal medicine zone (pools steeped with traditionally prepared herbs), a flower-petal zone, a warm stone-slab zone, a hydrotherapy pool, and quieter corners for a slow evening soak. Your room choice is between the Apartment Hot-Spring Twin (35㎡, two 1.2m beds, floors 2–4) and the larger Deluxe Hot-Spring Twin (40㎡, two 1.25m beds) — both with their own private soaking tub, so the last soak of the night happens in your room, at your temperature, after the kids are down.

⚠️ **Extra-kid math, in writing:** the package's "2 kids" means two children under 1.4m. An additional child pays at the venue: breakfast ¥38 (under 1.4m) or ¥48 (1.4m and above), hot-spring park ¥88 per person (same price for adults), Pinecone Tribe ¥38 (under 1.4m) or ¥10 (1.4m+). Book by **23:59 the day before**; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Unused package items aren't refunded piecemeal — it's a bundle price.
