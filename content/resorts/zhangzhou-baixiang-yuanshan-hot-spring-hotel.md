The water is the hotel's signature: an **ancient-seawater flower spring at 23°N latitude**, spread through a **30,000㎡ garden spring park with 20-plus outdoor pools** and 16 spring "check-in" experiences among the flowers. Location is the other half of the appeal — Zhangzhou's old town, the science museum and the library are all nearby, so the trip mixes soaking with actual sightseeing instead of resort captivity.

The rooms in this deal are properly big. The **family room starts at 65㎡** (floors 5–17) with a 2-meter king plus a 1.35m double, a sofa, a big TV and even a small kitchenette corner with Chinese tea set laid out. The **kids room starts at 67㎡** with floor-to-ceiling river views, an in-room play tent and a building-block table — and the parent-proofing details are already done: padded table corners, safety socket covers, cartoon touches throughout. Either room comes with **three buffet breakfasts** at the Yuanshanpan western restaurant, your choice of a **Hong Kong-style dim sum set for two at Baohuixuan** or a **buffet lunch for two**, and **three single-entry tickets to the Yuanshan Huaquan spring park**.
