The Songcheng Resort Hotel sits a short shuttle ride from Yangzhou's Slender West Lake （瘦西湖）, the city's signature willow-lined lake of pavilions and arched bridges. Rooms are 25–28㎡ view rooms (king or twin, no extra bed), and every package adds **welcome fruit, a couple photoshoot and a scenic-area shuttle**. The single-night bundle goes further: **Qunyuan morning-tea set for two** — Yangzhou's beloved breakfast-tea ritual — plus **two Slender West Lake daytime tickets and two hot-spring tickets**, so one night covers the lake by day and the springs after.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Reserve by 17:00 the day before use. Refundable if unreserved. The two-night package is one room for two consecutive nights, or two rooms for one night — same price. Self-service laundry is included.
