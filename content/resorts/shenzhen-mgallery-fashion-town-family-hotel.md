Accor's MGallery hotel in Shenzhen's Dalang Fashion Town — a new, art-led five-star where the rooms themselves are the attraction for kids. The themed family room is randomly assigned one of five themes (Alice AR Wonderland, Balala Little Demon Fairy, Milk Dragon, Chibi Maruko or Super Wings), so each stay is a surprise. All tiers include breakfast for two and unlimited play at the hotel's Kirin Cat children's park, and the 2-night tier adds a greenway hiking energy pack (2 fruit-and-vegetable juices, 2 waters and 2 sandwiches) plus 2 tickets to the Yizhihui Centennial Fashion Museum next door.

Practical notes: check-in from 15:00, check-out by 12:00. Book by 23:59 the day before; rooms are first-come, first-served, and unreserved vouchers can be refunded. December 24–25 costs ¥200 extra per night. A suite upgrade is +¥500 per night (the themed room setup is not kept after upgrading). On the dinner tier, dinner covers 2 adults plus 1 child under 1.2 m; children 1.2–1.4 m pay half the walk-in price on site and over 1.4 m pay the adult price. All guests must show valid ID at check-in (children with their household registration booklet). Address: MGallery Shenzhen (Accor), 8 Langyun Road, Longhua District, Shenzhen, Guangdong.
