**Deal price: 1 ticket ¥98** (≈$14, was ¥188) · Usable Dec 4, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Blackout Jan 1–2 & Feb 5–13, 2027 · Refundable if unused

A hot spring in the forest without leaving Shanghai. The **Sun Island Forest Hot Spring** sits on a 2,500-mu island in Zhujiajiao — the resort area is a cluster of villas on its own island, about 1.5 hours from central Shanghai — and the deal is an early-bird ticket to its **"Forest Aroma Spring · Healing Time" package**: 8 outdoor pools at 40°C+, energy sweat rooms, a short massage or craft session, a bottle of custom essential oil to take home and a Chinese tea break. The pools are set among trees and lakeside paths, and the spa lends hanfu-style robes, so the whole visit feels like a walk through a classical Chinese painting.

**What the ticket includes**

- **Quiet forest hot spring** — an outdoor infinity hot pool, a bubble power pool, 3 forest pools, 2 lakeside pools, a waterfall pool, 3 indoor Chinese herbal tubs, 3 indoor family pools and a foot-bath pool
- **Energy sweat rooms** — a salt-heat palace and a jade room, plus other herbal steam rooms
- **One activity** — a 30-minute Chinese partial-body massage (book on site) or one DIY bead-craft session
- **A 5 ml bottle of custom energy essential oil** to take home
- **A tea break** — a health drink plus red-bean dessert with tangerine peel (weekdays 15:00–16:00; weekends 14:00–16:00 and 19:00–20:00)

**Good to know:** The ticket covers entry to the Sun Island resort area as well. Hours are 13:00–22:00 on weekdays and 10:00–22:00 on weekends and public holidays (last entry 20:30). Children under 1.2 m enter free, one per paying adult; children 1.2–1.5 m can use this same-price ticket. The spa provides a lounge, a cinema room, smart lockers, sweat robes, towels, bathrobes and Wi-Fi — bring your own swimwear, or buy it on site. Parking in the resort area is paid.
