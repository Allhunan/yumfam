**Qingshui Tianyi Hot Spring Resort** sits in the green mountains of Liancheng County, Longyan — about a 3-hour drive from Xiamen, and roughly 15 minutes from the 5A-rated Guan Zhai Mountain scenic area and the Peitian Hakka ancient village. The resort is built around a large semi-open-air hot-spring park themed on Hakka culture, with **50+ pools** — lavender, milk bath, fish therapy, herbal soaks — plus a spa house, an outdoor pool and a wave pool. Every room has natural hot-spring water piped in, with its own private soaking tub, so you can bathe without leaving the room.

The headline ¥299 tier is the Double-11 flash deal: a room in the annex Qingxianghe hotel (economy style, small private tub), breakfast for two, and unlimited hot-spring passes for two. The main building is the 5-star option with bigger private tubs, and there is a duplex family room with its own tub for families. One quirk to know before you go: the resort's water park is **closed from November** for the cold season — the hot-spring pools are the winter draw, not the slides.
