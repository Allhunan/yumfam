A villa stay rather than a hotel room: Shanhaiguan sits among the villas of the Banyue Mountain hot-spring area in Changtai District, Zhangzhou — the hot-spring country between Xiamen and Zhangzhou city. The entry tier is a Japanese-style villa room with a choice of three layouts (luxury king bed, tatami king bed, or a room with a bathtub), and every booking gets free Wi-Fi, parking and use of the villa's shared living room and kitchen — handy for families who want to cook or spread out beyond one room. Check-in is from 14:00, check-out by 12:00, and you show your booking voucher and ID at arrival.

The step-up tier adds the reason most guests come here: a private hot-spring pool in the room, unlimited soaking, plus a garden afternoon tea for two and a barbecue tool kit (bring your own ingredients). It runs ¥398 (≈$55, was ¥728) for one night, or ¥688 (≈$96, was ¥1,298) for two nights in one room or one night in two rooms. For a group, the whole-villa tier — a three-bedroom villa or a three-courtyard-house layout, sleeping a larger party — is ¥1,788 (≈$248, was ¥3,199) for one night with breakfast for six, private-spring soaking in the room and courtyard pool, afternoon tea for six and a mahjong machine.

Practical notes: surcharges are ¥100 per room-night on Saturdays and ¥300 per room-night over December 31 – January 2 (¥300/¥1,000 per whole villa respectively); using the courtyard pool in the villa garden costs ¥88 per room on site. Single-room tiers cannot be combined into a whole villa — book the villa tier for that.
