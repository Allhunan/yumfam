**Sujia Puyue** （宿家·璞悦温泉民宿， 2-4 Xinzhen Road, Xiang'an, Xiamen) is a villa stay built for group getaways: standalone three-bedroom villas inside a Suzhou-style garden estate in the Shoukai–Vanke Puyue Shan park — white walls, dark tiles, little bridges over water — about **45 minutes' drive from Xiamen island**, with Beichen Mountain (4A), the Tong'an Film City and Fangte within easy reach. Each villa is a small private resort of its own: a **private hot-spring pool on the basement level**, living room and kitchen on the ground floor, bedrooms (kids' room, king room, suite) across the upper floors, its own courtyard, **2 parking spots**, and an entertainment room — mahjong/cards or a KTV movie room, depending on tier. The bathrooms get the boutique treatment: dry-wet separation, double vanities, smart toilets. Behind the estate there's a private hillside leisure area with lawns, a campsite and a small kids' playground.

Every tier folds in the leisure kit: an **afternoon tea set**, a **farm fishing experience**, a **local specialty gift box** (pick it up at the farm), and a **hanfu dress-up experience** — the garden architecture makes the photos. The 2-night tier swaps the tea for a **BBQ tool kit** (grill, nets, brushes, trays — charcoal is ¥25/box extra and you bring your own ingredients) for courtyard grilling.

⚠️ **The window closes Oct 31, 2026** — and Saturdays carry a **+¥300/villa/night** surcharge (the Mid-Autumn and National Day uplifts are already past). Book by 23:59 the day before. One sizing note from the reviews below: the villas are charming rather than huge — two households fit comfortably; three households in one villa is a squeeze.
