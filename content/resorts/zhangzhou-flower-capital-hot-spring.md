In Zhangpu county, Zhangzhou — about an hour's drive from Xiamen — the **Southeast Flower Capital Hot Spring Hotel** sits beside the Flower Expo park, and this November bundle folds a villa-style stay, hot springs and two parks into one voucher. Every tier includes **1 night, buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.4m), a family or wellness set dinner (pick one), hot-spring entry, Flower Expo tickets and Snail Village tickets, all for 2A1C**. The set menus are proper Fujian cooking, not filler: the family set runs to pineapple sweet-and-sour fish, the house braised-pork sauce and crispy chicken wings; the wellness set swaps in a 片仔癀 (Pien Tze Huang) tofu claypot and milk-root steamed free-range chicken.

The springs are the headline act: rare **seawater hot springs** arranged as a terraced hillside complex of **60+ pools in five zones** — flower springs, a karst-style grotto spring, a vine-garden spring, zodiac pools and a functional area with stone-slab springs and steam rooms — plus a "Minnan village" of swallowtail-ridge houses on the way up. Rooms step up with the price: the 60㎡ Bamboo Dwelling comes with a **private in-room soaking pool and private garage** (drive in, check in without leaving the car), and the hillside villas add **private outdoor pools**, fishing and a head-therapy voucher.

⚠️ **Plan the logistics:** vouchers are usable only **Nov 1–30, 2026**, and you must reserve by **23:59 the day before** (check-in 15:00, out 12:00). Extra guests beyond the included 2A1C pay on site — breakfast ¥40, hot spring ¥68, Flower Expo ¥20, Snail Village ¥20 per person.

**Good to know:** the hotel spreads across a hillside, and guests consistently praise the on-call electric buggy shuttles that whisk you between rooms, springs and parks. One honest caveat from repeat reviewers: on-site dining beyond the included meals is limited — the nearest small restaurants are about 15 minutes away, so drivers may want to plan dinner accordingly.
