Snow in Guangzhou, minus the flight to Harbin. The **Sunac Huajiantang Yuexue** (69 Fenghuang North Road, Huadu) is a Lingnan-palace of a hotel — bronze, rosewood and white walls channeling an old Cantonese Xiguan mansion, 348 rooms and suites, a pool that looks out over the city and a gym walled in glass — sitting right beside **Sunac Snow Park （广州热雪奇迹）**, South China's biggest indoor ski hall. This fourth-generation indoor snow world holds a constant temperature all year at 23°N latitude: five runs of graded difficulty with a 66-meter vertical drop, gradients from 7.6° to 21°, snow kept about 50 cm deep, and a **460-meter beginner run billed as the world's longest indoor starter slope**, served by a double chairlift and five magic carpets. Beside the slopes, the snow-play world runs to the size of ten football fields — sledding, ice bumper cars, ice bikes and aerial walkways.

The package mechanics are the good kind of simple: the room comes with **buffet breakfast for two** and **two tickets to the snow park, each good for one entry with unlimited time inside** — no clock-watching, no session slots. Pick the ¥699 snow-play version for building snowmen and sledding with the kids, or ¥829 to swap the play tickets for **unlimited beginner-slope ski passes for two**.

⚠️ **Surcharges differ by tier:** on the ¥699 package, Friday and Saturday nights add **¥200/room** and public holidays **¥400/room**; on the ¥829 ski package it's **¥270** on Fri/Sat and **¥470** on holidays. Rooms are allocated first-come-first-served from limited promo stock, so reserve early — and note that once a reservation is confirmed, the room is held all day but becomes non-refundable in principle. Unreserved vouchers refund anytime.
