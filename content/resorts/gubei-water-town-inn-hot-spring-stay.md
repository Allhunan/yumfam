Sleep inside **Gubei Water Town**, the canal-laced old town at the foot of the **Simatai Great Wall** in Beijing's Miyun District (Simatai Village, Gubeikou — 2 km north of the Simatai exit on the Jingcheng Expressway). The interesting quirk: this is a **multi-inn voucher**. You don't pick a specific inn — you're assigned one from a pool of courtyard inns, most inside the scenic area （雁门， 洗尘， 古寨， 得月楼， 英华， 永顺， 邻家 — expect a 1.5 m king room or a traditional heated **kang** bed room), a few just outside the gates （木兰， 镶红， 纳兰）. Guests rate the lottery well: these are small, host-run inns woven into the town's lanes, and the bundle around whichever you draw is the same.

And the bundle is generous: **2 scenic-area tickets with unlimited re-entry**, **unlimited in-town shuttle rides**, **2 hot-spring bath tickets** (Yu Da, Yu Dao or Chengshang bathhouses — whichever is open that day), **breakfast for two**, and one hands-on **DIY experience** (paint a white-blank craft, or assemble and color a shadow-puppet set). Staying inside the gates is what makes Gubei work: the lantern-lit canals after the day-trip buses leave, and the quiet morning before they arrive.
