**Deal price: hot-spring pool room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + fish-head-pot dinner for 2 + Earth Art Park entry + 3 two-day play wristbands — ¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,299) · Valid Nov 18 – Dec 30, 2026 · Any day of the week

This is the pool-room tier of Tianrenshanshui Valley Camp — a different package from the tent/RV/cabin stay already on this site. Here you pick one of three rooms, each with its own hot-spring pool: a courtyard pool room, a duplex RV pool room, or a wooden cabin tent with one bedroom and one living room. Breakfast for two and a fish-head-pot dinner for two (with Xufu stewed rice) are included, and the deal is built around two days in the park rather than one night in a room.

The play wristbands are the core of it: three wristbands, valid on your check-in day and the next day, covering the park's own rides and activities — rainbow slide, spinning chairs, the little train, kayaks, paddleboards, water bikes, jungle crossing, the magic net and the carousel — plus free shuttle carts, the reservoir cruise, the Earth Art Park and the non-powered playground at Nuonuo Manor. Mornings bring a free mindfulness yoga session and middays a free singing-bowl sound-therapy session, both by reservation. On Saturdays and Sundays the deal adds a mountain-therapy pick (2 of 2) and a mountain study activity for 2 adults + 1 child, and there is a Saturday-evening valley concert on the lawn stage.

**Packages**

- Hot-spring pool room, 1 night (courtyard pool room / duplex RV pool room / wooden cabin tent, 1 bedroom + 1 living room) — **¥699** (≈$97, was ¥1,299): breakfast for 2, fish-head-pot dinner for 2, Earth Art Park entry for 2 adults + 1 child, 3 two-day play wristbands, morning yoga and midday singing-bowl therapy (both by reservation), Saturday–Sunday extras as above

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 18 – Dec 30, 2026, any day of the week. Saturday nights carry a ¥200 per room per night top-up.
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00 the next day. A deposit is required at check-in (WeChat, Alipay or cash) and refunded in full at check-out if nothing is damaged.
- The play wristbands are valid on your check-in day and the next day; the rides open on the day are whatever the park posts on site.
- Park entry covers 2 adults + 1 child per room, plus 2 children under 1.2 m and one non-banned-breed dog free. Extra adults pay ¥69 for breakfast and children ¥35; dinner extras are charged on site.
- Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book. The voucher is usable as soon as it is issued; unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded, and once booked the room is generally non-refundable.
- Quiet hours after 22:30 in the camp; keep the room tidy — a ¥200 cleaning fee applies if it is left very dirty.
