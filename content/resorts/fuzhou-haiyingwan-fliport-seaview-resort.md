Haiyingwan Fliport Resort (福州海瀛湾佰翔度假酒店) covers about 100,000㎡ beside Nan'ao Beach in Fuzhou's Changle district — roughly 10 minutes from Fuzhou Changle International Airport and the F1 metro airport station, and about 30 minutes from downtown. The wave-curved modern buildings hold 670 rooms, some with sea-view balconies, and the resort brings its own 7,000㎡ garden beach, so the sand is steps from the lobby rather than a drive away.

Every tier includes free airport shuttle and free use of the fitness centre, indoor heated pool and sauna, plus a long list of free activities that changes with the day: for kids, the indoor and outdoor playgrounds, sea-view train, archery, sand toys, bubble balls, LEGO wall and climbing frames; for adults, dry-land curling, lawn frisbee, archery and beach jogging. Breakfast covers 2 adults + 2 children under 1.2 m (3 adults + 2 children on the suite tiers). The 1-night tier adds a gift of your choice — beach horse riding + off-road buggy, a parent-child clam-digging session, or afternoon tea for two — and the 3-night tier adds a red-wine or coffee set.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 23:59 the day before use; unreserved vouchers are refundable. Some activities (table tennis, pool, squash, kayaking, arcade) are paid extras. Address: 1 Yingbin Avenue, Fuzhou Changle International Airport area, Changle District, Fuzhou.
