Hangzhou Senbo is the Xianghu Lake family-resort institution: 415 rooms scattered through the valley, including the forest wooden cabins that feel straight out of a fairy tale, plus treehouses, castle rooms and family villas. Next door, the newly upgraded **Fantasy Island** kids' park adds a ball pit, two-level ninja course, high-altitude expansion, aerial magic net, soccer field and three slides, alongside LEGO rooms, a submarine cabin, princess dress-up, role-play supermarket and kitchen, karting, grass sledding, trampolines and a climbing wall.
