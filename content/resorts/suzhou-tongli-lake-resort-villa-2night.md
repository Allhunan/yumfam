Tongli Lake Resort sits on the shore of Tongli Lake in Suzhou's Wujiang district, surrounded on three sides by water across about 60,000㎡ of Jiangnan gardens — white walls, grey tiles and tree-lined paths, with the lake on the doorstep. The resort's 253 rooms run 40–47㎡ with bright floor-to-ceiling windows, and the deal adds daily breakfast for two (children under 1.2 m free), two servings of late-night congee per room-night, unlimited indoor and outdoor kids' playgrounds, the petting zoo, and indoor heated pools for adults and children, plus the gym and table tennis.

The location is the draw for families: **Tongli ancient town is about 1 km away** (a short walk or a few minutes' drive, and free to enter after 17:00 for an evening stroll), and the Tongli Lake Wetland Park nearby offers rowing-boat rides and a mini train. The resort itself runs weekend parent-child classes, and the small train and merry-go-round keep younger children busy without leaving the grounds.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Check-in 14:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 23:00 the day before use; rooms are first-come, first-served, so book early. Unreserved vouchers are refundable. Spring Festival stays (Feb 5–10) carry a surcharge of ¥200 / ¥300 / ¥500 per room-night by tier. Address: 88 Huan Hu West Road, Tongli Town, Wujiang District, Suzhou.
