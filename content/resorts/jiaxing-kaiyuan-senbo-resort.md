**Kaiyuan Senbo Resort** （嘉兴开元森泊度假酒店， 1677 Ganpu Avenue, Haiyan) sits beside Nanbei Lake — the 4A scenic spot where hills, lake and sea meet — about an hour's drive from Shanghai, Hangzhou, Suzhou or Ningbo. It's one of the Senbo brand's flagship family resorts: a **31,000㎡ water park** whose indoor half runs year-round at a constant ~30°C under a transparent dome (13 large rides, wave pool, family slides, lazy-river-style water play), plus **Fantasy Island** （幻想岛）, a 2,300㎡ indoor playground built around an 800㎡+, four-storey "cloud factory" exploration castle with rainbow climbing nets, trampolines, mini go-karts and a kids' disco. With 439 rooms across the resort, woodland cabins and a hot-spring hotel block, it's a proper stay-and-play base rather than a hotel with a pool attached.

Every package below is stacked the same generous way: buffet breakfast at the **Cave Restaurant** (a grotto-style dining room with starlit rock walls), **2 park tickets per night — your choice of the water park or Fantasy Island**, a handicraft workshop session (pick 1 of 3), a **Yiqi family travel photoshoot** (3 retouched photos + a 15-second video), welcome fruit, first-day minibar drinks, the nightly 15-minute music water show (19:00 & 20:00), and free parking. Fantasy Island entry even includes anti-slip socks; the water park includes a locker. A dedicated butler service looks after the stay.

⚠️ **The clock and the Saturdays:** this "off-peak back-to-school" season ends **Oct 31, 2026** — days, not weeks, left. Within that window, **Saturday stays add a supplement** (¥400 on the ¥818/¥899/¥1,498 tiers, ¥600 on the family room), while Sundays to Fridays are supplement-free. Book by **23:59 the day before**; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; unreserved vouchers are refundable.

If you pick the water park, go early on day one: tickets are per night, and the indoor park's 30°C dome makes it the wet-weather plan B as well.
