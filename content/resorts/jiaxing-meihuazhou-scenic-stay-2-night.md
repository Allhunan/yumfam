Meihuazhou is a national 4A scenic area in Fengqiao, Jiaxing — about 1.5 hours from Shanghai, Hangzhou and Suzhou — a 1,500-year-old water town whose land is split by waterways into five petal-like pieces, hence the name "Plum Blossom Isle." November is ginkgo season here: the area's two millennium-old ginkgo trees, both over 29 metres tall and standing across the river from each other for 1,250 years, turn gold, and locals call them the "love trees" joined by the Milky Way. Staying guests sleep inside the scenic area itself, in either Zitaoxuan (a Jiangnan water-town style inn) or Shuixicaotang (a Zen-themed inn built around the 24 solar terms), and can come and go from the scenic area as often as they like with their room card.

The stay includes daily breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.4 m), unlimited Meihuazhou entry during the stay, the petting zoo (sika deer, peacocks, squirrels, rabbits), the ancient Zhuangqiao village, the century-old Feng family house, weekend shadow-puppet performances, a free shuttle to and from the scenic-area gate at check-in and check-out, and free parking. The 1-night option adds a DIY tie-dye souvenir (small scarf or hair tie, with instruction) and 2 drinks at the Xingran coffee shop.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Reserve at least 3 days ahead (by 20:00); room type (Zitaoxuan or Shuixicaotang, king or twin, 22–30㎡) is assigned at random. Weekend stays carry no surcharge; Spring Festival (Feb 5–10) adds ¥100 per room-night. Room upgrades are available at checkout (e.g. elegant room +¥100). Address: inside Meihuazhou scenic area, 10 Sanxing Road, Fengqiao Town, Nanhu District, Jiaxing — check in at the visitor centre.
