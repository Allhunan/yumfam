**Deal price: 1 night for 2 adults + 1 child — ¥299** (≈$42, was ¥888, about 66% off) · Valid Nov 13 – Dec 31, 2026 · No weekend top-up · Book by 17:00 on the day

**Dongfang Xiawanna Hotel （广州东方夏湾拿酒店）** sits in Conghua — Guangzhou's green backyard, about an hour from the city centre and reachable on Metro Line 14 — styled as a "Little Cuba" villa town on the Liuxi River. The draw is the hot-spring side: guests get **unlimited access to the health-soak pools and the dry sauna** during their stay, plus a swimming pool, gym, free 1-hour bike rides along the greenway, free 1-hour mahjong, free parking and Wi-Fi.

The **¥299 package** covers 1 night in a senior twin room, breakfast for **2 adults + 1 child under 1.4 m**, one set dinner for 2 adults + 1 child (steamed fish, pan-fried stuffed tofu, eel rice), and the pools and sauna for 2 adults + 1 child. There is **no weekend top-up** — the same price holds Saturday and Sunday. Two-night options are also on sale: **¥518** for a small-balcony twin room with breakfast and afternoon tea each night, or **¥368** for a large-balcony twin room without breakfast.

The area rewards a slow wander: the No. 8 Warehouse outlet town is 2 km away, and the Liuxi River Xiawanna Wetland Park is right by the hotel.

**Good to know**
- Book by 17:00 on the day of arrival; show the booking code and your ID at check-in
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; the child in the deal is under 1.4 m
- Extra breakfast is ¥30 per person on site; room upgrades are available online (+¥30/night for a small-balcony room, +¥50 for a large-balcony room)
- Unbooked vouchers are refundable
- Address: No. 1 Xiawanna Avenue, Taiping Town, Conghua District, Guangzhou
