**Deal price: garden room + breakfast + eco-park tickets — ¥399** (≈$56, was ¥999) · 2 adults + 2 children · Valid September 17 – December 31, 2026

**San Zheng Banshan** sits in the hills of Qiaotou, Dongguan, looking out over gardens and a 300-mu lotus lake — a proper wind-down base about 30 minutes from downtown Dongguan. Every tier covers **2 adults + 2 children** (one child under 1.4 m stays free), and the headline ¥399 tier already bundles a **garden room, breakfast for the family and two tickets to Qiaotou Eco-Park**. Step up to ¥599 and the **buffet dinner** joins in: salmon and tuna sashimi, sushi, teppanyaki cooked to order (steak, chicken, fish), hotpot and a dessert table.

The water is the draw here. The hotel has a **jungle water slide channel, a 2,000㎡ outdoor pool, a kids' water castle and a kids' pool**, plus a beach play area, an indoor kids' park, a gym and a sauna/steam room — all unlimited for the family during the stay. Outside, you can stroll the lotus lake and climb Songshan Pagoda in the neighbouring park, or drive 10 minutes to the **Qiaotou lotus fields**. The eco-park (6 km away, tickets included in four of the six tiers) is a 500-mu farm park with a **400-metre glass water slide**, a rainbow slide, a petting zoo, a mini train, farm experiences and 20+ play items.

**Good to know**
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00. Book by 17:00 on the day you want to use the voucher; weekend and holiday rooms are limited, so book at least a week ahead for those dates.
- Friday and Saturday nights add ¥50/room/night. Extra children beyond the package pay about ¥138 (under 1.4 m) or ¥190 (older children and adults) for breakfast + dinner.
- The eco-park is a separate site 6 km from the hotel — drive there yourself; extra eco-park tickets are bought at the park, not the hotel. The lotus fields are a 10-minute drive away. Unbooked and unused vouchers are refundable. Address: San Zheng Banshan Hotel, No. 2 Hubin Road, Qiaotou Town, Dongguan, Guangdong.
