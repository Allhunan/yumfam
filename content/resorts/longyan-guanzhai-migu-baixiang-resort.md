**Deal price: 1 night from ¥499** (≈$69, was ¥999) · Valid Nov 19, 2026 – Jan 31, 2027 · Any day of the week · Book by 23:59 the day before · Refundable if unbooked

This is the hotel to pick if your kids would rather be outside than in a lobby. The resort sits at the foot of **Guanzhaishan, a 5A national scenic area** — the hotel's back gate is effectively the mountain's entrance, about a 4-minute drive to the main trails — and it owns **400 mu of private forest** filled with more than 400 species of rare plants and animals, from red-bellied squirrels to kingfishers. The three low buildings are built in the local **Hakka roundhouse style**, with grey tiles and rammed-earth walls, and the lobby runs a 30-metre wooden front desk under a glass roof. In winter the forest here runs 3–6°C cooler than the city, and the air smells of pine and woodsmoke.

The rooms are **mountain-view rooms** dressed in Hakka blue, with lattice windows and camphor-wood-style furniture. A note from the hotel: the Zhuying rooms (new building) and Shangu rooms (older building) share the same layout, but only the Shangu rooms have bathtubs — ask at booking if a tub matters to you.

**Packages** — breakfast is for 2 adults + 2 kids under 1.2 m; the Hakka lunch for two is served at the Baikuixuan Chinese restaurant

- **Mountain room, 1 night — ¥499** (≈$69, was ¥999): 1 night in a Shangu mountain-view king or twin room, breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids, a Hakka-style lunch for two, plus the activities below
- **Mountain room, 2 nights — ¥998** (≈$139, was ¥1,399): the same room and inclusions for 2 nights — or split as 2 rooms for 1 night

Both packages include a long list of **free activities**: the kids' naughty-castle playground and trampoline, forest tennis, table tennis and basketball, bikes to ride through the woods, a mountain hike to fetch spring water, woodblock-printing and tie-dye workshops, a moss-bottle craft session, a dragon-lantern folk experience, and a shuttle to the Guanzhaishan west gate. The hotel also offers a **dried-flower fan DIY or a leg herbal-steam session** (pick one), 10% off lunch orders and two ¥20 breakfast vouchers. The **Liancheng County Museum** — 11,258 artefacts of Hakka history and folk culture — is a 3-minute drive away, and the ancient village of Peitian is an easy half-day trip.

**Good to know:** the hotel is at 30 Guanzhai Lower Road, Liancheng County (about 3.5 hours' drive from Xiamen; high-speed rail to Guanzhaishan station, then 30 minutes to Liancheng and 10 minutes to the hotel). Book by 23:59 the day before; unbooked orders are refundable and unused vouchers auto-refund after expiry. Not usable Jan 1–3, 2027 (New Year holiday). Some craft activities are charged separately — confirm at the front desk when you book.
