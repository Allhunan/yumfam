**Deal price: 8 hot spring visits + 8 buffet dinners — ¥1,111** (≈$156, was ¥3,088, about 64% off) · Valid Nov 4, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Hot springs daily, buffet dinner Fri–Sun

**Longjia Eco Hot Spring Resort （龙佳生态温泉山庄）** in Jiaomei, about 25 km from Zhangzhou and an easy drive from Xiamen, is a 200,000㎡ garden-style hot spring resort built in Tang-palace garden style around 40,000㎡ of landscaped hot springs. Zhangzhou is officially rated a "China Hot Spring City", and Longjia has been the area's best-known hot spring resort for over 20 years, with 30+ pools across hydrotherapy, herbal-medicine, petal and fish-therapy zones. This Double 11 bundle covers 8 hot spring visits and 8 buffet dinners — about ¥139 per visit-and-dinner pair.

The buffet dinner runs Friday to Sunday (17:30–21:00) with 10+ food stations: fresh seafood, sashimi and sushi, teppanyaki grilled to order, Chinese and Western hot dishes, Zhangzhou local snacks, and desserts and seasonal fruit. The outdoor hot springs are open daily 10:00–22:30, and an evening soak under the warm lights is the resort at its best.

**Good to know**
- 8 hot spring vouchers (any day) + 8 buffet dinner vouchers (Friday–Sunday only); the bundle is redeemed in one go at the hotel and swapped for paper tickets, which can then be split up and used separately
- Book at least one day ahead (by 20:00 the day before); unbooked orders are refundable, and expired orders auto-refund
- Hot springs 10:00–22:30 daily; buffet dinner 17:30–21:00, Friday to Sunday only
- Address: No. 1 Longjia Road, Longjia Eco Hot Spring Resort, Jiaomei, Longhai District, Zhangzhou
