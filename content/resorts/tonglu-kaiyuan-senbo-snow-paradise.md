Tonglu's Senbo is the newest of the Senbo family — "Senbo 3.0" — a resort the size of a small town (about 3 million square metres across three phases) built around two parks, and this deal bundles both the stay and the park tickets. The headline draw is the **brand-new Aoneng Snow World**, an almost 8,000-square-metre indoor snow-and-sports centre that opened this autumn: three floors split into **Ice Lake Wonderland** (10+ ice activities — bumper cars on ice, ice slides, curling, snowmobiles), **Snow Kingdom** (an ice castle, ice bar and a snowfall zone) and **Exploration Town** (high-ropes, climbing, a reverse-gravity bike and a CS arena — 23 sporty challenges that suit older kids and adults). Prefer water? Swap the snow tickets for the **water park** instead — every tier lets you pick one park or the other.

The base tier puts you in a **deluxe garden-view room (king or twin) for 1 night** with **breakfast for 2**, **2 park tickets**, **20 arcade tokens** at the Player No. 1 arcade, a **photo session (3 prints)** and **welcome fruit plus minibar drinks** on arrival day. The resort sits in Yaolin Town, Tonglu County — about 1.5 hours' drive from downtown Hangzhou and 3.5 hours from Shanghai — right by the Asian Games equestrian centre, and the whole complex mixes resort hotels, parks and eco-leisure in one car-free-feeling valley. Book before December 31 and pick the snow-park tickets, and you also get **2 drinks at Yami Bar or 1 ice-bike rental free**.
