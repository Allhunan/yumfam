Twenty minutes from Xiamen's island center, **Riyuegu (日月谷)** has been the city's hot-spring landmark for over two decades — and it's still the one locals buy every year. The resort hides **100+ pools and 40+ themed springs** inside a lush subtropical garden that past guests keep comparing to Bali: the **petal pool** (rose petals on the water — the photo-op pool), the **red-wine spring**, the tea-scented spring, all shaded by palms, bamboo and running streams.

This ticket is the midweek base tier with a generous stack of extras: a **healing ritual** (singing-bowl-style emotional-decompression session), a **meal-set voucher**, access to the **recreation lounge and petting corner**, the resort's **treasure-hunt activity**, and a **free shuttle between the resort and Dongfu Metro Station**. One child **under 1.2m** goes free with each adult ticket. The ¥239 and ¥399 tiers add a **full Thai meal set** (Tom Yum soup, green-curry beef, Thai lemongrass chicken wings, pineapple fried rice) — note the restaurant swaps the Thai set for a **hotpot set from Nov 10**.

⚠️ **Fine print that matters:** usable **Monday to Sunday**; weekend visits add a **¥40 per-ticket surcharge**. Refundable anytime before use, and expired codes auto-refund. Buy-and-go — no ticket exchange needed. As the deal title says, pack a swim cap: pools are open-air, and on rainy days the pools have umbrellas/sheltered pavilions.
