Xiamen Langham Place (厦门朗豪酒店) is an art-styled hotel on Xianyue Road in Huli, next to the Wanda shopping district and a short trip from Wuyuan Bay wetland park — the "city green lung" where families stroll, picnic and watch black swans. The brand traces back to London's Langham hotel of 1865, and the Xiamen property leans into that heritage with a modern-art collection and a music-themed design: artworks such as "Red Apple" and "Riding on a Dream" hang through the hotel, and the all-day restaurant The Place carries the musical motif into its wood-and-glass interior. The hotel has 321 rooms and suites, most with floor-to-ceiling windows over the city.
