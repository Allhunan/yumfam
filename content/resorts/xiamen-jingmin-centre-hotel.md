**Xiamen Jingmin Centre Hotel （厦门京闽中心酒店）** is a 29-year-old hotel in the old city centre's Songbai neighbourhood, 5 minutes by car from SM City Plaza — the practical choice for a family that wants to be in the middle of Xiamen rather than out by the beach. The **¥569 package** covers 1 night in a deluxe king or twin room with a buffet breakfast for **2 adults + 2 children under 1.2 m**, plus free use of the indoor swimming pool, gym and kids' play area, free Wi-Fi and parking, and a welcome gift. Book a Friday-to-Sunday stay and the deal doubles up: a **free upgrade to a family room** (a 1.6 m double bed plus a 1.1 m single bed, with cartoon-themed bedding) and one child's breakfast height limit raised to 1.4 m.

A second option suits a bigger group: **¥999** for a two-bedroom suite of about 85㎡ (two bathrooms, Chinese or European style) with **four salt-steaming sessions including a healthy set meal**.
