**Beijing Minmetals Junlan Hotel （北京五矿君澜酒店）** sits at the foot of the Western Hills in Haidian, right by Phoenix Ridge — a proper family hotel where the kids' club is the main event, not an afterthought. The **Lan Jingling Tribe** runs bookable parent-child activities (think old-school games: spinning tops, shuttlecock, hoop rolling) plus a free kids' play centre, and the hotel's own **Super Wings experience hall** is a near-1,000㎡, three-storey play space with an 11-metre ceiling, a giant slide, a ball pit and climbing challenges. There is also a sci-fi **Cloud Illusion exploration park** with a C919 flight simulator and a Shenzhou spacecraft mock-up, a 25-metre indoor pool with a warm kids' pool, a gym, table tennis and a chess room.

The **¥659 package** covers 1 night in a Junlan Western Hills king or twin room, a buffet breakfast for two (kids under 1.2 m eat free), a two-person noodle set (one bamboo-shoot tomato noodle, one prawn oil-splash noodle) and **2 Phoenix Ridge scenic-area tickets**. Other tiers on the same base: **¥699** adds a Super Wings single-session ticket for one child + one accompanying adult, with either the Phoenix Ridge tickets or the noodle set; a second **¥699** tier gives two Super Wings tickets (valid to Dec 31, 2026); and **¥1,198** covers 2 nights (or 2 rooms for 1 night) with daily breakfast for two.

**Good to know**
- Valid Nov 13, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 (the two-ticket Super Wings tier ends Dec 31, 2026); book by 23:59 the day before; show the booking code, phone number and ID at check-in
- Breakfast: kids under 1.2 m free, 1.2–1.5 m half price (¥69), above 1.5 m full price (¥138); the noodle set is used with your room card during the stay
