**Deal price: 1 night from ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,499) · Valid Sep 23 – Dec 31, 2026 · Any day of the week · Book by 23:59 on the day of use · Refundable if unbooked

Ningde is one of Fujian's quieter coastal cities — a deep-water harbour town the poet Guo Moruo once described as a lake in the sky — and this is its big lakeside hotel. The Baixiang Sanduao sits on the **East Lake waterfront** in the Dongqiao district, backed by Damen Mountain, with **295 rooms** spread along the lakefront promenade. The 12-metre lobby is hung with a relief of Huotong, the historic town nearby, and the whole building leans into a grand new-Chinese style: lake greens, coastal blues, and a lot of glass facing the water.

**Packages** — breakfast is for 2 adults + 2 kids under 1.2 m

- **Garden-view room, 1 night — ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,499): 1 night in a garden-view king or twin room, buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids, and a two-person set meal (lunch or dinner — **free upgrade to a buffet dinner on Friday–Sunday**). Each adult can bring one child under 1.2 m free to the buffet dinner. Includes a **She ethnic costume experience** (pick up the outfit at reception).
- **Garden-view room, 2 nights — ¥999** (≈$139, was ¥1,499): 2 nights in the same room type — or split as 2 rooms for 1 night — with breakfast for 2 adults + 2 kids each night, the She costume experience, and a **kids' drawing-board experience** for one child.

The **She costume try-on** is the package's signature: the She people are Fujian's largest ethnic minority, and the hotel lends the embroidered outfits for photos around the lakefront. Beyond that, the hotel has an **indoor heated pool and gym**, a chess-and-cards room and a tea room, plus two restaurants — the popular Pinxuan Western restaurant and the Huatingxuan Chinese restaurant serving Min cuisine, Cantonese dishes and fresh seafood.

**Good to know:** the hotel is at 8 Beihu Bin Road, Jiaocheng District, Ningde — about a 7-minute drive from Ningde railway station. Book by 23:59 on the day you want to use it; unbooked orders are refundable. Bring ID for every guest — adults with an ID card, children with a household register. Not usable Oct 1–6 (National Day) or during large conferences. Children over 1.4 m pay the adult breakfast rate at the hotel.
