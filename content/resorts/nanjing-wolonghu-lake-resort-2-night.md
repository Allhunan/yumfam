Some hotels sell a room; this one sells square meters and lake air. The **Wolonghu Yaquandao Yuehu Resort Hotel** （卧龙湖雅泉道悦湖度假酒店， formerly the Wolong Lake Wyndham Grand) stands inside the 7,000-mu Wolong Lake scenic area in Nanjing's Lishui district — Mediterranean-style architecture designed by Wilson Associates (the firm behind the Venetian Macao, which makes this their sister property and their first mainland China hotel), an 8-meter crystal cherry-blossom chandelier in the lobby, and a limited-edition Bösendorfer piano from Vienna. The rooms are the headline: a **72㎡ king with a 2 m × 2 m bed** or a **70㎡ twin with two 1.5 m beds**, dry/wet separated bathrooms, oversized tubs and double sinks — space a family can actually spread out in.

Days fill easily without leaving the resort: the included **water-play pass for two** covers an indoor surfing experience, the indoor pool, an indoor hot-spring fish-therapy and sauna area, a flower-sea photo zone, NPC battle activities, KTV and board-game rooms; there's also a gym, a **1,300㎡ lakeside infinity pool** outdoors, and **lakeside cycling for two** (bikes are first-come, first-served). Around the lake town, guests add horse riding and, in spring, bamboo-shoot digging — one review below describes the hotel cooking your own dug shoots for the table, free of charge.
