Tucked into the bamboo hills of Anji, Licheng Tangquan is a Japanese-style hot-spring resort built around private soaking: several packages put a **private hot-spring pool on your own terrace**, fillable whenever you like. Even the entry-level room comes with breakfast for two, afternoon tea for two, fishing, an open-air cinema, billiards, an infinity pool, a viewing terrace for photos, a kids' play area and a gym — and the two-night stay and the villa tiers add tea around the stove (围炉煮茶) and a photo shoot.
