Longjia Hotel anchors the 200,000㎡ Longjia Eco Hot Spring Resort in Zhangzhou's Taiwan-investment zone — a lakeside property in classic Minnan swallow-tail-roof style, shaped like a spreading eagle, with open grassland in front and a forested lake island behind. Every hot spring room comes with its **own private soaking pool, open 24 hours**, and the resort's hot spring park — a local favorite for over 20 years — holds 30+ pools across hydrotherapy, herbal-medicine and flower-petal zones.

The location makes it an easy Xiamen add-on: with Xiamen Metro Line 6 now running to Jiaomei, the hotel pairs well with the century-old Tianyi General Post Office (free entry), the Jiulong riverside promenade at Yinxiang Binhai, and the 276-house Daimei ancient village nearby. The themed family room options include the Alice XR tech-adventure room, Little Yellow Duck and Little Bear Neo themes.
