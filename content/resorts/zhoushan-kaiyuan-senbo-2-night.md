Zhoushan's Kaiyuan Senbo is the seaside branch of the family — a resort on Zhujiajian Island, 15 minutes from the ferry to Putuo Mountain, where you can watch the sunset from the 17th floor and fall asleep to the sound of the waves. The centrepiece is the **7,500㎡ indoor heated water park** under a giant glass dome: 30°C year-round, with slides, a wave pool, a lazy river and a water-climbing wall — plus **Fantasy Island** for younger kids (ocean-ball pits, rainbow slides, climbing nets) and **Player One**, an arcade hall with AR/VR games, karting and an interactive cinema. The headline 2-night deal is the garden room at ¥998 with a ticket per night; the first 10 rooms booked each day get a free one-tier upgrade.

- Mountain-view room (king/twin) for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + 2 park tickets — **¥599** (≈$83, was ¥1,028): 1 night, breakfast for 2, 2 single-person tickets to the water park, Fantasy Island or the Player One gift pack (karting + interactive cinema + 50 game coins)
- Garden-view room for 2 nights + breakfast for 2 + 1 park ticket per night — **¥998** (≈$139, was ¥1,698): 2 consecutive nights, 2 tickets in total; first 10 rooms a day get a free one-tier upgrade + 15 extra game coins per night
- Sea-view room (king/twin) for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + 2 park tickets — **¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,598)
- Sea-view room for 2 nights + breakfast for 2 + 2 park tickets per night — **¥1,398** (≈$194, was ¥1,598): 4 tickets in total
- Sea-view family room (1.8 m bed + bunk) for 2 nights + breakfast for 4 + 4 park tickets — **¥1,888** (≈$262, was ¥3,698)

**Good to know**

- Valid Nov 23, 2026 – Jan 30, 2027, any day of the week, no weekend top-up. New Year Jan 1–2 carries a +¥300 per night top-up. Book by 23:59 on the day of use; check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00.
- **The water park closes for maintenance Jan 4–31, 2027 and reopens Feb 1** — if you book for January, plan on Fantasy Island or Player One instead. The outdoor infinity pool is photo-only (closed for swimming since Sep 7), and the outdoor land-playground items closed Dec 1, 2025.
- Park hours: water park 10:00–18:00, Fantasy Island 09:30–20:00, Player One 10:00–20:00 (to 21:00 Fri–Sat). Park tickets are valid from check-in until noon on check-out day, with unlimited re-entry. Water park entry needs swimwear; Fantasy Island needs non-slip socks.
- Also included: welcome fruit and minibar drinks on arrival day, the 3F kids' play area, lobby parades, a traditional rubbing-print craft experience in the lobby, and a photo shoot (4 edited photos; book 24 hours ahead).
- Unbooked and unused vouchers can be refunded; once booked the room is held for the whole day and is generally non-refundable. Bring ID for everyone: adults need their ID card, children (including babies) need their household registration book.
