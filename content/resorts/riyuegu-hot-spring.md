**Deal price: ¥149 (≈$21)** (was ¥228) · Valid over National Day — no surcharge · ⏰ National Day special — ends Oct 7

Riyuegu is Xiamen's best-known hot spring resort, and this day pass is valid right through National Day with no surcharge. One ticket covers a single adult and you can bring one child under 1.2m.

**What's included**
- Single admission to the hot spring resort
- Wellness experience + dining voucher
- Treasure-hunt activity for kids
- Free use of the Moonlight Pavilion entertainment room
- Free shuttle between the resort and Dongfu metro station

**Good to know:** Hot springs are an evening ritual in China — go after 4pm for the full atmosphere.
