**Deal price: deluxe room for 1 night + breakfast for 2 + 2 tickets to the Xihe Hot Spring or Fantasy Island — ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,298) · Valid Nov 14, 2026 – Mar 31, 2027 · Book by 23:59 the day before · Refundable if unbooked

Jiaxing's Kaiyuan Senbo sits beside the South & North Lakes — a 4A scenic area where mountain, sea and lake meet — about an hour's drive from both Shanghai and Hangzhou. The headline this winter is the return of the **Xihe Hot Spring**: a 5,300㎡ "natural healing" spa with outdoor pools, sweat rooms and a kids' play area, built around the idea of soaking in the forest rather than in a concrete bathhouse. If the kids would rather climb than soak, swap the hot spring for **Fantasy Island**, a 2,300㎡ indoor play zone with 20+ rides and activities for every age. Either way the deal also throws in a craft-workshop experience, a short photo shoot (3 edited photos + a 15-second video) and a mountain-and-water show at 19:00 and 20:00 each evening.

The resort has 439 rooms across a hotel, cabins and a hot-spring wing, with shuttle carts running around the grounds, a Chinese restaurant and the cave-style restaurant where breakfast is served. Note the timing: Fantasy Island closes for maintenance from 13:00 on November 30 to 13:00 on December 4 — during those days only the hot spring is open.
