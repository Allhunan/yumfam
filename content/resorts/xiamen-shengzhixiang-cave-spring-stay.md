Shengzhixiang Hot Spring Resort (厦门盛之乡温泉渡假村) sits in Tingxi town, Tong'an district — a 210,000㎡ forest resort built around a natural underground sodium-bicarbonate spring, nicknamed the "beauty spring". The signature pools are the four-season cave hot springs, kept at a constant temperature in a quiet, grotto-like setting, plus garden pools tucked among flowers and trees around the infinity pool, all continuously refreshed and cleaned. A boardwalk winds up the wooded hillside for a walk in fresh mountain air between soaks.

Rooms are generous — the deluxe and family rooms both sleep on twin beds of 1.8 m + 1.5 m — and the deal's breakfast can be swapped for a Chinese lunch set (braised beef brisket with radish, white-cut chicken or honey-glazed char siu, pick two) if you tell the hotel a day ahead; the swap applies to the whole room, not per person. The themed family room adds a small piggy-bank gift for the kids. The Shilin restaurant by the lobby serves the buffet breakfast with fresh ingredients, Chinese dishes, desserts and southern-Fujian snacks.
