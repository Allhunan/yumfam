The **Minmetals Narada Beijing** （北京五矿君澜酒店） sits at the foot of the Western Hills in Haidian (55 Bei'anhe Road, near Fenghuangling) and has built its name as one of Beijing's go-to family hotels — this is the Xishan property, not the Fangshan Narada hot-spring resort also listed on this site. The family machinery is extensive: its own **Lan Jingling Tribe** activity program for ages 3–12 (old-school games like spinning tops, shuttlecock and iron-hoop rolling), an outdoor unpowered playground with swings, slides and a sandpit, and a **Super Wings experience hall** — roughly 1,000㎡ across three floors with an 11-metre ceiling, ball-pit battles, climbing courses and dress-up role play. For older kids there's the Cloud Exploration Park with a C919 flight simulator and a Shenzhou spacecraft (separate fee).

Rooms are new-Chinese style ("one room, one view"), and the stay includes the 25-metre indoor pool and gym. Every package below bundles breakfast for two with **kids under 1.2m eating free**, free use of the Lan Jingling kids' play centre, free parking and Wi-Fi — the differences are in what else is stacked on top.

⚠️ **Plan the activities, not just the room:** the Lan Jingling Tribe sessions have **headcount caps and need advance signup**, and popular slots go fast — register at check-in, not the next morning. Most packages need booking by **23:59 the day before** (the ¥666 anniversary tier needs **3 days**). The weekend dinner package can't be used **Oct 16, 23 or 24, 2026**. Check-in is 15:00, check-out 12:00, and every guest — children included — needs ID or a household-registration booklet at check-in.

- **Super Wings Land package — ¥799** (≈$111, was ¥1,688): Junlan Xishan king/twin room, 1 night + breakfast for 2 + **1 Super Wings Land ticket (1 adult + 1 kid)** + Lan Jingling activities & play centre + pool/gym. Valid daily through Dec 31, 2026.

Valid through Oct 31, 2026.
