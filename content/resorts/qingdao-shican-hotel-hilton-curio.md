**Deal price: 1 night in a deluxe room — ¥559** (≈$78, was ¥1,298, about 57% off) · Valid Nov 6 – Dec 31, 2026 · No weekend top-up · Book by 18:00 on the day · Unbooked vouchers are refundable

**Qingdao Shican Hotel, Curio Collection by Hilton （青岛石灿酒店希尔顿格芮精选）** opened in July 2025 on the site of a former quarry, and the quarry is the design: a 60-metre vertical rock face is kept as a monument, the facade and courtyards use raw local stone, and the name itself comes from a line of the *Chu Ci* about rocks and sunset light. It sits in the Sino-German Ecopark on Qingdao's West Coast, backed by Longdou Mountain and looking over Jiaozhou Bay — about 30 km from Golden Beach, the Qingdao Wildlife Park and Fantawild Dream Kingdom, and 1 km from Metro Line 6.

The **¥559 package** covers 1 night in a 50㎡+ deluxe room (king or twin) with breakfast for two, an afternoon-tea set, and unlimited access to the **1,100㎡ indoor kids' park** on weekends (Friday–Sunday, 10:00–19:00) — a children's theatre, craft area, projection sandpit, high ropes, climbing walls and an indoor slide. Three other options run at the same base price point: **¥568** for a family room (deluxe king plus a 1.3 × 1.8 m sofa bed, breakfast for three, and 2 hours of billiards/chess room on weekends), **¥699** for the deluxe room plus a buffet dinner or Chinese set dinner for two (the buffet runs Saturday nights), or **¥888** for 2 nights that can be split into two rooms for one night or one room for two nights.

**Good to know**
- Valid Nov 6 – Dec 31, 2026, any day of the week with no weekend top-up; book by 18:00 on the day of arrival, and show the booking code and your phone number at check-in
- Check-in 15:00, check-out 12:00; children under 1.2 m eat breakfast free
- The kids' park runs Friday–Sunday 10:00–19:00 (closed Oct 9–10); the buffet dinner is Saturday nights only, while the Chinese set dinner runs daily
- The afternoon-tea set needs 3 hours' notice; the billiards/chess room needs 2 days' notice
- Unbooked vouchers are refundable; once a booking is confirmed the room is held for the full day and is generally non-refundable
- Address: No. 2528 Tuanjie Road, Huangdao District, Qingdao — 47 km from Qingdao Jiaodong International Airport
