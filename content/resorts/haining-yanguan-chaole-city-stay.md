Yanguan, on the Qiantang River in Haining, is the town of the famous tidal bore — the "first line tide" written up in the classic essay *Watching the Tide* — and the birthplace of writers Wang Guowei, Xu Zhimo and Jin Yong. Chaole City is a 3,000-mu theme town rebuilt from the old city, themed around tide-watching and music, run by the same group behind Wuzhen and Yixing Yao Lake Town. Staying guests get unlimited re-entry to the town during their stay, plus a free shuttle, free luggage storage and a welcome snack, and can walk to the Confucius temple school, Wang Guowei's former home, Jin Yong Academy and the old city wall.

The room is your pick of five inns inside the town — Jiangyuanzi Inn, Baoren Apartment, Pingchu Inn, Pipa Inn or Jingguan Apartment — each with its own style, from riverside rooms to courtyard houses. The deal also includes a hands-on heritage craft (bamboo weaving, rattan weaving or seal incense, one per room card) and the "Tide Music" immersive real-scenery role-play game that kids in particular love. The two-night version of the same package is ¥599 (≈$83, was ¥1,195).

Tide tip: the biggest bores come around the 18th day of the eighth lunar month, but large tides also run on the 1st–5th and 15th–20th days of each lunar month — check the tide timetable before you go.
