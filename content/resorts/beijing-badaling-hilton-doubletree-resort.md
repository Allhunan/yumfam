**Beijing Badaling Hilton Doubletree** sits in the centre of Yanqing — Beijing's "summer capital" — about 20 km from the **Badaling Great Wall** and close to Longqing Gorge and Wild Duck Lake Wetland Park. It's a 2021 hotel with 276 rooms; the premium rooms run about 40 m² with a 1.8 m king bed or two 1.35 m twins. This Double-11 deal packs a lot into one night: breakfast for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.2 m), hot-spring tickets for 2 adults + 1 child (under 1.3 m) at the Shiyuan Yueqin Hot Spring — a roughly 10,000 m² spring centre with 30+ pools — plus kids' park tickets, a traditional-craft session and 2 adult tickets to Wild Duck Lake Wetland Park. The hotel itself adds a kids' club, a heated indoor pool and a gym, and a welcome cookie on arrival.

The hot spring and the kids' park are nearby attractions, not inside the hotel — the spring is at the Beijing Expo Park site and the play park is the iQIYI Qibabu park, so plan a short trip out for both. The Wild Duck Lake tickets are collected from the hotel concierge on check-in day. If you'd rather eat than soak, the second package swaps the spring and play park for a Yanqing-style family dinner (hot-pot included).
