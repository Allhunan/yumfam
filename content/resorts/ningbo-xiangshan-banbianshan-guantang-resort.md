Banbianshan Guantang sits inside the Banbianshan seaside resort area in Xiangshan, Ningbo — a hotel designed around its coastal setting, with reef, fishing-boat and wave motifs woven through the lobby and rooms, and 126 rooms in total. The beach is a short walk from the room blocks, so sea views, sand play and paddling are right outside the door, and the hotel's Jiangnan-courtyard corners, covered bridges and pavilions make for easy photo stops.

The deal covers a 30–40㎡ Guantang room (king bed about 2 m × 1.8 m, or twin beds 2 m × 1.2 m), two buffet breakfasts, a **seafood dinner for two**, a **hanfu dress-up experience**, free first-day minibar, and the outdoor kids' playground and picture-book room for younger guests. The two-night version swaps in two servings of the signature seafood noodles plus two Lianquan sea-view hot spring tickets.

⚠️ **Good to know:** Weekend stays at no surcharge. Book at least one day ahead (by 17:00 the day before use). Extra bed on site ¥200 per person per night including breakfast. Address: 102 Yuwan Road, Banbianshan Scenic Area, Xiangshan County, Ningbo.
