Nicknamed **"Guangzhou's Little Greece"** and the "Oriental Santorini," the **Santori Hot Spring Manor** （圣托利温泉庄园， 112 Wenquan East Road, Conghua) is a blue-and-white Mediterranean fantasy planted in Guangzhou's historic hot-spring town, on the banks of the Liuxi River. The geology underneath is the real luxury: the manor is built over a **rare radon soda spring** — one of the uncommon spring types in China — rich in calcium, potassium, strontium and 20-plus other mineral elements, feeding **50-plus outdoor garden soaking pools** scented and themed by the day: rose, lemon, apple, milk, geranium. The grounds run to over 30,000㎡ with about 5,000㎡ of water areas, 100-plus rooms, a constant-temperature pool and a kids' water park.

This package's room is the one people come for: a superior king or twin (20–25㎡, ground floor) with its **own private terrace soaking pool** — your room's hot spring, no sharing, no walking to the spring center in a robe. Both meals are in: **buffet breakfast for 2 adults + 1 kid (under 1.2m)**, and **dinner for two** — which upgrades to a 2-adults-1-kid buffet dinner on nights the hotel runs the buffet. You also get the spring center's **10th-floor suspended transparent infinity pool**, plus the 3rd-floor recreation center, gym, kids' playground, billiards and mini golf.

⚠️ **Fine print worth knowing:** Saturdays add **¥100 per room per night** (the Mid-Autumn ¥200 dates and the Oct 1–6 blackout are already past). The stay window runs 15:00 to 12:00 next day. Rooms are first-reserved-first-served reference inventory — book ahead for weekends rather than on arrival. Bring **physical ID for every guest including children** (household register for kids): the hotel checks one document per person at check-in. Individual facilities can pause for maintenance or weather without compensation, per the hotel's terms.
