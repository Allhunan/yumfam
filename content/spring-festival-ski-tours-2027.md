## Shennongjia Snow Family Tour — 6 Days / 5 Nights

<a href="/family-packages/shennongjia-snow-family-tour-6-day/"><img src="https://img.xialv.com/uploads/2026/10/09/item_pic1791557798558243.jpeg?imageView2/1/w/1050/h/588" alt="Shennongjia snow family tour — snow forest and ski slopes of Shennongjia" loading="lazy" referrerpolicy="no-referrer"></a>

The quiet alternative to the big-name resorts: Shennongjia sits on the "Roof of Central China" at over 3,000 metres, with a 100-plus-day snow season and soft powder the booking page compares to the Alps — and far smaller crowds than Xinjiang or Changbai Mountain. The difference from most ski tours is the time on snow: a **full day at Shennongjia International Ski Resort** on 15 runs, with ski ticket, skis and boots included and **two hours of beginner coaching at a one-coach-per-family ratio**. Off the slopes: the golden snub-nosed monkeys, the 3,106-metre Shennongding summit, the Shennong Altar and a Three Gorges Dam study course for the kids. Five nights in 5-diamond hotels, 5 breakfasts and 5 main meals, and travel insurance are included.

**Good to know:** the ski ticket covers skis and boots only — snow clothing, goggles and **helmets are not included**, so bring your own or rent on site. Children under 4, pregnant travellers and travellers over 70 cannot join; travellers over 60 need a direct relative travelling with them. If 1 adult travels with 1 child, the child must occupy a bed. Contact customer service after booking to confirm your departure.

## Yichun & Lesser Khingan Mountains 6-Day Snow Family Tour

<a href="/family-packages/yichun-xiaoxinganling-snow-family-tour-6-day/"><img src="https://img.xialv.com/uploads/2026/11/09/item_pic1794229064065325.jpeg?imageView2/1/w/1050/h/588" alt="Yichun snow family tour — deer in the snow forest at Jinshan Deer Park" loading="lazy" referrerpolicy="no-referrer"></a>

This one is built around skipping queues: four days in the snow forest of the Lesser Khingan Mountains — the forest from the Chinese primary-school textbook chapter *The Beautiful Lesser Khingan Mountains* — instead of the crowded Harbin–Snow Town circuit. Skiing is **1-on-1 lessons at Riyuexia** (a 4S-standard resort) with skis, boots and **helmet included**, plus a snow ATV, a snow tube train and ice fishing. The rest of the trip: hand-feeding sika deer at Jinshan Deer Park, a night in the Xingyue Fairy-tale Village, the Northeast Tiger Park, the Harbin Institute of Technology Aerospace Museum and forest walks with a ranger. Harbin bookends the trip with two nights at the Harbin International Convention Centre Hilton, with free time for Central Street, St Sophia Cathedral and the Ice and Snow World — whose ticket is **not** included, buy it separately.

**Good to know:** **helmets are mandatory throughout** — the booking page says so twice, and they are included with the ski gear. The child price does **not** include a bed: add the ¥700 room difference if your child occupies a bed, and if 1 adult travels with 2 children, one child pays the adult price. Minimum booking is 1 adult + 1 child. Unused vouchers are refundable. Groups need 15 people to run — confirm with customer service after booking and before buying your transport.

## Which one fits your family?

- **Pick Shennongjia** if you want the most time on snow (a full day, not half a day), a lower child price, and a trip closer to central China — reachable by high-speed rail or flight via Yichang. Bring helmets.
- **Pick Yichun** if you want a coach entirely to yourselves, helmets and gear included with nothing to rent, and the snow-forest experience — deer, ice fishing and the tiger park — with Harbin on both ends.
